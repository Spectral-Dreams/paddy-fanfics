Vampire: The Masquerade- Aeterna Servitus
Act 1: British Autumn
Gloughchester, England (NOT to be confused with Gloucester, or Gloucestershire for that matter).
The year is 201X. The air is crispy and the colors of the autumn season are warm, inviting, and somewhat welcoming. Yet, that jolly ol' British "gray and gloomy" feel persists in the overall atmosphere.
A few birds sing sweetly in the distance. The world seems to be at peace. Yet, a sense of unease still lingers in the air.
Her cabin is located quite a way off from the city proper. So far, in fact, that even its bustling sounds cannot be heard. It's a quaint little place, nestled amongst the sturdy trees and rolling hills of the countryside. A small, cobblestone path leads up to the front door.
The cabin itself is quite old, with a rustic charm that is not easy to come by in the modern age. Yet, it is remarkably well-maintained. Smooth wooden flooring, a solid roof, and an impressive stone fireplace. It's more than enough for her needs, and she is quite happy with it.
She has been here for quite some time now. He...lives not that far away, in the fancier house, the real crowning jewel of this part of Gloughchester.
Inside, she readies herself to go and meet with him. She has a Rubenesque physique and is in her late-20s. Her brown hair is long and cascades down to her shoulders. She has lovely hazel eyes. She is dressed in a comfortable outfit of jeans, a t-shirt, and a light jacket.
The lass is seated in front of her dresser, drying her hair. The sound of her hairdryer dominates the room. She dries her brunette hair whilst smiling at her reflection in a rather cheeky fashion.
After that, she takes a deep breath and closes her eyes. He called on her this evening. Last night, all he told her was to come to his house prepared as soon as the sun had set.
He had that ever-so-familiar distance and withdrawment about him. It's something that she herself has gotten fairly inured to over the years that she has not only known him but also served him. He is not an easy man to understand, that much is for certain.
She rises from her seat with a sort of semi-practiced grace and goes to get her things. She grabs her purse, keys, and nothing much else.
"I don't need anything else, do I?" she asks herself, her accent utterly Northern and thick.
She shakes her head. "No, no. Cor blimey, I got another...eh...hour before he gets up, yeah. May as well do some light reading."
She goes to the bookshelf and picks out a random book. She shrugs, throws herself on the recliner, leans her head back, and begins to read. The time passes by rather quickly.
She breathes out a few times and readies herself. She knows how people like him tend to get soon after waking from a slumber that lasts throughout the whole day.
"Alright, alright. Deep breaths. He--he's not gonna lunge at ya and bite ya, right? Eh. I should just keep me distance."
She makes her way out of the cabin, locking the door behind her. She hugs herself when a light breeze blows through the trees.
"It's bloody cold out tonight, innit?"
The woman makes her way through paths which she has traversed (by foot) so many times before. In the few years that she's lived here, she has gotten to know the surrounding area very well.
Coming here from London was more of his idea- she just had to tag along for the ride. Nevertheless, she has always been quite fond of nature, and living out here has done wonders for her mental health.
Whether it's done the same for him, on the other hand, is hard to determine. Even after all these years, he has been a fairly closed-off person. But then again, he has never been the most social of people. His times were very different from hers.
They are separated by decades of history. Yet, they are Bound by the Blood.
While walking the winding paths, she licks her lips and tries to remember when her next feeding is supposed to be. She stops and gives the sky above her a good, long stare. Shrouded by clouds, the moon's light is barely visible.
"Ah, English weather. Never gets old!" she says and follows it up with a long chuckle.
Soon enough, she arrives at the front of his...Haven.
It's a Bauhaus-inspired (as in the school of design) structure nestled between the thick trees. Sleek and modern, it's definitely quite the contrast to her own humble cottage.
(Not that she complains, mind you! She knows better than to complain.)
She stops and admires the plain white walls, the floor-to-ceiling windows, and the impressive glass door. It's a beautiful piece of architecture, that much is for sure. And the price that he got it for is a story in and of itself.
She raps her knuckles on the glass door and patiently waits for him to answer. A few moments pass by, and she hears his voice coming from within.
His accent is somewhat rough but not necessarily caustic, "It's open, Cassandra." His delivery is dry, lacking in any real emotion.
She nods and opens the door. She happily calls out, "Hiya, Greg! Are y--you awake or are you still feeling a bit tired?"
The lights inside are dimmed. The temperature is cool, almost cold. She shivers slightly and rubs her arms. The flooring of his Haven is made of marble.
The interior is minimalist, stark, and modern. He kept most of the previous owner's furniture and decorations. There are a few paintings on the walls, mostly abstract pieces that he tends to get lost in while staring at them.
She looks around and doesn't see him anywhere.
Cassandra has a look of concern on her face when she calls out, "Greg? You there? Y--you wanted to see me tonight? Remember?"
He emerges behind her, having silently moved through the shadows. His voice is soft yet commanding, "Yes, I remember. I don't need reminding of what I already know."
She turns around and sees him standing there. He is a tall and pale man with thinning hair, scruffy facial hair, and lifeless blue eyes. He is middle-aged but looks far older than that.
He is dressed in a green t-shirt and dark sweatpants. His face is angular and somewhat gaunt.
Cassandra smiles and nervously compliments him, "Looking...nice tonight." Her follow-up smile is just as nervous.
The wrinkles on Gregory's face deepen as he glares at her in a very dry and unimpressed fashion, "Hm. If you say so."
"You...uh...fed yet? Need me to help you, you know...?"
"No. That can wait, Cassandra. I summoned you here for a purpose. Make yourself comfortable. I'll need to retrieve something. After that, we'll sit down and have a talk. It is time, Cassandra."
Gregory wordlessly turns on his heels and strides up the stairs.
Cassandra watches him go with a curious expression on her face. Her cheeks redden slightly, and she feels her heart skip a few beats.
She thinks to herself, "Time for what? Bloody 'ell, he's always so vague with me, yeah? Maybe I should 'ave a talk with him about that. But, uh...I dunno if I'm ready for that, innit?"
For now, Cassandra scratches her head and sits down on his fancy L-shaped couch. She leans back and folds 1 leg over the other.
A few minutes later, Gregory comes back down the stairs, holding a piece of paper in his cold, dead hands. He looks at Cassandra and silently hands it to her.
On it, are listed the names of the Clans of the Kindred, along with a few Bloodlines. Cassandra's face scrunches up in confusion.
Gregory sits down with a groan and anticipates her questions. He dryly begins, "I'm sure that you have many questions. Best to get them all out right away. Ask." He lazily motions for her to begin.
Cassandra blinks a few times, "What's this?"
Gregory answers, "As you are very well aware, over the years, I have been something of an art dealer. I needn't elaborate on what I hope you already have knowledge of, yes?"
"Yeah. What's that got to do with a list of all the Clans...and a few Bloodlines, though? Isn't having something like this lying around considered a Masquerade breach?"
Gregory shrugs, "It makes very little difference to me on nights like these, Cassandra. Put that aside for a moment. During my...time, I have come across an array of Kindred. Certain deals and promises were made- formally, informally, or otherwise. I strongly feel that now is the time to make good on these promises. All of them."
Cassandra furrows her brows, "Why is this the first time that I'm hearing about all of this? Also, all of them? Cor blimey, you owe every single Clan on this list a--a favor of some kind, eh?"
"Some of them owe me. Personally."
"Oh. Wow. That should be mighty useful in getting out of a sticky situation, yeah?"
"Hm. Perhaps. Perhaps it would only serve to thrust me into even greater messes. But I digress. This is where you come in. As my Ghoul, I am authorizing you to not only act but also speak on my behalf."
"Wow. Uhm..." the words fail to form on her lips.
Gregory raises a brow, "I feel that you have something to say. Please, say it now rather than keeping it to yourself. I have never been a cruel Domitor to you and I most certainly don't intend to ever be as such."
Cassandra takes a deep breath, "This is--blimey, this is quite a lot. ALL the Clans? You've been busy! So, that FINALLY explains all those trips you used to take for weeks on end! How do I factor into this equation? Is it permissible for a Domitor to authorize his Ghoul to do what you said? I feel that territory is a tad murky, Greg."
Gregory plainly replies, "It is permissible because I say it is so. Truth be told, I'd rather not deal with some of the...individuals on this list. But I have made promises. Promises that I intend to keep. And many of them owe me, and it's time to, pardon the crudeness, cash in on those favors."
Cassandra takes a deep breath and nods slowly, processing the weight of her immortal master's words.
Gregory continues, "There is a few more things that I must tell you."
Cassandra chuckles, "I'm all ears, Greg!" She tugs on her ears.
Gregory ignores her attempt at humor, "You are free to tackle it in any way that you so please. I will elaborate on who owes me and to whom I owe. Us Kindred deal in something far greater than the fluctuating pettiness of the fiat currency--"
"Favors. I know. I know all too bleedin' well."
"Hm. Yes. You do. Now will be the appropriate time to deploy all of that accumulated knowledge and insight that you have been thought. I expect nothing less than your best efforts."
"Sure."
Gregory insists, "I mean it, Cassandra. You will be representing me. You will also be representing my--our Clan...Toreador. For me to tell you to be on your best behavior would be a disservice to you. But you have always been a rather...clever woman."
The Ghoul smirks lightly, "I suppose so, yeah."
The Aesthete firmly reminds her of her place within the structure of the Camarilla, "It's the insect's lesson: be on your best behavior around someone who could easily crush you like 1."
She gulps as a look of fear washes over her face. She nods and quietly says, "I--I understand, yeah. I get it. Manners. Posh, extra posh, innit?"
"This is not a joke, Cassandra. This is serious. I am owed favors and I owe just as many favors to many, many of Caine's Childer."
Cassandra queries, "Any reason why you're doing it all now? Why not only do a part of it and let the others simmer so when shit really gets hot, you can cash 'em in?"
Gregory's dead eyes flit to the side for a moment. He looks back at her and shakes his head, "I'm afraid that it is not that simple. I have my reasoning. And I surely do not owe you an explanation. But I will say this: I want to put all of my affairs in order."
He ominously tells her, "There's a reason why I have kept you away from most of it for the past 2 or so decades. Many reasons...but now is hardly the time for such things."
Cassandra's curiosity is piqued, "So, you're finally going to tell me what you've been up to, yeah? All those sudden trips to fancy locales and--"
"I've been doing it long before you became my Ghoul, Cassandra. But I strongly believe that now is the time to finally cash in on all of these favors. And it's time for you to represent me. It's time for you to put your wits, whatever insights you may possess, and whatever skills you have acquired over the years to good use."
Cassandra licks her lips and stares at the paper. On it are listed the names of the Clans (and a few Bloodlines) written in block letters. A generous amount of space has been left between each entry.
Gregory tells her, "You will be free to tackle this however you deem fit. I shall explain to whom I owe favors...and who owe me. I intend to see the proverbial slate being wiped clean in a...matter of weeks."
"Weeks? Wow. OK. What kind of...err...favors do you owe these people?"
Gregory dryly answers, "I have been involved in a few dealings over the decades, I have met many a sort of person- if some can even be called people anymore. They vary from eccentric to those in whose presence I shan't want to be in. Through my art-dealing, I have done them favors and they have done me favors. That's usually how it goes in Kindred Society."
Cassandra mumbles, "That's fucking vague. OK, never mind. Sorry. I'll just...take your word for it, yeah?"
"Yes, that would be preferable. Have I ever lied to you? Misled you? Backstabbed you?"
"No, no, and no. I'd hope bloody not! Why? Should I expect any surprises when I run around carrying these errands out for you?"
Gregory flatly answers, "You should be ready for anything. It is simply the nature of things, Cassandra. The nature of our world is one of treachery, manipulation, uncertainty, deceit, and betrayal...and violence."
Cassandra notes, "It's a pretty dark world, yeah. But I try me best to see the good in it, too."
"That's always been something admirable about you, Cassandra. What's that word they throw around? Oh, yes, bubbliness. Hm, yes, that. But be wary, all the same. Some of these people will not hesitate--" the words get caught in his mouth.
The Toreador goes silent for a moment. His eyes glaze over, and he seems to be lost in thought. The Domitor dreamily stares at something over his Ghoul's shoulder.
Cassandra has gotten used to this. She follows his gaze and spots a few falling leaves in the far distance.
Gregory shakes his head and comes back to reality. He clears his throat and blinks, "Oh. Where was I? Never mind. And another thing."
"Yeah?"
"Try not to sound so bloody Northern all the time. Some of the people whom you will find yourself interacting with are rather...peculiar, to say the least. Mildly put--"
Cassandra claps back, "I'll sound as bloody Northern as I damn well like! It's me accent, it's me way of--of speaking and expressing meself! How the fuck am I supposed to change that, eh? I'm not gonna start talking like a posh wanker, Greg!"
She does an imitation of someone who's posh. She elaborately lifts her chin and pouts haughtily, "Oh, look at me. Oh, darling butler, the poors are sooooooo quaint. Look at their council houses and their clothes. Is that a chav I spot in the distance! Oh, we simply must have a picture of it!"
Gregory sighs, "Yes, yes, fine. OK. I get the point loud and clear. You've made your argument. Fine. Be as Northern as you like. Just don't let it get in the way of your duties. It was stupid of me to--to even bring that up."
Cassandra nods, "Hmph, yeah. No shite. I am who I am. And is the other thing going to be an issue? Y'know, the--"
"I don't know. But if I were you, I wouldn't go around telling people. I--I know that times are different now. Everything's more open, but a lot of our kind simply haven't gotten with the modern era just yet. You know how some of us can be."
She ruffles her hair slightly and fixes her gaze to the ground, "Yeah, yeah. I know. So, I 'ave to keep me mouth shut about it, yeah? But it's a--a core part of who I am, Greg. I can't just shut that aspect- that part- of me off, y'know?"
Gregory clasps his hands together and nods, "I know. And, for what it's worth, I am terribly sorry about any...prejudices you may have to encounter in regard to that."
Cassandra winces, "Apologizing in advance is hardly confidence-boosting."
Regardless, she waves her hand and clicks her tongue, "Ah, it's fine. I'll find a way to...manage it. It's not like I'm gonna go around shouting it from the rooftops, innit?"
Gregory's smile doesn't quite reach his eyes, "Yes, yes, of course. But you should be free to be who you are. I've personally never had any problems with it. I've always seen it as something that makes you...well, you."
Cassandra lips curl into a smile, "Aww, thanks, Greg! It's--it's nice to hear that coming from you, yeah. And also, a bit reassuring."
"Just be careful out there. You have a deep and intimate knowledge of our kind. Utilize it to the max. This is something that you'll have to do on your own. Figure things out, improvise where you must. I shan't be accompanying you on any of these...journeys."
"Journeys? Cor blimey? Where are you sending me off to? Ibiza?"
"Nothing as fun or--or exotic as that, I'm afraid. Everything is well within the confines of the United Kingdom of Great Britain, Scotland, Ireland...and Wales, for some reason. Reasons that shall soon become apparent to you."
"Alright. Well, this is gonna be a bloody long night, innit? You sure you don't want--"
Gregory holds his hand out, "I will hunt later. This simply has to be done. The Hunger can wait."
Cassandra notes, "It's never a good idea to deny the Beast for that long."
"It can wait. This is simply of the utmost importance to me."
Cassandra pointedly asks, "Why now? Why all of a sudden? Is this some sort of eccentric Toreador thing that I'm not aware of? Is it something else? Some kind of...err...mid-Unlife crisis?"
Gregory's dead eyes narrow, "Eccentric? Mid-Unlife crisis? Far from it. Needless to say, I do not owe you an explanation as to why I'm doing this now. It is my prerogative to do so. As your Domitor, I am asking you- my Ghoul- to simply carry out these tasks on my behalf. Nothing more, nothing less."
Cassandra's eyebrows shoot up, "Wow. OK. S--sure. So, you either owe favors to- or are owed favors from- each and every single Clan? And a few Bloodlines, eh?"
"Yes." A very plain and emotionless answer.
"That's...uhm...oh, I'm pretty stoked about this, Greg. Any suggestions on how to get started? How to tackle this?" Cassandra taps the piece of paper on her thigh.
Gregory reaches out and grabs it from her. He takes a pen from the nearby glass table, clicks it, and starts making some edits. After 20 seconds or so, he hands it back to Cassandra.
"Cor blimey! I always knew that you were busy, but never THIS busy! And that writing was...fast, like, blazing fast, yeah."
Gregory dully says, "I have been in and around the art world as a dealer. And in those few short years, I have come across all sorts. And it is highly likely that you will cross paths with those same...people. Be on your guard, Cassandra. Watch what they say, how they say it, and always remember your learning."
She nods at him, a tight and uneasy look slowly forming on her otherwise soft features, "Yeah, yeah, OK. You're scaring me a bit, Greg. What's all of this really about?"
Gregory vacantly stares off into the distance. He comes back to reality and dryly explains, "It is simply the way things have to be. I...this needs to be done and there is nobody else in this world I trust enough to accomplish these tasks for me. I hope that I haven't made a mistake by choosing you."
The Ghoul clicks her tongue, "No need to be so condescending! Of bloody course, I'll do it! And I won't let you down!"
"Hmm. We have much to discuss. For now, I need some time to...mull some things over. You're free to go and do whatever it is that you usually do for the next few...hours, Cassandra. When you return, we shall talk about the specifics."
"Sure thing."
Gregory looks right into her hazel eyes and comments, "There's a reason why I'm not coming with you. As such, I'm authorizing you to act on my behalf and represent me. Furthermore, try not to call me up so bloody much. It's not safe to be using the phones, anyway, right?"
Cassandra nods her head up and down, "Mm-hmm. Not that safe, yeah. How about if I tackled it in alphabetical order, eh? Or sort of alphabetical order?"
The Toreador Domitor shrugs indifferently, "It's your choice. I shall simply give you some parting advice and leave you, largely, to your own devices. Know that your words and deeds reflect on me. And there are people- especially in this Domain- who are always watching, waiting for the slightest of slip-ups before they come swooping in."
Gregory's tone gets far more ominous, "And when they come for you, they won't come alone. Please, be on your best behavior. Be discreet, cautious, forge alliances wisely, know when to back away from a situation, and...well, you already know what to do. Keeping you insulated from the broader happenings of Kindred Society was a...choice on my part."
"A bad one or a good one?"
"I don't quite have a satisfactory answer to that, I'm afraid. Don't worry about work; I'll call them and speak with them later. You are to take your vacation now. This may very well drag on for a while."
Cassandra sighs and shakes her head, "Oh, a long one, innit? Ah, fine. I was hoping to use me vacation time to actually, y'know, go on a nice holiday. Sunny Spain or--or France or Italy."
Gregory reassures her, "Worry not, everything is well within the confines of...this country and its most immediate neighbors."
Cassandra softly chuckles, "You still dislike Wales, eh? Whatever happened there, anyway?"
"You might find out. Or you won't. Please be careful, Cassandra. This is not a game. This is not a holiday or a--a thrilling vacation filled with wonder and excitement at every turn, this is serious business. I myself am going to be very busy the next few nights. You have an advantage, however."
The Ghoul smiles widely, "I still get to go out in the sun, eat ice cream, do all that fun stuff. Poor you, hmm?"
"Yes. Woe is me. On your travels, you will come across a myriad of...individuals. Each with their own agendas, desires, and goals. Your journeys will take you to places and people unexpected. Don't take anything at face value and be ready to come across things that could very well challenge your view of the world."
"Wow. Uhm...sure. Yeah. I'm absolutely ready. It's not gonna be violent or--or really, REALLY dangerous, though? Or is it?" Cassandra searches his blank gaze for any sort of answer. None are immediately apparent.
Instead, he simply tells her, "I will give you a bit more of my Vitae if you're worried. Other than that, expect the unexpected. Be ready to adapt- rapidly, if need be- to situations that change at the drop of a hat. I can't promise a smooth and easy ride- it would be foolish and irresponsible of me to do so."
Cassandra nods slowly, "Yeah, no shit. Just don't cast me into a fucking mess without a bit of--of prep. Maybe throw in a pep talk, too. Eh? What about money, supplies, that sort of thing?"
"As your Domitor, it is my responsibility to take care of such things. I'll also transfer some money into your account so that you can make purchases as you see fit. Worry not: you'll always have enough for your needs. Maybe the occasional want, but I leave that sort of thing to you."
The Ghoul gives him a thumbs-up, "Thanks, Greg! Y'know, you don't 'ave to pump a lot of dosh into me account; I got...enough. What about IDs and all that other fun stuff?"
Gregory equably answers, "Again, I shall take care of it. I've been around long enough to know the right people in our circles who fancy themselves experts on matters pertaining to...forged documents." Mild disapproval laces his words.
Cassandra smirks, "Ooh, sounds proper shady! But we should really cover our tracks and...err...watch our backs, yeah? I'd hate for the baddies in the Second Inquisition to find us out."
"Hm. Yes. As a Ghoul, even you are responsible for ensuring that the Masquerade- the most important of all our Traditions- is upheld. Do not take it lightly, Cassandra. You and I are in this together."
"Heh. In more ways than 1. You look a lot paler than usual. Are you sure you don't need me help to--"
Gregory's palm faces her. He shakes his head, "I can handle it myself, Cassandra. I got on fine long before you came along. But...you have been good to me. As have I, I--I hope."
Cassandra smiles warmly, "You 'ave. Can I take the Lexus? Do you need me to take you somewhere? Are we expecting any guests this week?"
"No. And not that I'm aware of. Cassandra, the Lexus is yours- it's always been yours. I...don't like driving vehicles of any kind. None whatsoever."
She rises from her seat and pats him on the shoulder. She briefly flashes him a look of concern, "Are you doing alright? Y--you'll tell me if something's wrong with you, yeah?"
"Hmm. Enjoy your drive."
Cassandra chuckles, "I'm just going to the shops, Greg. I'm a bit low on some...err...necessities. I'll pay with me own cash, don't worry, OK?"
Gregory nods and waves her off, "Yes, yes. Be careful out there. And drive safely. You're going to need to be in peak physical condition for the road ahead."
Cassandra chuckles, "It's not the bloody Olympics, is it?"
Gregory responds, "Don't take these things so lightly, Cassandra. I have already told you what will happen if you let your guard down- many, many times. I shall also take my leave. When you return, we have much to discuss and plan for. We also need to make sure that you have everything that you need for this."
The Ghoul pats the side of her jeans and slowly exits her Domitor's fancy Haven. She stops and takes a wee look at some of the abstract art pieces he has adorning the walls of his living room.
"Very pretty!"
"Hmm. Goodbye, Cassandra. I shall be out, hunting. Are those hikers still near that creek?"
While still staring at the paintings, she nods, "Mm-hmm. A few lads and a single lass. Definitely uni students on a wee adventure or some sort of holiday."
"They will have to do."
"Don't kill them, yeah?"
"Of course. I may be a damned monster condemned to drink blood and hide away from the sun for all eternity, but I still have my Humanity, Cassandra. Some shreds of it left to--to cling to."
She murmurs, "I hope that it's a lot more than a few shreds, Greg. I--I don't like disposing of dead bodies. It's fucking terrible and--and it's bloody traumatic! Don't let 'em die, alright?"
The Toreador Domitor puts on a faded jacket, "I shall strive to do my best." Without saying another word, he slips into a pair of running shoes and starts to walk towards the door.
Cassandra steps outside and takes a moment to appreciate her surroundings on this beautiful autumn evening. Despite the gray gloominess that never quite seems to go away, she finds it to be quite lovely. She always does.
She inhales a lungful of fresh air and smoothly lets it out through her nostrils. A quick check of her pocket reveals that she still has her keys. She decides to stop by her cottage before heading out into the city proper.
Cassandra takes slow and deliberate steps, making sure that she does step on a few of the crunchy fallen leaves that blanket the ground. She lightly giggles, skips, and runs around, kicking some up into the air and watching them flutter to the ground.
Once back in the cozy place that she has called home for the past few years, she grabs her phone. It's a Blackberry device with a QWERTY keyboard; she's been meaning to get an Android smartphone, but she's not quite ready to let go of something that's she's deeply familiar with.
The Ghoul's phone begins to vibrate. Her ringtone is a pop song of some kind. Her brows knit together when she spots an unknown number, but she answers it anyway.
"Yeah? Who's this?"
A man's voice is on the other end of the line, sounding slightly excited and breathless. He sounds young, "Hey, it--it's me. Yeah. Me. D--don't say me full name. They're listening, right? Listen, I just heard about your new job. Congratulations."
Cassandra thinks to herself, "Horace? Horace Grimley? But--?"
Horace chuckles and admonishes her, "Yeah, congratulations on the new job. Listen, watch it out there, love."
"But how do you--"
He hangs up without saying a word. Cassandra stares at her phone in shock, disbelief, and worry. The last time she saw him, he was still a Ghoul in the service of a Malkavian named Damien. That was a decade ago. Now, he somehow has...insight to phone her- just as soon as Greg wrapped up with her- and congratulate her on...getting a new job?
Cassandra wonders to herself, "Is Horace a Malkavian now? But how did he know about--? Ah, shite, I'll have to make time to see him sometime this week."
Yes. He is. His Embrace was quite recent, actually. And the visions that he's been getting have been on the overwhelming side of things. She'll find out soon enough.
Like Cassandra, Horace was a Vassal Ghoul.
Anyways, the Lexus that Cassandra has came along with the house and the nearby cottage that she now resides in. Long story. The car in question has already done a lot of kilometers over the years. Despite being secondhand, it's a sleek and very modern-looking thing.
Gregory gave it to her since he has never been much of a vehicle man. Not even bicycles! In his time, he used to walk to his place of work (since it was so bloody close by). He also liked taking the Tube when he was still back in London. And maybe the occasional ride on the double-decker buses.
The Ghoul slides into the driver's seat, starts the car up, and begins to make her way into the city. The engines of the Lexus purr like kittens. She makes sure to stick to the speed limit.
For a car that came thrown in with a cheap (by any and all standards) house and a nearby cottage, it's quite impressive all things considered.
Cassandra lets her lovely hair hand loose, the wind blowing it around. She elects to keep the radio turned off, instead taking in the sounds of the cityscape that she cruises through.
She softly smiles to herself, feeling pretty damn good about the new "job" that she has, assuming one could even call this set of complex tasks a proper job.
The city of Gloughchester has a population of about 600,000 souls, give or take. It's a decent-sized place with some urban areas and some rural ones, too. Pretty average as far as British metropolises go, really.
For Greg and Cass, the move to this Domain from London was done for a lot of reasons. But perhaps the most important was the fact that it's a relatively quiet and low-key Domain where they could both live, go about what matters the most, and function in the 21st century.
Cass slows down when she passes by the Jobs Center. The line has been rather large as of late. People of all ages, genders, ethnicities, and backgrounds wait for their chance at some form of gainful employment. A sympathetic look flashes across her features as she drives by.
