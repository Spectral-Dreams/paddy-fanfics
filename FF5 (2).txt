Elise's eyebrows shoot up at that blunt remark. She chuckles a little, "You favor the direct approach, don't you, Miss Adams? You really are quite adorable."

But in this iteration of the world, perhaps it was never meant to be. In this version of things, in this variation, Lena's fate was bound up with other forces, other intriguing and interesting characters.
And they were already bound to meet. Sooner or later, Lena Adams was going to run into them. And they were going to run into her.
