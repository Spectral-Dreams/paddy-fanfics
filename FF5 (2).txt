Chapter Two: Totally NOT A Batcave
Later in the night...
Under an Irish pub named "O'Rory's Tavern," a cavernous maw is hidden deep beneath the ground and only accessible through a set of secure doors from the smoky pub's backroom. A narrow set of metallic stairs lead down into a secret space, and at the bottom of the steps is another secure door.
(One can't be too secure these nights!)
The metallic steps carry the echo of rapidly descending footsteps. The sound reverberates and bounces off the damp walls. The door at the bottom is shoved open by a broad shoulder.
A well-dressed man with deathly pale skin, cropped auburn hair, a smooth face, and three piercing, green eyes stomps in. The eye on his forehead glows a bright and unnatural shade of ivy green.
He tugs on the lapels of his suit and forces a deep breath through lungs that ceased functioning decades ago. The breathing is just out of habit, if nothing else.
Dampness pervades the underground space. There are pipes on the wall, dim lighting, a few humming freezers, a row of metal cots (for emergencies), a bunch of storage shelves, a workbench, and other assorted bits and bobs that may (or may not) be construed as junk.
The man kicks a crumpled can aside. His sharp and angular features twist in disgust as he notices the mess. His three green eyes dart all over the room.
A hiss of displeasure escapes his cold lips, "Goddamnit. Nossies made a mess. Again. Hmph. I've gotta talk to Cedric about this."
Sheriff (Kindred Sheriff, that is) Marshall Fairfield of Clan Salubri (three-eyed vampires) paces around the area and grumbles while cleaning up the mess that some of the local Nosferatu had made- they mainly used this space as a temporary resting/experimentation spot.
The Prince of this Domain, for his part, didn't mind such things- and the Nossies can be a persuasive bunch!
Sheriff Marshall grumbles some more while piling the empty beer cans, scrunched up blood bags, and other trash into a garbage bag. The plastic crinkles as the frustrated Sheriff tosses whatever he can into the bag. He grabs the bag and flings it halfway across the area.
"This is a fucking Masquerade breach waiting to happen. I'm gonna have to have a stern sit-down with those Nossies. And soon. Who leaves blood bags lying around the goddamn floor like this? That's not how things are supposed to be done!"
"And yet that's how the Crawlers in this Domain conduct themselves, darling," a feminine voice dripping with syrupy honey-sweetness replies.
A woman in an elegant, form-hugging dress saunters down the steps. She is of average build and height, pale as a sheet, has a round face free of any major blemishes, and black hair that cascades down to her lower back.
Her three brown eyes fixate upon the Sheriff.
He sighs, "Rosalind. So good of you to show up. Why don't you help me clean up the mess, hmm?"
Seneschal Rosalind Meyer (or Meyers to certain folks) waves her hand and sashays her way towards a worktable. She picks up the nearest thing to her (which happens to be a piece of paper) and daintily holds it in her cold fingers.
Seneschal Rosalind flicks the paper aside and smiles sweetly, "It is what it is, Marshall. The others should soon be here. Hopefully. There's...a bit to discuss and go over tonight. And how was your evening, Sheriff?" Her tone is genuine, gentle, and filled with care.
Sheriff Marshall rubs the bridge of his nose and answers, "Busy maintaining order in a city with half a million souls going to and fro. How 'bout you?"
The Salubri Seneschal languidly waves her hand about, "Oh, you know. The usual. Wedding prep, planning, and the like."
Sheriff Marshall mumbles, "It's been going on for half a century. Just stick the ring around his finger, say 'I do,' and give him a sloppy kiss on the lips. End of story."
"It's not that simple, Marshall. You- of all people- ought to know that. Come, now. Help me lay out the chairs, would you?"
"Hmm. Sure."
The two of them get around to diligently unfolding a bunch of metal chairs and setting them up in a way that they are now facing towards a slightly elevated platform. A few of them have duct tape covering the spots where the padding is coming undone.
While they work, Sheriff Marshall asks her, "So, uh...how are your Blood Dolls doing? Any fuss? Anyone getting any bright ideas?"
Seneschal Rosalind's pale face tics, "They know what they've gotten themselves into, Marshall. Don't worry, if they...try to pull a runner, my first call would be to...you. Satisfied?"
The other Salubri shrugs a shoulder, "I don't like hurting people unless it's absolutely necessary. And even then, I still don't like it. I already got the gist of how your arrangements work, Rosalind, but I have to ask--"
"Are they happy? Are they fulfilled? How intact is the Masquerade?"
Sheriff Marshall sighs heavily, "Took the words straight outta my mouth. Like always. Yeah, are--are they happy with you biting into them on a nightly basis?"
She plainly declares, "It's an entirely consensual feeding arrangement. We both stand to benefit in the long run, and they're perfectly safe and secure. And if it's worth anything, I do make a hearty meal for them. It's only just, isn't it?"
"I guess so. I hear someone."
A dapper man in his late thirties with very dark skin, full lips, luscious dreadlocks, and trimmed facial hair comes down the stairs. His clothes are immaculate and very well-tailored- a purple suit jacket, off-white slacks, and snakeskin shoes.
Some beads are tied around his icy hands, and his three dark eyes are somewhat glassy, but they quickly snap into focus when he looks at the Seneschal. A smile asserts itself across his face, and his teeth are all very white and very straight.
Seneschal Rosalind puts her hands on her chest and smiles back with just as much intensity. Her pupils dilate.
Prince Cedric Montgomery of Clan Salubri (again) approaches his Seneschal and kisses her on the lips. Just a brief peck. The two of them embrace each other and sway from side to side, longingly staring into each other's many eyes.
The Sheriff stands aside and lowers his head reverentially, "Prince Cedric. How have things been?"
Prince Cedric's deep voice almost echoes throughout this underground not-a-Batcave, "Well enough, Marshall. I heard that some Nosferatu had been...havin' a sleepover down here? Well, damn, I know that times are tough, but them Nossies are loaded."
Seneschal Rosalind nudges her fiancé (of fifty years!) with her elbow, "Oh, come now. Let's not paint in such broad strokes, honey. While the Primogen and his Whip may be...loaded in their own right, we still have plenty of Crawlers in this Domain who very much live on the margins. We mustn't forget that."
The Prince takes his Seneschal's hand into his, "I'm not saying it like that, baby. I'm just saying that them Sewer Rats gotta take better care of each other- like they do in other Domains! Warrens and shit. How come we still don't got none of that over here, hmm? Makes ya wonder, don't it?"
Sheriff Marshall chuckles, "It sure does, boss. It sure does."
Prince Cedric paces around and coolly asks, "So, how many we expecting for tonight's meetup? How's the security lookin'? Marshall?"
"All quiet, chief. Masquerade breaches are at an all-time low, everyone plays their role, stays in their lane, and...they get along with each other. Well enough for our liking, I guess." There is a hint of uncertainty in the Sheriff's voice.
Prince Cedric nods firmly, "That's good to hear! Hey, Rosalind, you decide on a wedding venue yet? And I do want them white flowers."
Seneschal Rosalind sighs, "Honestly? I'd prefer lilies. How 'bout we meet halfway through, hmm?"
"We still don't got a venue yet..."
Sheriff Marshall mutters, "Just do it in a church already."
The two lovers overhear that and chuckle amongst themselves. Sheriff Marshall joins along, but only a little. And the three of them go way back, so there's a lot of history between them.
The Prince tugs on his cuffs and sits down on a chair set up for him on the elevated platform. His Seneschal is seated to his left, while his Sheriff is seated to his right.
Now, they patiently wait for the other Kindred to file in.
A horrendously deformed, albeit well-dressed, man hobbles down the steps. His ashy, boil-ridden skin is stretched tightly over his elongated, gaunt, and twisted face. Bits of frizzy hair stick out from his scalp, and his mouth is wide open, revealing deeply rotten teeth and a chipped fang.
But his three-piece suit is immaculate.
Seneschal Rosalind addresses him, "Laurence! Come, come. Did anyone spot you?"
Laurence Lancaster of Clan Nosferatu glowers at everybody with his bloodshot, bulging eyes, "Of course not. I've been doing this shit for years on end." His voice is gruff, hoarse, and gravelly.
The Sheriff dryly says, "It's not 'this shit', Laurence. It's important! And where's Otto? Knowing him, he'd probably take our alternative entrance." He does a thumb-back gesture to a loose grate leading down, deeper into the cavern.
Laurence answers, "He ain't comin'. Sent me in his place. As Whip to the Primogen, I got the right to be here. I'll relay whatever the hell we waste our time talking 'bout to him, he'll sign off on it, and everything'll be copacetic."
The Prince sighs deeply and shrugs, "If you say so. And how has your week been, hmm?"
Attempts to inject cheer and be polite are met with foul grunts and fouler glares. Laurence folds his arms and sinks deeper into his seat. His sulking is obvious, and the others choose to ignore him for the most part.
(He's got myriads of issues).
His Embrace was a decade ago. He originally hails from Flimont City and is of the infamous Lancaster family (moneyed assholes, basically). One would think that being Embraced into such a Clan would've humbled him, given him a fresh perspective on matters, but it did quite the opposite.
His Embrace was a little too sudden and the process too rushed, and it did a helluva number on him. He couldn't go back to his crazy, overbearing father and as a result, his company- Lancaster-SYS- completely collapsed into many pieces and was sold off for pennies on the dollar. Parts, practically!
Laurence had no choice but to remain in Magnolia City ever since those events transpired. He's got three adult children (all sons from different women) who don't really bother keeping in touch. And none of them have really cared to find out what happened to daddy dearest.
Even then, Laurence is still a wealthy man in his own right. His shrewd business acumen, his callous ruthlessness, and his complete lack of mercy and human decency have kept him afloat and allowed him to live a very, VERY comfortable existence.
Heavy, nearly obnoxious footsteps descend down the metal stairs at a very rapid pace. The kind of footsteps one would expect from a person with very sturdy legs and very little patience.
A well-built, muscular man in his indeterminate thirties cracks his knuckles and walks over to the closest unoccupied chair. He sits down with a huff and grunts. His alabaster skin is slightly scarred, and he's wearing a blue blazer and pleated pants.
His two green eyes glare at everyone around him. His expression is stern, brooding, and very, very focused. His blonde hair is neatly combed to the side, and his ears are plugged with wired earphones.
Prince Cedric stares at him and calls out, "Amos Greenberg!"
Amos clicks his tongue in frustration and pulls out one of his earphones, "What?"
The Prince sighs and addresses him, "How you doing?"
"Fine." Amos shoves the earphone back into his ear, folds one leg over the other, and shifts about a little in his seat. He throws his head back and shuts his eyes. His pale face is smooth, but there are a few spots here and there.
Amos Greenberg is, by all accounts, an Assamite (Clan Banu Haqim), but say that to his face and he'll punch you dead- those muscles aren't just for show!
He identifies as a "Bnei HaShem". They are a bunch of Israel-loving, Jewish supremacist Assamites who vehemently and ferociously reject the heritage of their Blood in favor of some made up, ahistorical (one might even say anti-historical) Zionist bullshit. Typical.
There is a small, but problematic, Bnei HaShem presence in Magnolia City. As such, the actual Banu Haqim are a bit cautious about him and his associates and are attempting to keep an eye on him. A very close eye.
And many real Assamites have been put on the back foot by Amos's many attempts to destroy them all and set up shop in this Domain. All of this is occurring behind Prince Cedric's back.
Amos has been trying to ingratiate himself into the Prince's Court, but thus far, it hasn't worked out as well as he hoped it would.
The Prince asks him, "Amos, I got a question for you, boy."
Amos sighs heavily and takes out his earphone, some really loud music faintly bleeds out of the device, "What?!"
Sheriff Marshall does not like this False Assamite. He grits his teeth and demands, "First of all, show some goddamn respect and take those fucking things off. The Prince wants to have a conversation with you- the least you could do is take off those goddamn things and HEAR HIM OUT!"
Amos's voice is calm and even, "I'll do that when HE asks me nicely, Cyclops." And in goes the earphone again.
The False Assamite defiantly crosses his arms and raises his chin, glaring daggers back at the trio seated at the elevated platform.
Seneschal Rosalind's gaze darkens, "Well, that went about as well as expected." She politely smiles at Amos and averts her gaze when he looks back at her with a sour expression.
Seneschal Rosalind murmurs to herself, "He's a funny one."
The Sheriff growls, "That's it. I'm taking his head off--"
Prince Cedric places his hand on the other man's knee and urges him, "No, you ain't. Marshall, we already talked about this. The...Bnei HaShem haven't done a thing that can be interpreted as a violation of the Camarilla's Traditions. We don't got just cause to do away with them."
Sheriff Marshall snarls, "They're shady. I know it, I just know it, but I--I can't seem to do a damn thing about it!"
Seneschal Rosalind's voice is very soft, "It's alright, Sheriff. If they are...shady, then they'll slip up. And we'll be there to put them in their place."
The Sheriff's voice is exasperated, "But I--I can't seem to find a single goddamn violation that they've committed against the Traditions or any other law that exists! I can't reasonably pin a single fucking thing against them!"
He grimly adds, "But all those recent disappearances and murders aren't a coincidence. It all started when muscle man over there made his presence known to us."
Prince Cedric sighs, "Now you're just jumpin' to wild conclusions, Marshall. Sure, he was lurking around without presenting himself to me in accordance with the Fifth Tradition of Hospitality, but it don't mean that he was goin' around killin' folks and makin' people disappear."
Seneschal Rosalind put in, "Maybe he has a point, honey. There are coincidences, sure, yes. But perhaps he's right and there is a connection that we should seriously be looking into."
Prince Cedric narrows his eyes and stares at the smug Assassin, "I dunno, Ros. It feels like we're grasping at straws here. Lookin' around for a boogeyman under a bed, so to speak. I dunno. But...we should keep a closer eye on him and his people, hmm?"
Sheriff Marshall stifles a groan, "That's, like, the least we should be doing. And why have the actual Assamites stopped showing up to these meetings, hmm? I--I mean, Amos isn't even anybody's fucking Whip. Why is he even here?"
Seneschal Rosalind shrugs, "Well, to be fair, Marshall, these meetings are open to any and all of Caine's Childer. Amos has just as much of a right to be here as anybody else does. Even Ghouls are more than welcome to attend these meetings, you know."
The Sheriff replies, "Yeah, but Ghouls aren't that big of a threat to the Masquerade compared to some asshole who looks like he spends all of his time at the gym and nowhere else. I don't like him. I just don't. And it's not because he's Jewish, it's because he's an asshole!"
The False Assamite snorts. It's like he heard a part of the Sheriff's outburst and found it amusing.
Seneschal Rosalind's gaze is withering, "Well, you've made that abundantly clear to us on numerous occasions in the past. Let's just do what we can to keep the peace, watch over things with keen eyes, and...don't let Amos get to you, Marshall."
The Salubri Sheriff balls his hands into fists and mutters, "Whatever. S--sure. Sorry, Seneschal. I shouldn't have disrespected you l--like that."
Prince Cedric puts his hand on the knee of his fellow Peacemaker, "It's fine, Marshall. We get it; it ain't easy being Sheriff. But we do our best to make each other's Unlives- and duties to this Court- as easy and painless as possible."
The Sheriff nods, "Sorry. But I'm not dropping it. Something stinks and we better be getting to the bottom of it."
Prince Cedric leans to the side and covers the side of his mouth with his hand. He lowers his voice to a whisper, "If we plan on doing any investigating, then we better do it off-the-books, you dig? He'll see us comin' from a mile away. Me and Ros got something that we wanna run by you after this meetup ends. If you're interested."
Sheriff Marshall nods, "I am. Y--you're the Prince, after all."
Prince Cedric pulls away and clicks his tongue, "It ain't like that, Marshall. All our voices matter equally. Ain't that right, baby?"
Seneschal Rosalind beams and looks into her lover's three eyes, "Yes, indeed. The Second Tradition of the Domain implies- strongly- that a Prince's word is law, but all voices are valued equally. That's just how we do things around here."
Prince Cedric takes her hands in his and reassures, "And that's how we always will, baby." He kisses the top of her hands, and she lightly giggles in response.
A very brief and uncomfortable silence follows, but nobody has anything else to add. Laurence sulks while Amos bobs his head to the music. The other three are still engaged in their own little discussions.
After a few moments, Prince Cedric checks his watch and shakes his head, "If that Italian don't show up in the next minute, we're starting. He can get the particulars of this meeting from whoever he so pleases. Is a little punctuality too much to ask for these nights? We got some serious stuff to talk about!"
Laurence's gurgling grunt is enough to confirm his displeasure with this.
Amos clicks his tongue and grumbles, "C'mon, c'mon, start already. I got places to be, important shit to do."
Sheriff Marshall snarls, "YOU got places to be, important shit to do, huh, well how about--"
Seneschal Rosalind quickly intervenes, putting on the best possible smile she can muster, "Now, now, Marshall. We'll give our Italian friend a few more minutes. After all, the night is still young, isn't it?" A forced chuckle escapes her lips.
Amos's dead eyes dart over to her. They stare at each other for a few seconds before Prince Cedric puts his hand on his girlfriend's knee.
Gentle footsteps can be heard from the metal stairs. Everybody looks over. Everybody except Amos, who has his eyes shut and earphones plugged in.
A woman in her forties strides in. She is pale-skinned and somewhat thin. Her bobbed, blonde hair is neatly done, and her lips are painted a deep shade of red. A silky white shirt and a black skirt adorn her frame.
She profusely apologizes for being late, her Italian accent flaring at certain points.
"Scusi (ITA: Excuse me). I am so sorry for being late, Prince. Am--am I late? Where are the others? Only this many?"
Francesca Livroni is the Whip to the Primogen of the Hecata Clan, the Clan of Death.
She sets her Gucci purse on the free seat next to her, straightens herself out, and bows her head reverently. Then, she takes her seat in an elegant and poised manner.
The Necromancer looks around the room, takes note of Amos, and frowns slightly.
Laurence mumbles, "They sent the wrong Italian. Heh-heh."
Francesca retorts, "Mr. Lancaster, you are like me. Whip to the Primogen of our respective Clans. Where is Otto, anyway? Still brooding in his mansion?"
Laurence counters, "And where the hell is your Vincenzo, huh? That old Graverobber still trying to play around with shit he got no business interfering in?"
Francesca scowls, "He is busy." She refuses to fall for Laurence's cheap barbs.
"'Busy'. Uh-huh, sure..." And he huffs, grunts, and ignores Ms. Livroni.
Seneschal Rosalind claps her hands together, the sound echoing throughout this subterranean cavern, "Kindred of the Court! Members of the Council! Could I please have your attention."
She fixes the muscular not-Assamite with a somewhat severe look, and he grunts as a sign of acquiescence. The earplugs come off and he nods to her.
The Seneschal continues, "Thank you. Before we begin these proceedings, let us take a moment to reflect on these past few weeks, hmm? Just a short and quick moment, if you'd all be so kind."
Amos shrugs and says nothing.
Laurence lowers his head and clasps his scarred, deformed, and pus-ridden hands together. His breathing is heavy and labored. He shuts his eyes and mumbles something that nobody else can quite pick up on.
Francesca contemplatively stares at her surroundings. Her gaze becomes unfocused. Her fingers idly twitch and her lips slowly part. She absentmindedly strokes her left hand, which is concealed by a thick leather glove.
Her hazel eyes linger on the natural rock formations on the walls and the ceiling.
Seneschal Rosalind waits for everyone to settle down before continuing, "Good."
She looks over her shoulder. Prince Cedric nods back, and she returns her gaze to the rest of the assembly. She promptly declares, "First and foremost, let us remember to always do our best to maintain our Humanity and strive to be the best versions of ourselves that we can possibly be."
She waits for a beat before continuing, "Next, let us remember those who have, unfortunately, perished. We are...looking into the circumstances surrounding their untimely demises. That's all that we can share with you at this moment. Please, Francesca and Laurence...make sure that your respective Primogens get this information."
The Hecata vampire nods and begins to rapidly jot some things down in a thick leather journal of some kind.
The Nosferatu Kindred merely shrugs, "I'll give Otto the abridged version later on when I go and see him."
Seneschal Rosalind slowly replies, "Of course, whatever's comfortable with you, Mr. Lancaster. And Ms. Livroni, please make sure that your...book doesn't fall in the wrong hands, hmm? It'd be a darn shame if it did."
Francesca equably answers, "Sì (ITA: Yes). It is always with me and never leaves my side. I understand the gravity of this situation, Seneschal Rosalind. I will do my best to ensure the integrity of the Masquerade. Always."
Seneschal Rosalind looks back at the Prince, and he nods. He stands up and relieves her, "OK, now onto the matters at hand. There's been some shit goin' down in this city, and it's bad enough that we're starting to get concerned."
The Salubri Seneschal sits herself back down and keenly watches her boyfriend pace around.
His dreadlocks sway around when he walks about, "Somebody is murdering our people, and we're not entirely sure why. So far, most of the victims have been Assamites...Ghouls and Kindred alike." He looks at Amos.
Amos softly chuckles, "What? We don't know jack-squat about the perpetrator or perpetrators, right? The fuck you gazing at me for with those three eyes of yours, huh? Do I look like I had anything to do with that? You know how heated our disagreements with those fakers got, but...I wouldn't stoop THAT low. Heh, it's not my style."
He elaborately throws his arms up, "Oh, c'mon! Besides, what about all those other murders, huh? From what I heard, it's connected to the port and the docks."
Sheriff Marshall strokes his chin, "Huh. Right. What about those grisly killings? Cedric? I--I mean, Prince Cedric, shouldn't we also be concerned about that? Port authorities are dropping dead like flies, left and right."
He recounts, "Dockworkers, supervisors, cargo handlers, vehicle operators, office accountants, clerks...even managers have been found around town with their throats cut or their chests split open. Some of them were still warm when the cops found 'em."
He glares at Amos balefully, "And all of the murders happened at night- I read the police reports..."
The False Assamite side-eyes the Salubri Sheriff, "I don't quite like what you're implying, Salubrian." His tone is quite menacing and very hostile.
Sheriff Marshall lowers his head in shame when Prince Cedric cuts in, "Marshall, can it. Now. Amos, we are terribly sorry for that. Tensions, as you can imagine, are quite high right now."
Amos's dead eyes narrow and his brows furrow, "Yeah, so you say. I don't come here to be accused, OK? My people have a long history of being blamed for all of society's woes. We've been put in the mud before, and we won't put up with it anymore. We're done being the perfect victim for the goyim to ogle and gawk at."
Sheesh, yikes.
Prince Cedric grimaces slightly, "OK, let's not even go down that road. We ain't here to be accusin' no one of anything. Do you, by any chance, happen to know anything that could be of use to us, Mr. Greenberg?"
Amos lifts his chin, "How so? Useful in what way, exactly?"
The three-eyed Prince says, "Any of your...friends work in the docks or around the ports? Do you...have contacts there? Anybody who might've seen something that can help shed some light on this nightmare? Innocent folks are getting hurt, dying, and losing their livelihoods. It's not right. It ain't right."
Amos callously shrugs, his gaze is intense and sharp, "Yes, it's all very tragic and all, but people- mortals, especially- die. From what I see on the news, crime's been going up lately. Heh, no surprise there. I've had to change the locks on some of my--"
Seneschal Rosalind counters, "I don't know what 'news' you've been watching, but crime in this city has actually declined over the years- it's even said to be at an all-time low! Maybe you ought to check your sources before confidently citing them as fact. Be very cautious before making such bold declarations."
However, she does concede, "But I will admit that the sudden spike in violence is a bit disturbing, yes. But we are creatures, primarily, of the long run. Just something for us all to keep in mind."
Amos snorts, "OK, whatever. Crime's up, crime's down. Makes no difference to us Kindred, right? Just as long as we got our warm bodies to chomp into. Speaking of which, is anyone here Hungry?"
Seneschal Rosalind's patience is quickly running out, "Mr. Greenberg. We extend these invites to you as a gesture of good faith. While we are acutely aware of your disagreements with the...other Assamites, this is no time for petty infighting. Please, try and conduct yourself a little better."
Amos sighs, "Yeah, well, I'm Hungry--"
Francesca tells him, "Then, you should've fed before coming here? Or did you expect to be given something- or someone- to slake your eternal thirst?"
Amos snickers, "Now that you mentioned it...do we get subsidized meals down here? Hmm? Hmm?" His eyebrows wiggle up and down.
Prince Cedric shakes his head, "No, we do not. But in the future, I STRONGLY recommend that you keep the Beast at bay before showing up to these meetings. We don't want- or need- anybody Frenzying around like a damn fool."
He gestures to Sheriff Marshall in order to reinforce his point. The Sheriff is a tad bit tense, but he nods and puts in, "Yep. I'd hate to have to put down a Kindred lost in the Wassail."
Amos scoffs, "What's the worst that could happen? Wassail is only if you go too damn long without feeding. You Frenzy, you Frenzy. Just pick up the pieces, get rid of the evidence, and move on."
Seneschal Rosalind visibly grimaces at how indifferent Amos is acting, "No, it's not as simple as that, Mr. Greenberg."
The False Assamite snickers, "Oh, yeah, you'd know, wouldn't you--"
Prince Cedric butts in and points a finger at Amos, "ENOUGH! Don't even think about goin' there! I strongly recommend that you drop this RIGHT NOW!"
Laurence dryly says, "What? Is Rosalind any better than us cuz she only bites into...willing vessels? Consensual feedings and shit, tch. But we're all Beasts."
Francesca stays silent, opting not to say anything or offer her own two cents on the matter.
A deathly silence follows for a moment. Seneschal Rosalind can't bring herself to look at anyone, and she's wringing her hands. Her jaw is clenched, and her brows are furrowed. Her fellow Salubri are just as uncomfortable as her.
Amos adjusts the patterned yarmulke on his head and casually asks, "So, uh...is that it for tonight? I got places to be."
Sheriff Marshall shakes his head and defers to the Prince, who is heaving with barely concealed rage. He calms himself down, forces a deep breath through lifeless lungs, and blinks his three eyes.
Prince Cedric straightens his back and lifts his chin, "We're done when I say we're done. Sit your asses down and don't move until I'm done talkin'. Understood?"
Everyone nods.
The Prince proceeds, "It ain't a full house tonight, but it's acceptable. Whips, please ensure that your respective Primogens get the message about this meetup. I...understand why they ain't present, but in the future, I'd strongly advise that they make the time, move their schedules around, and show up."
Laurence mutters, "Yeah, sure thing."
Francesca firmly nods and quietly jots down some notes, "OK. I will be sure to remind Vincenzo."
Prince Cedric stares at Amos, "And YOU wouldn't happen to know what happened to the Banu Haqim Primogen, would you?"
The Bnei Hashem dismissively waves his hand and scoffs, "He probably got Beckoned away? Disappeared? Went for a really long fucking walk? Gone with the wind? Who gives a shit. I don't."
Sheriff Marshall sighs, "Dr. George Fateh informed us that the Assamite Primogen hasn't been seen...in a while. And I'd trust the good doctor...any night over--" He stops himself from saying more, but he makes sure to give Amos a very pointed look.
The False Assamite does not return it. All he does is smirk fiendishly.
Seneschal Rosalind narrows her three eyes and calls out to the Hecata, "Miss Livroni, that's a nice purse you've got over there. When we last saw each other, I don't believe you had it with you."
The Italian vampire looks at the purse and says, "Oh, this? It is...I found it. Sì, it is...something I...err...located. It is a long story, and I do not know if I would be allowed to share it with you, Seneschal. Scusa (ITA: Sorry)."
