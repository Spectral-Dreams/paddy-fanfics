Chapter Six: RBF
The sun begins to rise over Magnolia City. It peeks over the horizon with an orange glow, coating the world beneath its radiance with warm light. The chill and dewiness of night fade away and give way to a (relatively) cool morning.
Lena awakens with a start- that damned infinite drowning/not-drowning dream haunted her again. She wipes the sweat off her forehead and tries to slow her racing heartbeat. She calmly breathes out, rests her tensed body on the mattress, and shuts her eyes.
For a few moments, of course.
Shower is next. But first, Lena checks out her clothing and checks her phone for messages and texts. A few are from Yvonne and she's just checking on her. The rest are work-related stuff, namely, stuff pertaining to her job as a PI.
Some clients wanna know about their respective spouse's (or partner's) activities, whereabouts, movements, and so forth.
Lena sighs. Her fingers glide across the virtual keyboard as she sends text after text to each and every curious (antsy) client who needs them answers.
The warm water brings a serene calmness to Lena's mind and body. She makes sure to use a nifty face wash that Yvonne absolutely swears by. Afterwards, Lena emerges from the shower looking (and smelling) fresher than ever.
She slips into her work clothes with a practiced ease, applies a bit of makeup, grabs her purse, locks the door, and shuffles outta her house in the 'burbs!
First stop?
GROUND BEANZ!
Like always, it is full at this time of the day. The Wednesday morning crowd is an eclectic mix of young hipsters, suburban moms whose SUVs are getting obnoxiously bigger (and nastier) with each passing year, baggy-eyed nightshift drones looking to stay awake long enough to get home, and some buskers preparing for the long day ahead.
Lena lowers her head while queuing up behind two geriatrics who are taking their time deciding on what to get. She takes a peek at her phone, skims through various news articles (with heavy skepticism), and then slips it back into the pocket of her duffel coat.
The baristas with aching feet and (slightly) hunched backs go about their usual routine with an efficiency born out of years of experience. If only their pay reflected that more accurately.
Lena, being the coffee fiend that she is, orders a double espresso and some ginger biscuits- those two people in front of her took quite a while, so she'll have to treat the biscuits as breakfast. She also texts Yvonne while patiently waiting for her stuff.
Yvonne texts back: "U looked gud in that dress, gurl. How was the date, anyways?? Deets, gurl, deets."
Lena chuckles faintly: "I don't see it quite working out. She's nice and all, but...ehh. Got too much baggage, to put it kindly."
Yvonne texts back with lotsa emojis: "Baggage? Hmmmm. Send her my way, then :-)"
Lena snickers: "Hm. Sure. But she wants to remain friends. just friends."
"We also started out as 'just friends' :-P and now look at us!"
"That's different, lol. How's life? BTW, we should catch up. U free 4 lunch 2day???"
Yvonne replies with a bunch of crying face emojis: "I fucking wish, gurl. Gotta pull off dat overtime. u know how it is. Retail's a bitch sometimes, but we should meet...and maybe..."
Lena's eyebrows go up at that. She can't help but giggle: "And maybe...?"
Yvonne texts back: "its a bit 2 early for a tease :-P. but u know wat i mean, rite? Anyways, gotta go now. If i had to take a guess, ur still waiting for your coffee. gurl, you got issues when it comes 2 caffeine."
Lena shakes her head: "Hah! I probably do. bye."
The PI pays for her stuff, nay a cent more in the form of tips and chomps down her on her ginger biscuits while washing them down with espresso. The morning sun paints a very picturesque sight.
Buildings of glass and steel reflect the glorious light from above. Streetcars chug along their tracks with mechanical precision. The ground beneath Lena's pumps rumbles softly as a metro train passes by. Pedestrians are either busy chatting away or talking on their phones.
Crystal Street Station looks as bustling as ever. It's business as usual here. People in suits flock to and fro the underground. The surrounding restaurants and fast-food joints are all crammed full of customers. There's a long line in front of a kiosk selling newspapers and magazines.
A nearby billboard promotes a new energy drink, of course. It's the most cookie-cutter neon sign imaginable- photoshopped white woman smiling eerily while "holding" a can of whatever that heart-exploding sludge is.
Ads flash on numerous screens positioned strategically around the subway station. Like all public transit hubs in this Domain, Crystal Street Station features countless small shops and kiosks (for commuters) who are either buying stuff or otherwise milling about.
Lena patiently waits for her train. The caffeine and ginger make her feel rejuvenated, but she's already getting the tingles. She ALREADY wants another cuppa joe.
A small one.
She shivers in place, licks her lips, and fidgets with her hands. To keep her mind off that itch, Lena decides to mentally go over her ongoing cases. The PI leans against a non-grimy portion of the wall and looks up at the ceiling.
Lena lowers her sunglasses and stares at the ceiling for a few moments. Her amber eyes slowly move from left to right as if she is reading something on the tiles above.
The choo-choo train soon comes to a halt. Lena dutifully waits on the side as the doors hiss open. The more impatient folks and the slow walkers hustle on out. She gives 'em a wide berth, keeping her head lowered.
At last, it is time to board! She holds onto a hanging strap and decides to call her mom up.
Susie answers, but there is a certain tiredness in her voice, "Mmm-hmm?"
"Hi, mom...you--are you OK? I can always call back later--"
Susie yawns and interjects, "No, no, it's fine, sweetie. It's fine. Yoshi's just resting his...eyes."
Lena concernedly asks, "How is dad really doing?"
"Oh, y'know. He's...hanging in there."
"Mom..."
Susie lets out a sigh of resignation, "OK, OK, he's...not doing too good, Lena. But listen, I don't want you getting all worried, OK? I can take care of my husband just fine. You go, live your life, meet someone who'll make you happy, and let me deal with things back home, OK?"
Lena sniffs and mumbles, "It doesn't have to be like this, mom. What's stopping me f--from moving back in with you two, huh?"
Susie points out, "Because this is America, sweetie. Living with your parents is...kinda frowned upon when you're at that age. Don't worry about us. Me and your dad are handling it."
Lena grits her teeth, "That's bullshit! I--I have no issues with living with you two; neither does dad!" Given how she raised her voice a little, some of the passengers give her varying looks from quirked eyebrows to sympathetic grimaces.
The PI, sensing the unwanted attention, takes a deep breath and continues in a much softer tone, "OK. S--sorry. I shouldn't have raised my voice at you, mom. I'm sorry. But the point is that I have absolutely NO issue living with you. Hell, I'm even ready to bring the both of you over here!"
Susie immediately dismisses the idea, "No. I--I dunno about that, sweetie. True, your dad would've liked it and all, but I feel that it might just affect your chances...at everything."
Mom chuckles and goes on, "Like imagine you wanted to bring somebody home and there your dad was, snoring on the couch. That'd be kinda awkward. Or wait, wait! What if the interviewer asked you about your living uh...arrangements? 'I live with my parents' is hardly gonna impress them, sweetie."
Lena rolls her eyes, "You're worried that the two of you would, what, slow me down? Or worse yet, jeopardize my future?"
The awkward silence on the other end of the line is quite heartbreaking. Lena does her best to avoid giving away her emotions and feelings, but it's quite difficult in the face of this...rabidly individualistic American society where adults living with their parents is viciously looked down upon.
Lena sardonically says, "No need to answer, mom. I--I get it. But I'm still sending some money over. And so is Roland."
Susie gratefully responds, "And we are grateful for it, sweetie. What you and your brother do for us is--is--"
Her voice breaks and she starts to gently sob. Lena bites down on her lip and waits for her mom to calm down.
Given that the gumshoe is almost nearing her destination, she hastily says, "Listen, mom. I--I gotta go. Take care, OK? And if things get...really bad, you can always call me up. Me and Roland are there for you- both of you. Just like you were there for us when we were little. Now, it's our turn to take care of you."
Susie snorts indignantly, "Aw, c'mon, we're not THAT old, y'know."
Lena briefly chuckles, says goodbye to her mother, and ends the call. It'll be quite a few more minutes before her station arrives. She wipes the tears away from her face and sniffs again.
Work is...work. The early hours of the early morning zoom by in the blurriest of blurs. Lena dutifully carries out the tasks that keep a roof over head and some cash in her pocket.
When she has the chance to do so, she routinely checks in with clients (PI clients, that is) via phone or text, schedules appointments with them (and others), updates them on various aspects of their respective cases, and otherwise stays on top of things.
(There is yet another clause in Lena's contract which unambiguously stipulates that engaging in any second job or side activities while on the clock are strictly forbidden).
But, eh, whatevs. Fuck that. Lena can literally see a co-worker of hers who is playing Counter-Strike (one of the older versions that runs on older machines) while giggling like a goblin coming upon a ton of shiny, shiny loot.
After what feels like an eternity of filing, pointless busywork, and even more spreadsheets, it is now time for lunch! Lena tosses her empty coffee cup into the bin, fixes her ponytail, and heads downstairs to see what she can fill her belly with.
After all, a hardworking gal like her needs her food. And her coffee.
ESPECIALLY HER COFFEE.
Miss Adams takes her scran at an open-air joint. It's more crowded than usual, but that just means that the food's good. Usually. She opts for a plate of extra-cheesy nachos and some churros. Hmm, yummy!
While eating, her facial muscles are as relaxed as they could possibly be. She rubs the side of her neck, slurps on her lemonade, and blandly looks around her while slowly chewing on her food.
Lena nods at passersby in the most cursory manner imaginable. And she does it because, well, it's just polite, innit?
Even then, Lena Akira has practically mastered her RBF (Resting Bitch Face) down to a tee- that's just how things are for her.
(It's worth noting that, in her younger years, she had an angsty emo phase that lasted for exactly five days).
Lena grunts out a chuckle when that memory resurfaces- it causes her to cringe a little bit.
Black makeup, stuck-out tongue, copious amounts of eyeliner ("borrowed" from the school's most popular girl), an attempt at a belly piercing (mom DID NOT like that), super-loud music at night (dad DID NOT like that), and shredding on the guitar (Roland laughed at that).
While still maintaining her RBF, Lena finishes up her food, collects her stuff, and walks out without leaving a tip. She already paid for her food and all, but tipping is just not something that she can, in good faith, do.
Lena cracks a smile for the first time that day. A small smile. As she heads back up to the second floor, her phone vibrates in the pocket of her duffel coat. She pulls it out and clearly sees the contact's name: "Toby Fetscher".
She sighs and answers it, "Make it quick, Toby. I'm getting back to work."
Toby is a little taken aback by his friend's tone of voice, "Um...wow. Not exactly how I'd greet a close friend, but OK. You do you, I guess. I uh...called because I'm worried about Eddie. You know, Eddie--"
Lena flatly responds, "I know who our friend is. Anyway, what's got you so concerned about him?"
Toby's cheeks puff out when he exhales loudly into the phone. He takes a second or two to collect his thoughts before going on, "Yeah, he's been...acting shifty lately. He said he was off to visit someone. Who? He--he wouldn't say. He's hiding something from us, Lena. I just know it."
"Maybe it's nothing. We already had this--"
Toby clicks his tongue, "C'mon, Lena don't be like this...all dismissive and--and catty and smart-mouthed all the time. Eddie's our friend. I mean, c'mon, we've got lunch planned for tomorrow. And you're, well, invited to join us. Anyways, back to Eddie. Are you getting my drift or what?"
"'Catty,' huh? Hmph. Never mind. Eddie has been...showing off. I scrolled through his Facebook profile and saw a whole bunch of pictures of him showing off."
"Heh, yeah, I took some of those for him. He still won't tell me how he suddenly came across all that cash, though. It's weird. And I--I'm worried. What if he got in w--with a bad crowd or--or--"
Lena finds it pretty odd that her friend is stuttering so much and being so paranoid, "Toby. Stop. Relax. Eddie's a big boy- he can take care of himself just fine, right? Besides, you work with him day in and day out. How is he when you're both at the garage?"
Toby shrugs, "He's...fine, I guess. He--he said that he wants to pay for tomorrow's lunch. And he wants to take us both to that fancy new place downtown. Said it'd be a treat for us."
"Wow. Um...OK. Great."
Toby makes a few grunting noises in his throat. He shuffles on his seat and grunts some more.
Lena frowns and asks, "Whoa, what's going on? You sound like you're in pain."
"Nah, just my old chest binder giving me some issues- y'know how it is. I've had it for--for quite a while now. We've been through a--a lot together. Look, regarding my previous comment about you being catty, I dunno if you intentionally mean it or not, but sometimes, you do sound kinda--"
The PI interjects, "Toby...let's not go there right now. I have work to do."
"Hmm. Fine. But we're not done having this conversation, Lena. Eddie's my friend- he's yours, too- and I just wanna make sure that everything's OK with him."
Lena reassures him, "Everything IS fine with him, Toby. But just in case it isn't, we can check on him later."
Toby chuckles, "'We'?"
"Who's being catty now, huh? Listen, I gotta go now. Take care, Toby. See you and Ed at lunch tomorrow."
"Mm-hmm. Bye!! And...just for the record, I wasn't gonna call you bitchy. But you can be kinda...dismissively cold and off-putting sometimes, y'know?"
Lena dully responds, "Never said I was perfect. Have a great afternoon."
After hanging up, Lena looks up at the ceiling and yawns a little.
She helps herself to a cup of coffee and gets busy on the remainder of her work. Her fellow Pluto Enterprises employees either work, watch the clocks (conspicuously), gossip by the watercoolers (inconspicuously) or otherwise sleep (and then get woken up by a grumpy co-worker who felt it their sworn duty to do so).
Lena- who is fueled by caffeine- powers through her work till the time's up. By four forty-four, she springs up from her seat, gets her erratic breathing under control, grabs her stuff, and prepares to head straight for her PI office.
There's work that needs to be done; there are people who need to be followed around!
Gary the bossman watches her saunter away with those oddly weary eyes of his. Lena shivers and slowly turns her head to meet his gaze. He musters up a smile, weakly waves, nods, and then goes back to looking through his iPad.
The afternoon sun beats down upon Lena's sunglasses-clad face. Her shades protect her from the glare coming from the vehicle's windows and other surfaces. Ms. Adams goes to her PI office and begins to prepare herself for the (long) evening ahead.
Lena checks that her DSLR camera is working perfectly and that the lenses are in tip-top shape. She also ensures that there's a fresh memory card inserted and that the batteries are charged up.
If she's lucky, it'll be an easy night- just follow her mark, get "the goods" on them (see if they're cheating or not), and then she can go home. If not, she'll probably have to hide herself in some bushes and wait a REALLY LONG time before taking those all-important (and compromising) shots.
Lena unzips her duffel coat, slings it over her chair, and leans against the desk while absentmindedly fidgeting with her hands. She paces around for a few moments, fetches herself a cuppa joe (or five), and settles back down into her seat with a long and drawn-out sigh.
The sun begins the unceremonious process of setting. For a moment, the city's landscapes and skylines are painted with beautiful shades of red, orange, and pink. Picturesque is the only way to describe it.
This is Magnolia City- sturdy brownstone buildings, a handful of stable brutalist structures, and a ton of magnolias.
The denizens of this Domain go about their lives. Throngs of people head home from work. The buses and subway trains are loaded full of tired people who just want to get home already, throw their (stinky) feet up on the couch, and unwind.
The sun soon fades away- entirely. Now is the time for darkness to engulf everything, to reign supreme, as it were. Once that great and terrible star of fire has completely retreated, nighttime firmly sets in.
And the Kindred begin to stir awake.
Lena counts down the seconds while shaking her leg up and down. She'll head out once it's a bit darker- she can't really be seen stalking people out in broad daylight now, can she?
(But she still has to keep the artificial light in mind. Then again, she didn't get to where she is by being careless and reckless).
In the meanwhile, the PI keeps herself occupied by scrolling through Facebook, liking some stuff from her friends (close, mutual, or otherwise).

Mortals prepare to go about their day, looking forward to the approaching weekend with varying degrees of glee and enthusiasm. Most of those citizens are blissfully unaware of the supernatural that lies under the surface.
It lurks beneath their shuffling feet, roaming the shadows and sewers, and awaiting its inevitable chance to feast on the blood of the innocent and the guilty alike.
Lena is awakened by her alarm clock. She pulls herself up out of bed and rubs her face, before going to the bathroom to freshen up. She slips out of her clothing, tosses all of it into the nearby hamper, and stands in the shower.
She turns it on to the perfect temperature and lets the water wash over her sweaty, grimy, naked body. She closes her eyes and takes a deep breath, clearing her mind and savoring the warmth and wetness in equal measures.
The water pelts the back of her neck and helps loosen all the stress and tension accumulated over the past couple of days. Once done, the dripping-wet PI steps out, dries herself off, puts her hair up in a towel, and then wraps another towel around her body.
Lena brushes her teeth, spits the toothpaste out, gargles some peppermint mouthwash, and then spits that out as well. She grimaces while it all gets washed down the drain.
She dreads tomorrow- Saturday- owing to the inescapable fact that she has work even on that day, too. For now, however, she slips into her office attire, applies a decent amount of makeup, and heads to the kitchen to fix herself up a pot of coffee.
And something to eat. Honey-flavored cereal clatters into a round, ceramic bowl. Copious amounts of milk flows into the mix, and a spoon digs into the sugary, edible cluster.
Crunching noises echo throughout Lena's suburban house as she eats her breakfast. She sips on her (first but by no means last) cuppa coffee and winces at how hot and strong it is- the ideal blend, no less!
Miss Adams clears up, finishes the remainder of her caffeinated stuff, and then ties her jet-black hair up into a ponytail. Before the sunglasses come on, her phone starts to ring- just the average and generic default ringtone.
She picks it up and asks, "Hello?"
Jake Woodward's shaky voice is on the other end, "Hey. Hi. Uh...sorry for bothering you, Lena. I hope that I'm not calling too early?"
"Oh, no, not at all. I was just getting ready to head to work. You're...calling about the case, right?"
"Y--yes. I don't mean to pry or--or be pushy- you are doing this for free, after all- but, um...have you made any progress with your investigation? Like I said, I don't mean to rush you; I understand how delicate these situations are. I'm just w--worried, y'know?"
Lena equably answers, "I'm gonna expand my search. I want to cover all of my bases, so to speak. I was meaning to do it last night, but something came up and it demanded my immediate attention, so...I'm really sorry about that, Jake."
Jake tells her, "It's no issue, Lena. Please keep me in the loop and let me know what you find, OK? As long as you're working hard, then..."
Lena promises, "I'll do what I can to find Tyler's killer. And if you think of anything else that may be helpful, just shoot me a text or call me, alright? I may not immediately respond, but rest assured, I'll see it and get back to you as soon as I possibly can."
Jake stammers, "T--thank you, Lena. If--if you do find something important, call me. The moment that you do, I wanna be the first to hear it. Umm...tell me one thing before you go, though."
"Sure, anything. What's on your mind?"
The client fearfully asks, "Is there still a chance of catching the people who did this to my boy?"
Lena herself is uncertain and it is reflected in her tone, "I don't wanna get your hopes up, Jake. All I can do from my end is my job. But I'll let you know if and when I do find something. You'll be the first to know if the situation changes, OK?"
"Yeah, OK, t--thanks for taking me seriously. After having been looked down upon by the city's authorities for weeks on end, it's finally nice to be, well, heard. Respected, y'know?"
Lena nods slowly, "Yeah, I can definitely empathize with that, Jake. I'll get to the bottom of this sooner or later. Goodbye."
Jake sniffs and says, "OK. Bye." He hangs up.
The mortal PI shoves her phone into the pocket of her duffel coat, fixes her hair a little, cleans up the tabletop, and locks the door behind her. With a brisk stride, Lena Adams moves forth, walking along familiar (and well-trodden) paths to get to her workplace.
Expansive and towering structures of glass and steel loom up ahead, their many reflective windows glaring the bright morning sun. People bustle back and forth, to and fro. Mortal humanity is out and about, flocking to their respective jobs or places of learning or whatever.
Since caffeine already flows through her system, Lena decides against swinging by GROUND BEANZ. She raises her chin and strides past the shop and straight towards Crystal Street Station.
Throngs of warm-bodied persons stand in crowded, grumbling masses as the train screeches to a halt and the doors hiss open. Clusters of people flow out of the train and walk to the steps, and others come clambering in order to board the train.
Lena manages to find a seat next to a pudgy kid who's wearing a red shirt and khakis. He's red in the face and his forehead is glistening with beads of sweat. He doesn't give the PI a second glance, engrossed as he is in getting his ragged breathing under control.
Ms. Adams regards him with only the slightest tinge of concern. Out of common courtesy, she dryly asks him, "You doing OK?"
In between pants, the young boy mutters, "Could be better. This summer heat is awful. I just ran a couple of laps around the station. Trying--trying to lose some...weight, y'know? Not exactly going swimmingly, though."
"Hmm. Sure."
The rest of the ride goes by in relative silence. The kid makes a few noises that Lena finds irritating- she wrinkles her nose and subtly shuffles away from him, just a little bit.
Once at the building where Pluto Enterprises is situated, the mortal PI gets checked- and frisked- at the usual spot. She passes the screening process with flying colors and is allowed to pass.
She settles into her chair and sees that today's desktop background of the day is a landscape photo. It's of a small town tucked away in the mountains, all snow-capped and very picturesque, indeed. A small factoid below the image causes Lena to mutter under her breath.
A small part of her partially resents not having Saturdays off. Lena was planning on going on some dates to top the week off and all- some rather intriguing matches have turned up, and she has taken the initiative to contact these lovely vixens.
During a short break, her amber eyes flit over to the TV screen mounted high up against the wall. It's the fucking financial-related news, and Lena actively hates every single part of it. She rolls her eyes and goes back to looking through her phone.
Some really pretty (to her) faces catch her attention. She sends a few mild messages of interest to prospective dates. However, one lass in particular has gotten her attention and is determined not to let go.
"Allison Moretta," Lena mumbles to herself while studying the profile picture. The False Assamite has dyed the tips of her hair green and blue. Her half-smile is soft and her dead eyes twinkle with suggestive mischief.
Lena bites her lower lip, "Hmm. Allison, huh? Maybe...hmm. OK...Allie, let's see where this will take us..."
However, before she gets the chance to send a message, the bossman steps up to her and somewhat urgently says, "Miss Adams. I've got an assignment for you."
Lena stifles a groan, locks her phone, and looks up at him. Without smiling, she blandly asks, "What is it, Gary?"
"I just emailed it over to you. Make sure that you get it done with before lunchtime, OK? And no staring at your phone so much- you're on company time. And company time is company money, so make the most of it and work hard."
Miss Adams dryly nods to her boss, "Understood. I'll get right on it."
"Good, good. Excellent," Mr. Stuttgart says. He jovially saunters away and leaves Lena to her own devices, including a scowl.
She half-whispers, "He didn't even mention my raise- even after I formally submitted a request and did all of that shit. I swear, sometimes, the people who run PE are nothing but bean counters. Tch."
She can't help but ask herself if the higher-ups just glossed over the whole thing entirely. It's certainly...possible, all things considered.
Her typing becomes angrier as the day drags on. Time is passed by engaging in some idle and mind-numbing watercooler gossip. Some of Lena's co-workers speak about the sudden rise in violent and grisly crimes- murders, particularly- happening across the city.
Another exclaims that he saw something unbelievable the other day. He says that he might've hallucinated it, but he swears on everything which is relatively sober that he saw a lanky person with a misshapen head (resembling a caved-in skull) pop out of a manhole and furtively creep through the alleyways.
"And I--I think he was dressed in this, like, really fancy coat that came down to his knees or something, y'know? Either way, it was weird and freaking me out. I think he saw me. And I know it was a he...if the growls were...uhm...anything to go by."
Lena's brows slowly and steadily furrow. Skepticism takes over as she shakes her head and turns away from the conversation. She dismisses the subject while the others gather around to hear this guy's tale.
Last night's funeral still weighs heavily on her mind. She wonders how Eddie felt before his final moments. A shudder of fear and the feeling of dread washes over her entire body. She fights the tears away, lest anyone see her like this and start asking questions.
But Lena does genuinely feel sympathy and sadness for Mr. Rivera-Torres and his grieving family. Her lips quiver as she clicks a few icons on her screen. She takes a deep breath and starts reading, "OK...so, let's see what we have here."
When lunchtime comes around, Lena elects to go for something simple and cheap- a simple bowl of salad and a triple espresso to go with it. Not the most ideal of combos, but whatever.
The mortal lady shoves the greens into her mouth and chews slowly, occasionally tapping her phone's screen and hoping to see a reply from this Allison chick. But alas, no new messages are received.
(Because Allison is busy day-sleeping.)
Lena clicks her tongue and scrolls through the profiles of the other lucky women who're listed in the dating app. But the picture of Allison keeps popping up and stealing her attention. Lena finds something about her...alluring, but she can't quite put her finger on it.
Je ne se quois, as they usually say. But Lena would soon find out that a creature as horrid as Allison Morenstein has no appealing, let alone redeeming, qualities.
In any case, the remainder of the PI's workday goes about as quietly as can be expected. If only the freakin' pay adequately matched the effort, time, and energy put into this damn job.
Lena trills her lips five minutes before work officially concludes. She decides to make well on her promise to continue investigating the murder of Tyler by expanding her search.
Her fingers fly furiously over her keyboard as she finishes up some last-minute stuff.
Yeah, stuff.
Ms. Adams hurriedly puts some stuff away in the biometrically sealed drawers. Then she speedily-yet-efficiently gathers her personal belongings and shoves them all into her purse. With a minute-and-a-half to spare, she brushes the few errant strands of her jet-black hair to the side and clears her throat.
With bated breath, she watches the clock intently, almost obsessively so. She hears a few obnoxious and knowing "tsssk-tssksk" sounds emerging from certain people. They, too, watch the clock along with her.
Phew!
The walk to her PI office is mercifully short. The sun will soon set. She looks up at the sky while shielding her eyes from the glaring orange and yellow hues- the sunglasses help, too.
"It's a beautiful sight," Lena wistfully says to no one in particular.
The late-afternoon sun soon fades from view. Darkness bleeds into the world until only a sort of scarlet redness remains. The Kindred of Magnolia City will soon stir from their near-Torpid states and go about their vampiric Unlives.
Lena guzzles down a cup of coffee whilst going over whatever she has pertaining to her investigation. It's not much, but it's mighty better than a whole lotta nothing.
Her mentor's voice echoes in her mind and reminds her, "Don't become complacent, Lena. Even an iota of something is way better than a pound of nothing. Keep looking. Stay sharp. Always."
The PI nods and hums softly. She thinks and reasons, "One step at a time. One slow and--and steady step at a time. But first...more coffee. After that, I'll return to the scene of the crime and work my way out from--"
She clicks her tongue out of annoyance, "Oh, goddammit. There I go monologuing again. I'll expand the search net, that's that. I'm still looking..."
Lena rests her chin atop her palm and chews on her bottom lip- the lingering taste of coffee invades her mouth. A cooling breeze blows through the open window and rustles the rainbow pride flag on her desk, causing it to flap and wiggle around.
It's not long before the sun sets and the night begins anew!
Somewhere else within this Domain...
The medium-sized home on the unassuming cul-de-sac doesn't particularly stand out amidst homes that are of a similar shape and size- rather plain-looking and easy to miss, if anything.
And yet, this suburban house is...important. The streetlights flicker on and the orange glow spreads over the nearby sidewalk. A few of the sprinklers on certain lawns chug and burble to life and shoot forth a stream of water.
Bicycle wheels spin and roll along the sidewalk as a group of kids take their time riding around and enjoying the cool wind. The bells of their cycles ring and ding out melodically and loudly. They laugh and play while the crickets and cicadas chirp a symphony of sounds.
Deep in the basement of that white-colored house with tiled flooring, plastered walls, and brown wooden doors is a fully furnished bedroom. A massive, king-sized bed with black sheets and a collection of pillows lays in the corner of the room.
A large desk is adjacent to the foot of the bed. Some books and assorted papers are scattered hither and yon. The gentle hum of the air conditioner provides a constant ambience and accompaniment.
Two people lie on the bed. Their three eyes are shut, and a soft sheet covers their cold bodies. They shiver and twitch and move about.
Prince Cedric wakes up with a shiver coursing all throughout his body. His fangs extend and his pupils dilate. His claws involuntarily pop out as well. A slight growl escapes him, but then he gasps and retracts his claws and fangs.
Another...memory from...those times. Those times when cruel people would say (and do) unspeakable things to him and other individuals like him. The backbreaking hours of hard, hard labor, and the relentless oppression- the dehumanization and the inhumanity of it all.
The Salubri Prince forces some breath through lungs that have long since ceased functioning, and he attempts to calm himself down. Flashes of whips rending his flesh and woeful cries wracking his weakened frame come forth from the deepest recesses of his mind.
Scruffy white men spitting on him, kicking him, and calling him things not worth repeating. Pot-bellied, mustache-twirling masters clad in the finest of clothing carefully inspecting their human property and deciding upon an adequate price like it was the most normal, regular thing in the world.
And the loss and pain and fear that Cedric went through- not just him but the others, too. Those who attempted to escape...oh, god, the screams alone make him sit up straighter.
It wasn't just the cruelty and viciousness of this or that individual on this or that plantation- it was a whole fucking system that enabled, allowed, and perpetuated this state of affairs. And it had to be torn down by any means necessary.
And torn down it was. 'Twas very violent and vicious and messy but done it finally was.
Prince Cedric takes a look at the framed photograph of Abraham Lincoln mounted on the bedroom wall- it's in-between the portraits of Frederick Douglass and John Brown.
Then he regards the writings hanging right below each said picture. He repeats them to himself as a way of reassuring himself that that chapter of human (and world) history is now closed.
But newer horrors have risen to take the place of those from yesteryear.
While the three-eyed vampire contemplates these things, the Seneschal stirs and groans a little. When she finally awakens, the tips of her icy fingers run themselves up and down the back of her hubby-to-be.
(Any night now!)
Seneschal Rosalind blinks her three eyes and stares at the back of Prince Cedric's head. She quietly whispers his name, "Ced...bad dreams? Again?"
He gently corrects her, "Not bad dreams. Bad memories. A dream is a mishmash of shit that didn't happen- or has no chance of ever happening. But bad memories remind us of what really did happen."
A small moment of silence passes before Rosalind speaks up, "Then, I strongly feel that it's time for another session, dear. And don't groan just yet. This is important. For you, mainly. It's a part of your healing process. Marshall's coming along, too."
Cedric trills his lips, "Aight, fine. It's not like we got much to do tonight. Our city seems to be quiet. But people are scared cuz of all these murders and shit. It--it's just an abnormality, right?"
Rosalind props herself up by her elbows and winces a little, "It could be, but it could also be the precursor of something worse. Do you...wanna talk about it?"
"I think we should all keep an eye on things. And get Marshall to keep his fingers on the pulse, too. And I never got to ask, but how are your studies going, Ros?"
The Salubri Seneschal shrugs a shoulder, "Eh. It's going well enough. Were it not for the obvious, I could've been done a lot sooner. But these online courses should ideally help. Still, as far as anybody's concerned, I am still a therapist very much in training."
Cedric puts in, "And a damn good healer. A credit to the Clan as a whole."
Rosalind blushes a bit, "Aww, baby, you got all the right words to say, huh?"
He chuckles and replies, "Well, I'd sure hope so, otherwise, we wouldn't have gotten this far in...this relationship. Do you ever feel that we're dragging things on? Fiddy years is a long-ass time, no two ways about it."
Rosalind snuggles up to her man and wraps her arms around his chest, "We were forced to postpone our ceremony for several reasons, which were almost all out of our control. Even then, I want your healing to be complete before we can take this next major step in our Unlives."
Cedric strokes her fingers and huffs a small sigh, "I'm trying, Ros. I'm trying. But shit like that- the kinda shit that I went through- ain't easy to purge. There is always gon' be a lingering presence...of that...institution, y'know?"
Rosalind's expression falls, "The scars on your body are--are testament to that, sweetie. It's--it's not fair."
"It never was, Ros. That was the whole damn point of it. And even after all these years, a part of me still feels like...nothin' much has really changed. I mean, we got a black President, but I don't feel reassured. Like, at all."
His voice cracks, "Thangs didn't quite pan out the way we had initially hoped, y'know? That's America in a nutshell, right over there. Ya make one plan, have all them big and great ideals, sooner or later, y'all gon' be runnin' up against a big-ass wall labeled 'reality'. And at the speed at which y'all run, your head's gonna end up turned to a mess."
His tone grows weary, "Ya dig? Don't get me wrong, things have improved and all. Conversely, things ain't exactly lookin' up at the moment. I mean, take a look at our current President. That nigga just as bad as Bush!"
Rosalind chuckles faintly, "Mm-hmm. He was...certainly not what I was expecting, honestly. More wars, more destruction, and a staggering increase in deportations. And the ways in which they put prisoners to death with reckless abandon, it...it...boggles the mind, truly."
Cedric rapidly nods, "Damn right it does, Ros. And sometimes, us Kindred ain't exactly helpin' matters, ya feel me? While we busy goin' about our Unlives, who's to say that some other Big Bad ain't getting ready to cause some major trouble? Werewolves, Mages, Wraiths, and shit."
"It's a whole world out there, baby. A world of darkness."
"And secrets. And a whole lotta other crap, too."
Rosalind mutters, "A Beast I am, lest a Beast I become."
Cedric barely chuckles, "The Riddle of our very existence, huh? Well, we better get cleaned up for the night ahead, hmm?"
Ros lets go of him and nods plainly, "Mm-hmm. You go first, I need to brush up on my...notes and stuff."
The Prince grabs a stack of papers off the desk and places them into a nearby folder. He strokes Rosalind's cheeks affectionately, stares into her three eyes with his own three eyes, and smiles.
He tosses a flirty wink towards her and gives her ass a slight squeeze. She titters and returns the gesture with greater fervor, even going so far as to flirtatiously growl.
A wee while later...
The two Peacemakers are showered, cleaned up, and ready for the next activity. They enter the spacious living room, and check that the windows are locked. The thick beige curtains are left closed and the lights are dimmed.
Cedric is clad in a dark-blue sweater and black pants. The stiff white collar of his shirt rises a little. His dreadlocks freely flow down.
Rosalind has opted to wear something a bit more comfortable and casual- a purple cardigan and capri pants- it's giving welcoming therapist vibes. Her hair smells of rosy shampoo.
Ced has seen his beloved with and without clothes on. He wolf-whistles at her, and she coyly pretends not to notice.
"Damn, gurl, you fine. I got an overdue book...and my library card could use a swipe...or two, ya feel me?"
"Oh, I feel you, baby. Mmm, I very much feel you. Now, come on and sit here."
She taps the floor and assumes a cross-legged position herself. Cedric lowers himself down and mimics her pose- his knees and back creak a little. They are now seated opposite one another.
Their hands touch. Their fingers interlock.
Cedric gulps and shakes his head a little bit, "You know I kinda hate this part, right?"
Rosalind reminds him, "It's necessary. We have to. It's all a part of your healing process. So, let's start from the top, hmm? Back to where, when, and how it all began. I know that we've been so many times before, but maybe we might discover something new or different? You never know, Ced."
The Prince mumbles, "That's what I'm worried about. OK, here goes nothing. I was born...a slave. Didn't know who my momma or daddy was. The masters probably knew, but they didn't tell me shit about 'em. Siblings? I dunno, I honestly do not know. I just got--I was alone, really."
The eyes of the Seneschal flash as she begins peering deeper into the very essence (soul, maybe) of her dearly beloved. Ced's memories become hers. And small parts of hers, his.
The plantation had thousands upon thousands of slaves working long hours under the harsh, unforgiving sunlight. Whips were cracked, racial slurs of the foulest and most dehumanizing variety were uttered gleefully, and bodies were severely broken for not meeting quotas.
The two-eyed (and very much mortal) Cedric Montgomery would work till his body could take no more. Even then, he would be beaten and forced to endure beyond the breaking point's breaking point.
Ros urges, "Steady, steady. If these parts of your past are--are too much to dwell upon, let's move things forward a bit. Does that sound like an agreeable arrangement, my love?"
Prince Cedric manages a nod, and Rosalind starts guiding him through the rest of the...process.
Tears of blood stream from her eyes. Even her topmost eye bleeds. She feels his pain, experiences his grief, and understands his anger on a visceral level.
That would be the Affective Empathy of the Salubri Clan in action. It's their Compulsion. In this case, Rosalind becomes overwhelmed with empathy for a personal problem that afflicts someone- her boyfriend. She seeks to resolve it regardless of the cost to herself.
In their case, the painful memories of yesteryears haunt both Ced and Ros alike.
Both of these vampires understand that sometimes suffering is part of a cumulative situation and not an isolated stimulus. As such, they are compelled to ease their burdens and mitigate these tragedies as much as they possibly can.
Sometimes, their duty and their desire align seamlessly and perfectly. Other times, it takes a greater deal of effort from one or all parties.
Cedric gulps and sniffles hard. The hairs on his body stand up. He unconsciously clutches Rosalind's hands tighter, eliciting a slight gasp from the Seneschal.
She winces slightly and keeps her focus on him. On all of him. The man, the human being, the freed slave, and the victim of a cruel and unjust system that has been abolished.
Rosalind politely guides him, "Let's keep moving. Do you...want to maybe stay a while or shall we--"
Cedric's lower lip quivers, "I--I wanna go forward. I wanna see...his face. Even if it's just another glimpse."
In time, Cedric would go on to be rescued by a white abolitionist of the Salubri Clan. Three very intense blue eyes looked down on a broken and shaken black man who was clad in a burlap tunic and filthy hemp pants.
The then-mortal Cedric was aghast at the sight in front of him. Skin as pale as snow, fangs bared and gleaming with blood, and despite all of that, kindness and the best of intentions.
The abolitionist would go on to Embrace Cedric into the Clan. Over time, Cedric would learn that his Sire relentlessly killed slave owners, slave catchers, and their families without mercy.
On a warm evening, the two-eyed (and deeply bruised) Cedric was given a machete- as were many of his fellow slaves. They rose up and did what had to be done. The only way to answer those who profit and trade in human flesh.
While the man with three blue eyes calmly watched, the slaves cried out and furiously charged. With machetes in hand and determination burning deep in their souls, they cut down ALL of their oppressors (and their families) and brought a long-overdue reckoning.
They burned down the whole fucking plantation after killing literally everybody. Cedric watched the flames dance. And his would-be Sire smiled. From that night on, Cedric had a newfound purpose.
But when the fangs sank into his flesh and third eye popped out of his forehead, things really did change and didn't exactly pan out the way he imagined them.
His Sire- whose name he never learnt- disappeared so suddenly. Many of his fellow Salubri started to scatter and hide away within the Confederate South, with many foes hot on their tails.
Pious mortal fanatics and their unquenchable thirst for ending the supernatural and the like. Cedric would have to learn how to survive, adapt, and overcome the various trials and tribulations that life- Unlife- had in store for folks like him.
As the years rolled on, he would live, struggle, fight, and make connections and alliances with other members of the Camarilla. Alliances with other Sects didn't pan out quite well. Reasons.
He bore witness to the horrors of the Jim Crow South as well. His nature as a vampire (and the obvious third eye on his forehead) meant that he couldn't directly intervene, especially during those times.
Cedric admits, "I--I lacked the courage of my Sire. I just...wasn't him. I couldn't be him."
But he tried helping and aiding wherever and whenever he could. He briefly ran with the Black Panthers but found certain aspects of their program quite limiting and restrictive. He even flirted with Black Nationalism but wasn't all that enamored.
(After all, it was a so-called "White Devil" who saved his life and gave him the gift of Unlife).
And the remainder of his memories fade into a blur.
When he first laid eyes on Rosalind, something inside of him...clicked. He saw the same kind, friendly expression on her face that he did on his Sire's all those decades prior. He felt her radiating genuine warmth, acceptance, and loving kindness, and he was won over.
Rosalind wistfully smiles as even she re-experiences these moments. Tears of blood stream down from the corners of her eyes. She gulps, gathers her composure, and focuses her energies- the sympathy and empathy are strong and unrelenting.
Cedric recalls a moment when one of the other slaves that his Sire had freed had cried out, "Three cheers, three eyes!" while holding a bloodied machete high up in the air. Fire raged all around them.
Everyone else would celebrate the destruction of this particular plantation...and the oppressive, cruel people who owned and ran it with nothing short of an iron fist.
Apart from the time of his freedom (and Embrace), Cedric cherishes the night that he first met Rosalind Meyer. And he shall indubitably remember his wedding (whenever that is) for the rest of his existence.
Rosalind strokes his cheek and slowly asks, "Do you...regret the violence that you and the other ex-slaves inflicted on the night that your Sire raided that plantation and freed you all?"
Prince Cedric calms his erratic heartbeat and thinks hard, "At the time, we were yearning for nothin' but vengeance. We didn't care about no decorum or takin' no prisoners. He gave us all machetes and told us that our freedom awaited us- all we had to do was go and get it. Fight for it. Kill for it."
The Salubri Prince blows a raspberry, "I don't regret doin' what I did that night and I sure as hell ain't gon' waste time condemning my brothers and sisters who wasted all those motherfuckers and did to them only a fraction of what they did to us for all those years, y'know?"
The Salubri Seneschal plainly replies, "I understand. And you're right, honey. It's a fool's errand to condemn the actions that the slaves took against their masters. I know how favorably you speak of people like Nat Turner and others."
"Only a fool would condemn what we did to all those white people that night. It wasn't pretty, but it was necessary. It was the only path to our freedom- the only path that us...broken Negroes had left. We didn't have the luxury of fuckin' options like voting and protesting and shit. All we had was a machete, a dream, a yearning for freedom, and the will to get shit done...for real."
Rosalind strokes his head and draws closer, "You don't need to be defensive. I was merely making an observation. For what it's worth, I will never condemn what you and the other enslaved individuals did that night. As...uncomfortable as certain parts make me feel, I...understand the reasoning behind all of it."
"Mm-hmm. It is what it is, Ros."
"Yep. It sure is."
