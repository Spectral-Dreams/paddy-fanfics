Act 4: Baali
"The Baali aren't a Clan, per se. But they're numerous enough to count as such (among other reasons). Anyways, their Bloodline is comprised of alleged...devil worshippers (or demon worshippers). I barely attend church these nights (can't even recall the last time I went). But none of the few Baali I've personally come across have erupted into flames. Perhaps there's another reason for their...infamy amongst our kind." -From the personal diaries of Gregory | Entry: On The Baali.
The sun is hidden behind clouds this morning, giving it a rather dim appearance. A few of its rays, however, manage to find their way through the clouds and gently caress the ground below.
Cassandra's alarm clock goes off, but she smacks it off the bedside table and lets out a long, long groan. It clatters on the floor and keeps on making an obnoxious ringing noise. She rolls over and covers her head with a pillow.
After Gregory spent most of last night giving her a long explanation on who this particular Baali was and why he owes the Toreador, she felt a headache coming on. And Greg really was vague about a great many details. He's always doing that- being vague and such.
The Ghoul, for her part, was simply too tired to press her Domitor for answers or clarifications. He simply said that more will be made known when the time's right. And so, she simply shrugged, gave him a thumbs-up, and trudged to her bed.
She was out within minutes. Her loud and grating snoring now fills the air as the alarm clock continues its incessant ringing. Cass wishes to sleep in for a bit, but she also has a nagging urge to wake up and do something.
She's a Ghoul, yes, and is beholden to the will of her Domitor.
Nevertheless, she snorts, wiggles on her bed, and rolls onto her stomach. The pillow does its best to muffle her loud snoring. The drool keeps on flowing, and her mouth hangs open. Cassandra's eyes are closed, and her brown hair is a bit disheveled.
She dreams of a great many things, which is the norm for her. It's the same old routine that has become somewhat comforting and familiar. Some are recollections from her past whilst others are just plain fanciful fluff.
Cassandra dreams of taking a selfie with her favorite singer- Soho Starlet, Nicola Green. Both are dressed in stylishly trendy clothes, and the latter has her arms wrapped around the former's shoulder. Cass is all grins and she squeals after taking the selfie.
In a separate dream, Cassandra is back in her comprehensive school days. She vividly recalls how her working-class voice and language of home were relentlessly belittled by the middle-class educators.
They did everything to get her to speak more "proper" but, despite their best efforts, her accent remains defiantly Northern. She has long since learned how to not let such snooty, elitist, posh wankers get under her skin.
Cass's dreams go hither and yon. She feels herself in a body that is both familiar and isn't quite hers at the same time. It is not her flesh and bones, yet she can control this other being's movements.
The sun is blazing, its piercing rays coming through the blinds. She stands in front of a small mirror and inspects her face. It's her face, alright. Hazel eyes, brunette hair, whatever else. She notices a tiny beauty spot near the corner of her eye. She rubs her finger over it and blinks a few times.
From whatever (and wherever) that was, she is yanked into a different dream. The landscape is completely dark, and a great number of strange, colorful, and glowing shapes are moving around.
By the time the Toreador Ghoul wakes up, it's nearly midday. She groggily opens her eyes, rolls over, and yawns loudly.
She stretches herself out, feeling some popping and cracking, and sits up in bed. She blinks a few times, rubs the crusty bits out of her eyes, and groans, "Cor blimey...what's--oh, that's the time. Hrrrrfhgh. Ah, well..." She trails off and readies herself for a bath.
While the water in the tub is filling up, she brushes her teeth and checks her phone. Just a few cheeky texts from Amy, but that's just about it. Cass replies with a stream of heart emojis and puts her Blackberry aside.
Since she's already naked, she steps into the bathtub and settles down in the warm, sudsy water. A sigh of contentment escapes her lips as she gently leans her head back.
Cass sings out a few lines (off-key) from some of her favorite Nicola Green songs. She's a bit rusty and can't recall the exact words at the moment, but that's no matter.
"LaLaLaLaLa, love is in the air...sweeping me through my hair. OooOoOh, I'll always love ya, babe...oh, baby..."
Once done with the bath, she steps out of the tub and wraps herself in a towel. She glances at the mirror and runs a hand through her hair, "Ah, yeah...that was proper refreshing!"
Tonight is when the real work begins. So, Cassandra throws on a simple Adidas tracksuit that she got back in the year 2000 for only a few quid; she's done her best to keep it pristine. White stripes run down the sides of the blue tracksuit.
She slips into a pair of pink trainers, ties the laces, and puffs her cheeks. After grabbing her cell phone, she leaves her cottage and makes her way outside. A few orange leaves have already fallen, and they crunch under her soles.
Cassandra inhales a lungful of the crisp autumn air and lets it all out. She heads towards a small bench a few paces away from the cottage and plops down.
The Ghoul fiddles around with her phone while deciding whether to take breakfast, wait till brunchtime, or just skip the meal entirely. She goes for a short walk, pops by the local pound shop, but doesn't end up buying anything.
The rest of her afternoon is spent lazing around in her cottage, reading a book. This is quite a heavy read: "The God Delusion" by Richard Dawkins.
Cassandra identifies as an atheist, but she is NOT an obnoxious "Reddit Atheist" who trashes religion (and people of faith) just for some superior gotcha points and hollow ego-stroking. To her credit, she doesn't even own a fedora!
She has a modicum of respect for the man and even looks up to him a bit. Keep in mind that this was long before Richard expressed all sortsa nasty, yikes views like harboring eugenicist views, supporting a genocide, being a massive transphobic twat, or most pathetically...going all goo-goo-gaga over his AI girlfriend.
Anyways, Cassandra isn't necessarily the type of lass to blame all of the world's ills on religion (organized or otherwise). She recognizes that the current socio-economic system is rotting on its feet and dragging not just the UK but the entirety of the world down into the abyss.
She can't help but grimace whenever some of her fellow atheists (online or offline) point their fingers at religion, especially when it comes to matters of the Middle East. They completely ignore imperialism, colonialism, capitalism, and a myriad of other factors that are directly responsible for the current (miserable) state of the region.
Cass has gotten into a few arguments with them- mainly on Facebook- and it always devolves into an angry back-and-forth before 1 of them ends up blocking the other.
Cassandra sets the book aside and yawns. She picks up some fiction, turns to a random page, and starts reading. Ever since she was a wean, she's always been a curious and eager reader- everything from her father's leftie newspapers to books not quite meant for her age to random Internet articles.
When Wikipedia first became a thing, she spent a whole week proper going through and absorbing every last word that the then-fledgling site had to offer.
In time, Cassandra elects to 'ave a quick nap. Sunrise isn't that far off. She tosses and turns a bit on her bed, trying her damnedest to get comfy. A light rain patters on her window, and she soon drifts off into a peaceful snooze.
Cars go to and fro. The streets of this Domain are slick with rainwater and puddles. It's a gray, drizzly day- typical British default weather. And the gloominess that hangs over the city does a good job at reflecting the overall mood.
A slight mist pervades the area, obscuring some of the buildings. This city is about to undergo...a shaking up in the next few days (and nights).
For now, it remains decidedly unstirred.
The sun begins to make its retreat from the horizon, and the shadows lengthen. The Kindred in this part of the globe begin to arise. Their senses sharpen, and the Beast within them growls for a liquid red treat.
Gregory's eyes pop open, but his body remains frozen. His breath hitches, and his eyes dart back and forth. With great effort, he turns his head and sees...it.
The Aapilu just stands there, menacingly cloaked in the darkness. The creature of the Blood twirls its 6 fingers and glares right at Gregory. Its gray and slimy skin appears to be slick and moist.

He desperately tries to free himself from this paralysis, but the Aapilu's very presence has rendered him entirely immobile. Gregory grimaces and tries to make a sound, but no sound escapes his dry lips.
The Aapilu cocks its head to the side and makes a clicking sound. Gregory can do nothing as he watches the creature stand in place and taunt him. The Beast within him is restless, but its roar is suppressed by the Aapilu's powerful influence.
Something resembling a tail abruptly flicks and smacks the wall behind it. Gregory tries his damnedest to look away, but the Aapilu's gaze is locked with his. His Blood runs cold and his body trembles.
Gregory squints and tries to get a better look at what the creature is doing, but it's quite hard for him to tell. Even blinking his eyes is proving to be Herculean task.
Another clicking noise comes from the Aapilu. Gregory's dead heart thumps wildly as his fear grows. The Aapilu doesn't move an inch. It crouches down and growls softly. Its 6 fingers twitch and clench into fists.
Gregory gulps and tries once more to move, but his efforts are met with no success. And just as suddenly as it had appeared, the Aapilu vanishes into thin air. Poof!
The body of Cassandra's Domitor becomes limp. Gregory lets out a painful wheeze and pulls himself out of bed. His face is a bit pale, and his forehead is covered in a layer of cold and somewhat bloody sweat.
He forcefully exhales and hobbles over to the bathroom. He switches the light on and winces when the bright and fluorescent light hits his face. Gregory turns the sink faucet on and splashes cold water on his face. He stares at his dull and baggy eyes in the mirror.
With his face still dripping wet, he dabs a fresh towel over it. The Toreador sighs, and the first thing that pops to his deeply troubled mind is how Cass is gonna deal with the Baali tonight.
This guy owes Greg a huge favor- it has something to do with the Aapilu that had just visited him. This particular Baali claims to have an understanding of these ancient beings and even offered to help "banish" the being who is currently tormenting Gregory.
The Toreador's expression is grim. He only hopes that the Demon Worshipper is true to his word and follows through on his side of the deal. Otherwise, it's going to be a massive problem.
Gregory walks back to his bed, plops down on its edge, and forces a deep, deep breath. He clasps his cold hands together, closes his eyes, and feebly mumbles.
Gregory is a sort of backslidden Anglican. He's been a C of E (Church of England) lad ever since birth. That being said, he does attend church whenever he feels like it- usually for Christmas or Easter services.
He sits in the back, stands when told, sings from the hymnbook, kneels on the cushions when told, folds his arms when the sermon begins, and generally goes through the motions. He even takes communion but eventually ends up spewing it out as scarlet vomit.
(Because if it ain't actual blood, the Beast declines it in the sharpest way possible.)

Anyways, he doesn't discuss religion that much and kind of understands why his Ghoul isn't a believer.
He opens his eyes and rubs his palms over his face. His shower is fairly brief and mundane. The hot water flows down his naked body and soothes his taut muscles.
His stomach growls. His eternal Hunger is making its demands known. And, judging by the sounds outside, there are a few mortals out and about.
Like always, Gregory is sending his Ghoul on his behalf. So, he slips into a plain pair of trousers, puts a nice polo shirt on, combs his hair a little, applies some body lotion, and steps out of his bedroom.
He heads downstairs and takes a seat at the couch. He looks around his sleek and modern Haven, appreciating the interiors and the view outside.
It's shaping up to be a pretty decent evening, all things considered. The issue of the Aapilu still lingers on his mind, but Greg is hopeful that the Baali will be able to aid him in getting rid of this supernatural pest.

Promote him on your social media...pages or however you call them. Just make sure that he keeps his word to me. This--this is important to me. I need his help."
