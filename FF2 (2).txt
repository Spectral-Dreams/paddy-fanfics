Act 2: Brujah
"The Brujah Clan are many things. Passionate (but not like us Toreador), angry, and loud. Sometimes that rage tends to boil over and lead into rather nasty and unpleasant conflicts. Their temperaments are quite volatile, and it's a rare moment when a Brujah isn't arguing or debating with someone. Nevertheless, I find them rather tolerable. Some Brujah are very well-read and knowledgeable, and that is to their credit." -From the personal diaries of Gregory | Entry: On The Brujah.
The sun rises in this part of the world. Unlike her immortal master, Cassandra can't really spend the whole bloody day in bed. While still in the nude, she pushes open a window and sighs contentedly as a gentle breeze flows into her room.
She looks at the nature surrounding her cozy little cottage and smiles widely. Despite the gray gloominess, there are some warm colors thrown about. The nearby flowers are a pretty shade of pink.
The air is crisp and clean. There are birds singing and chirping in the background. Cassandra extends her hand and the rays of the sun touch her skin. She grins, her cheeks warming up.
This is nice, she thinks to herself. At least she's able to experience the waking world, unlike the one whose Blood she is reliant on for the lite version of immortality.
She turns away from the window and readies herself for a bath. She walks to the bathroom and brushes her teeth while badly singing some or the other song. She spits out the Colgate toothpaste into the sink and wipes her mouth.
She then plugs the tub and lets the water run. It's on the warmer side, just the way she likes it. The Ghoul settles into the water with a small titter. Cass spends an awful lot of time in there, letting the warm, bubbly water soak her entire body.
She then grabs her cellphone and shoots Amy a few goofy texts, throwing in a few random emojis to boot. Cassandra giggles softly and sets her phone aside, a small smile lingering on her lips.
Cassandra went absolutely crazy when emojis first became a thing. She practically spammed them everywhere and used them all the time.
Amy's response is a series of suggestive texts and a few question marks. She still doesn't get the thought process behind the Ghoul's steady stream of random emojis.
But it's all in good fun, yeah.
After washing her hair, scrubbing her body, and soaking herself, she gets dressed and goes about her day. She idly lazes around, does a wee bit of cleaning and rearranging, and then pops out to the nearby pound shop.
This time, Cassandra doesn't take the Lexus. She decides to go by foot. She has her phone in hand. She sees an older lady walking a really pampered-looking Pomeranian. The dog looks at Cass and barks loudly.
Cassandra uneasily winces and looks down at the pavement. She quickens her pace, eager to get as far away from the barking dog as possible- she's not much of a dog or cat person. But she does have a soft spot for capybaras, despite never having physically seen any in all her life.
She reckons, "Maybe I should go to the city's zoo and check if there are any, yeah?"
As she makes her way, she notices that her cellphone is vibrating. It's Amy.
Cass smirks and brings the phone up to her ear, "Couldn't keep away, eh?"
"You know me all too well, Cassie."
"Oh, do I, now? You're in a cheeky mood today, ain't you? Heading to work?"
Amy answers, "Mm-hmm. School admin is hardly exciting, but it keeps the lights and heating on."
Cassandra inquires, "Did they fix the cracks on the walls yet? You told me about it a few weeks ago, remember?"
Amy scoffs, "Bloody bastards put it off till the next election cycle. Tossers, tch. The cracks have actually gotten bigger, if you can believe it. 1 of the kids got scared that a wall was going to come crashing down on them! I share the same apprehensions, too."
Cassandra gasps, "Cor blimey! That's scary! How's everybody else handling this mess?"
"Petitions, Facebook posts, talking to bloggers who are barely read by anyone, the usual. We're not using our own funds to patch the bloody cracks because we're already running on a bloody deficit! This isn't right."
Cassandra asks, "Is there anything that I can do from me end? Like, reach out to our local MPs--"
"Fuck that. We've tried that already. I voted for the bugger. And she even dropped around for her usual photo-ops and shit. But when push comes to shove, that lot forget all about their own bloody constituents and go gallivanting off with the party bigwigs."
Cassandra sighs, "Well, I'll see what I can do, yeah? Have you tried Twitter? You'd be surprised how--"
Amy clicks her tongue in mild annoyance, "We've exhausted every available option, sweetheart. We even 'ave a WhatsApp group. Or HAD a WhatsApp group, more like. It devolved into a petty shouting match. Sort of like a catfight. I still have the screenshots saved; some of it is a right laugh!"
Cassandra crosses the road and nods, "Yeah. Some people can be massive twats, eh? Not all people, but some people. And those are unfortunately the ones who usually have the pull, power, and influence over the rest of us."
Amy chuckles, "Speaking from experience, are you? You're not even 30, babe. Why do you sound a lot older than you look? But you do look awfully sexy."
Cassandra laughs softly, "Oh, please! I--I--it's complicated, right?"
"We're complicated. But that's what makes us so exciting and fun, yeah?"
Cassandra stops and waves at the mailman, "I guess you're right. Anyways, I'm going to the pound shop, right? You...err...you want me to get you something?"
Amy replies, "Oh, no, no, no. Please. I'm fine. Really, I'm fine. Have yourself a lovely day. Mwah! Cheers!"
Cassandra makes a kissy sound back, "Mwah! Byee! Talk later, yeah? Send me a text, preferably something spicy. You know how...hot I like it."
"Will do, dear. Will do. Ciao!" Amy hangs up.
The Ghoul smiles and enters the pound shop, her Blackberry cellphone clutched tightly in her left hand. The establishment in question is fairly modern-looking. The moldy patches on the walls, however, are not easy to look away from.
Cassandra walks down the aisles with an easygoing smile on her face. She stops at the section that is filled with all sorts of cloths. She needs a few wiping cloths for keeping her cottage clean. The ones that she currently has were brought over from London and are quite worn-out.
She tosses a few packs of various colors and continues with her shopping. Cass drags her wheelie-trolley and walks to the next aisle. The employees seem to be a lot older than your average retail worker.
She swears that she once overheard an employee talk about how their pension was insufficient for their daily living expenses. Hence their need to seek this job and work extra hard. Cassandra still shudders whenever that conversation replays in her mind.
The Ghoul still remembers that man's harsh Cockney accent and the way his eyes drooped as he spoke. She doesn't see much of him around anymore, and she's been a bit shy to go around asking about him.
Cassandra grabs a few hand-wiping cloths while she's at it. These feel soft to the touch and are only £1.50!
She hoists her wheelie-trolley onto the conveyor belt and stands in line. She glances at the various newspapers displayed on the stand beside her. She snags a copy of the Guardian while cringing at the copies of the Sun and the Daily Mail. She also nabs another copy for her Domitor.
Cassandra pays with cash, says a sincere thank-you to the freckled-face lad working the till, and exits the establishment with her purchases in a plastic bag. She takes a deep breath of fresh air and walks on her merry way.
Sometime before actual lunchtime, her stomach growls a bit. She stops and checks out her phone. There are a few texts from Amy.
Cassandra bites her lower lip and reads them. She sits on a wooden bench by the nearby park. There are a few children running around, laughing loudly.
Cassandra's fingers mash into the keys of her Blackberry cellphone and she texts Amy back. She can't help but grin at every message.
She eventually goes around to asking: "What should I get 4 lunch?"
Amy texts back: "Scampi fries, I reckon? And no, I'm not free to meet up right now. Work. And allaying fears about the cracking walls of this school."
Cassandra pouts and replies: "Maybe later this week?"
Amy huffs a sigh: "Cass, love, you can get a bit clingy sometimes..."
Cassandra's brows knit together and her expression falls a little: "You say that like it's a bad thing, Amy. We're...err...you know..."
Amy rolls her eyes: "Yes. Now, I have a call coming in. We'll meet when we meet. That's that. I don't want anyone getting suspicious. The only way this works is if we be discreet about the whole thing. Getting caught is not in either of our best interests."
Cassandra sighs and types: "I'm sorry. I just...like you so much, yeah? I enjoy being with you and all. Me making you laugh is always brings a smile to me own face, innit?"
Amy smirks: "Oh, you're sweet, Cassie. I have GOT to go now. Bye. XOXO!"
Cassandra responds with a kiss mark emoji. After that, she grabs her shopping and heads off to find some scampi fries for today's lunch. She's rather peckish, and she hopes that scampi fries will be able to satisfy that hunger.
The rest of her afternoon goes by. A light rain begins to pelt the city once the sun begins its rather unceremonious departure from the sky. Cassandra spends a few hours binging some whatever's on the telly while texting back and forth with Amy.
Amy's replies are sporadic and somewhat dry, while Cassandra's texts are a lot more enthusiastic and frequent. Cassandra has no idea as to what the telly is even showing, but the background noise is nice.
She lays her head down for a quick nap. Within seconds, she's out. After about a few minutes, her eyes pop open and she lets out a grating yawn. Her hands instinctively reach for her cellphone.
Cassandra rubs her eyes and sits up. She opens up the message app and sends Amy a few more texts. No reply is forthcoming, however. Cass frowns and decides to give it some time.
For now, she has to get ready to carry out the will of her Domitor. She freshens up in the bathroom and changes her clothing. Since Gregory is still rather vague about a great many things, she decides to wear something rather bland.
A nice off-white blouse, wide-legged pants, and very light makeup consisting of lip gloss (from the pound shop), eyeliner, and a touch of blush. Her long brown hair is tied into a bun...of sorts.
She smacks her lips a few times, chuckles at her reflection, and grabs her handbag. She heads out into the rainy night with a large umbrella and makes her way over to Gregory's Haven.
The Toreador has already woken from his day-sleep. He'll stalk the shadows for his prey a bit later. For now, his inner Beast is as quiet as a church mouse. Gregory stands in front of a vanity mirror and examines himself.
He looks dull, tired, and older than he actually is. His skin is paler than usual, and there are some deep lines on his face. He shakes his head and groans as he walks away from the mirror. He glances at the bed and sees how bloodied the sheets and pillows are.
"I'll change it myself. I don't want Cassandra seeing this and bombarding me with questions. Questions for which I simply lack a cogent answer to."
Gregory lets his Ghoul in and wordlessly ushers her to the living room.
Cassandra greets him earnestly, "Hiya, Greg! Sleep well?"
He dryly replies, "Yes."
Cassandra regards him with a concerned look, "Not gonna ask how me day was? You...usually do that."
Gregory stifles a sigh, "Was it pleasant?"
Cassandra smiles widely and declares, "I had scampi fries! I also popped over to the nearby pound shop. That mold's getting worse, yeah? They ought to fix that up."
Gregory blinks, "Hm. Sit. There's much that needs to be discussed before I can officially send you off."
Cassandra sits down on the plush sofa and places her handbag next on the center table. A tight smile is plastered on her face, and she keeps glancing at Gregory out of the corner of her eyes.
He's in the kitchen, milling about and checking the state of the fridge. He mainly keeps it stocked in case a mortal guest shows up and is in need of some hospitality. The Toreador, however, doesn't keep bags of blood in there. He's not a bagger. He hunts for his meals.
Gregory walks into the living room with a bowl of crisps. He puts it on the center table and plainly gestures towards it, "If you'd like."
Cass grabs a single piece, thanks her Domitor for his consideration, and bites down. Gregory watches her chew with a rather unreadable expression on his face.
The crunching sounds fill the living room as Cassandra continues to chow down on the crisps.
Gregory begins, "I owe the Brujah a favor. I believe that now is the time to make good on that promise."
Cassandra finishes chewing, "Oh. What kind of favor?"
"It was open-ended. If they ever needed me, they were free to call upon me and I'd come running. Now, I'm authorizing you to do that running on my behalf. Do whatever they tell you without fail, Cassandra. It's in both of our best interests to remain on their good side."
Cassandra's shaky hand reaches out for another crisp, "What if they ask me to...do something messed up? Or something downright illegal? I don't wanna go to jail, Greg! Or get me head chopped off because I inadvertently breached the Masquerade or something!"
Gregory sighs, "I'll take...some responsibility for your actions. It's only fair, I suppose. That being said, don't make a catastrophic mess out of things. You're not a child, Cassandra."
"Condescending much? I know how old I am...and how old I really am. Blimey. Sorry. So, I represent you, eh? It won't be anything too outlandish, would it?"
"I haven't a clue what the Brujah have planned. I'm not going to lie to you. Be prepared for anything that they might throw your way."
"Who exactly do you owe this favor to, anyway? I've never heard about any of this before this evening! Why am I hearing about all of this now? Why are you keeping stuff from me, Greg?"
Gregory shrugs, "Because I didn't feel that it was relevant in the performing of your duties as my Ghoul. There are a good many reasons why I have kept you in the dark in regard to certain matters pertaining to Kindred Society and its broader happenings. But that ends tonight."
He emphasizes, "You won't just be representing me; you'll be representing me AND our Clan while dealing with the Brujah. Be on your best behavior, Cassandra. No quips or witty retorts. You have to be...a lot more serious than usual."
Cassandra fumbles with her crisp, "I--I can be serious. When I want to, yeah. I'm serious and plenty responsible! Got employee of the month at work, y'know?"
"Yes. In regard to your work, your vacation officially starts now. I've already taken the liberty of speaking with your superiors. We have to make the most out of these next few nights. It's quite possible that we won't be seeing each other for a good while."
"How far are you sending me, anyway?"
The Toreador Domitor flatly answers, "It depends. Ensure that the Lexus has enough petrol in it."
Cassandra licks her lips and asks, "Will you be sending me back to London?"
"No. Not with those blasted Garou running all over the place- the risk is far too great. Everyone that I know from London has either met their Final Death, moved out- like us, or have fled."
Cassandra shakily laughs, "We--we got out of London just in time, yeah?"
"Yes."
The Garou Nation (werewolves) were responsible for bringing about what is now dubbed the "Fall of London". They came in and viciously purged that entire Domain of its deeply entrenched bloodsucker population.
Greg and Cass moved out of the city long before the attacks began.
The Garou waged their war on multiple fronts (dimensions). They moved from the Umbra to the physical world and back again. They used any means necessary to get the job done. By the end of it, the ashes of many Kindred were a testament to the savage might and power of Gaia's mightiest warriors.
And now, London is firmly Garou country. But why?
Because the werewolves are preparing for a war against the water companies that have been polluting Gaia's oceans and seas (all those nasty sewerage discharges and spills in the UK, for instance). That's their primary concern.
The wolves are readying themselves, training vigorously, bringing new cubs into their fold, and getting stronger by the day. In another decade, they hope to bring the fight to the Bane Spirit of the Filthy Iguana (which has its talons deep in the water bosses and corporations).
Driving out the Kindred was a necessary component of the Garou's plans. They needed a staging ground from which to launch their attacks on the privatized water companies.
Bringing about the Fall of London was absolutely necessary to this end. The Garou couldn't possibly launch their campaign from the heart of Kindred territory.
The attacks were savage and caught the entirety of the Camarilla elite by surprise. By the time it was over, the ashes of many supposedly untouchable Cainites were all that was left of the legacies that they had spent centuries cultivating.
Even the Ghouls were not spared.
For instance, Gregory's own Sire was torn apart by a large pack of werewolves while attempting to flee from them. Even till now, many of London's survivors (Ghouls, Kindred, or otherwise) haven't fully recovered from the traumas and horrors visited upon them by the Garou Nation.
(As an aside, there are many Fomori compromising and tainting the UK's Environment Agency from within, so the warriors of Gaia have their work cut out for them.)
Anyways...
Gregory fixes his Ghoul with a significant look, "I intend to get even with the Brujah before the next sunrise. Whatever they ask of you- no matter how trivial it may seem- do it to the best of your abilities. Yes?"
Cassandra's expression gets a lot more serious. She nods, "Fine. Yeah. Where am I supposed to be, anyway?"
"The Horn 'N' Stag pub. It's quite a way off from where we are, so you best take the Lexus if you want to reach there quicker."
She asks, "What do I tell them if they ask me who I am and where you are?"
Gregory answers, "Tell them what I told you: you are a Ghoul who is authorized- fully- to act on behalf of her Domitor. There will be no need for them to come around and seek me out. And I would prefer it if I weren't dragged into any further messes."
Cassandra slowly exhales, "Wow. OK. It's not gonna be too hard of a task, right?"
"I don't know what they'll throw at you. Just...try to keep a low profile. Don't call me unless it's an emergency. We don't want to draw any unneeded attention to ourselves. Especially with how...compromised phones may be."
"Tch, yeah. Don't remind me. We have to change ours soon, yeah? We're not taking any chances! We can't, right?"
"Hmm. Tell them that I sent you. The Brujah will know me. They all know who I am. I owe an Elder named Winston. If Winston isn't there, find a man named Harry- he'll point you in the right direction. Winston usually hangs around the pub; it's where we conducted most of our business dealings and negotiations."
"Oh. Nice! Sure, yeah. Anything else before I go off?"
Gregory grimly tells her, "Be very cautious, Cassandra. Be vigilant at all times. If you feel that something is amiss or that someone is watching you, then do your best to get out of the situation. Do not be afraid to defend yourself if need be."
Cassandra's expression falters, "Cor blimey. Defend meself against...?" She lets the question linger in the air.
Gregory shrugs, "If that's not an option, then run as fast as you can. Do whatever you can to slow down your pursuer...or pursuers. We've already established that you are not a warrior. And I myself have no issue with admitting that I'm not exactly cut out for combat either. But please be careful out there."
Cassandra chuckles uneasily, "You know how much I love me life, Greg! I love living in this world! You know how much I--I love being a Ghoul, forever me age and all that other stuff! I--I'll do my best, pro--promise."
He nods, "Yes. So please, please be very careful. Only reach out to me if absolutely necessary. I've told you all that you need to know...for now. If anyone raises a fuss with the arrangement, feel free to take on a more assertive tone. Be polite, but don't let them push you around. Then again, with Brujah, you're going to want to have a bit of thick skin."
Cassandra bites her lip, "Oh. Uh...sure, yeah, I can do that. I think."
Gregory notes, "You know quite a lot about the Brujah Clan. Use that to your advantage. As a matter of fact, utilize everything that you know to the best of your ability. The more information that you have in regard to any given subject, the easier it is for you to formulate a plan. And you possess a great deal of information."
Cassandra laughs softly and taps her head, "It's all in here, innit?"
"Hmm. I hope that those lessons were well worth your time and effort. You know things about Kindred Society that even I do not. It's undoubtedly a lot of information. But you've done well in retaining most of it, yes?"
Cassandra musters up a smile, "Mm-hmm. Are we sure that the Brujah aren't planning to pull some kind of nasty trick on us?"
"The Brujah are many things. But they aren't crass tricksters. Well, not this lot anyway. They may not be as well-liked as the Gangrel who mill about near the city's zoo, but they are not deceitful. They can, however, be given to their anger. You must be careful about how you approach them. And watch your words around some of them. You wouldn't want to set them off because of a poorly chosen word."
She winces, "I--I don't. Yeah, I know how...err...testy the Punks can get. So, Horn 'N' Stag pub, Winston. Got it. And if he's not there, ask for Harry, yeah?"
Gregory smiles tightly, "Yes. Precisely. Good luck out there, Cassandra. Be safe. And come back...intact."
She faintly chuckles, grabs a few crisps on her way out, and leaves the Haven with a lot of thoughts going through her head.
The drive to this public house is quite long- there's a small buildup of traffic along a few major roads, and Cassandra has to make a few turns and use side streets to bypass the worst of the traffic. And the pouring rain isn't exactly helping matters.
The Ghoul eventually reaches the pub. She parks nearby and keenly stares at the establishment. Its exterior exudes a sort of rustic, old country charm. Its windows are completely opaque, and there's a warm yellowish glow emanating from within.
The sign out front depicts a twisted pair of antlers and a large black horn. She can faintly hear the dull roar of the patrons from within. The Toreador Ghoul grabs her handbag, exits her car, and crosses the street. The rain pelts her umbrella. She glances at the pub's windows once more and takes a deep breath.
She forces it out and pushes the front door open. All the laughter ceases when she steps inside. So many dead eyes turn their gaze towards her. A chill goes up her spine, and she can't help but flinch.
Cassandra is frozen in her spot. The interior of this pub is quite spacious. There are several tables and chairs spread about, a bar with a good many bottles behind it, and a rather large crowd of working-class vampires. A strong, odorous, and pungent smell hits her nose.
It's not necessarily a bad smell, but it is rather overpowering all the same.
A bald man with a scar across his face harshly asks, "Who are you?"
"C--Cassandra. I'm here to see a Winston."
"Winston, eh? Ah, shit. Oi, 'Arry! Some bird's here asking about Winston!"
A rather short and thin fellow with a round face, fair complexion, and dark, greasy hair emerges from the backroom. A bloodied baseball bat is casually swung over his right shoulder. A large scar is visible on his forehead.
He speaks with a sort of nasally and grating tone, "Who the 'ell are you? What do you want?"
Cassandra looks over his shoulder and gasps when she spots a dead body slumped over in the backroom. She quickly averts her gaze and tries her best not to think about what might have happened to that poor lass whose head is a mushy mess.
She says, "I--uh...My name's Cassandra. Gregory sent me."
Harry points his bloodied bat at her, "You're Gregory's Ghoul, eh? Where the 'ell is that fucking muppet?"
Cassandra nervously gulps, "He--he sent me. I am authorized to--to act on his behalf."
Harry groans and shakes his head, "Wonderful. He sent his Ghoul to do his dirty work. How typical of him. What did he tell you?"
Cassandra shakily points to the dead lady slumped over on the floor, "Wh--what happened to her?"
Harry looks over his shoulder and shrugs, "The clone was acting up. Had to be put down."
"Clone?!"
The Brujah plainly nods. He rubs the bloodied sleeve of his bomber jacket on the doorpost, "Yeah. She was a fucking clone. Not a real person. Stop gawking at me like that. She's hardly the first one who 'ad to be put down this week."
Harry nods over to a random Brujah wearing a pair of dark-blue coveralls, "Oi! Muppet! Take that shit off when you're in here, yeah? Hang it up over there." He uses the bat to point to a row of coat racks on the left-hand side.
Cassandra is getting a bit lightheaded. She blinks rapidly and sits down on the closest chair. She stammers, "She--she's not a real person?"
"What are you, daft? She was a clone, not a real human being! Dunno what happened to the original and the CODACORP security team- of which I am a part of- isn't paid to care about the...product."
Another random Punk lifts her scuffed bag of blood high in the air, "Aye! Hear, hear! We drink to all the dead clones, though? Right, Harry?"
Harry flatly nods, "Hmm. So, Gregory sent his Ghoul in his place. Can't say that I'm surprised by that, honestly."
Cassandra grimaces when asking, "Where's--where's Winston?"
Harry's chuckle is mirthless, "Wouldn't you just love to know. Oi, any of you wanna tell this bird where that Elder currently is?"
There's a resounding "No!" from the gathered Rebels.
Harry sighs and lowers his gaze, "Right. Here's the situation, love--"
A young lass comes bursting through the front door. She's drenched in rainwater and heaving for breath. Her clothing is a scattered mishmash of styles and patterns. There's an expression of abject horror on her fair face.
1 side of her chin-length hair is bleached blonde whilst the other side is brown like chocolate. Her eyes are a dull shade of green. She looks to be in her early-30s, at most. And she's absolutely fuming.
Harry glowers at her, "Marble Cake...what do you want--oh, shit--"
(It's not too hard to see why she's called that!)
Marble Cake points an accusatory finger at her fellow Brujah, "Where is she? The latest clone? Did--did you kill her? She--she was showing signs of--of higher thought! You bloody bastard, you already--"
Harry sighs heavily, "Oh, great. This is what happens when you go and make friends with the fuckin' clones! I 'ad to do her in, alright? She was acting all erratic and you know how pissy the company gets when their product tries to pull a runner. No risks, not when our salaries depend on it."
Cassandra quietly whispers, "Clones. Cor blimey, what the 'ell am I--am I getting meself into here?"
Marble Cake hears that and glares at the Ghoul, "Who even are you?"
Harry answers, "Gregory's girl. He was too chicken to show up himself. So, he sent her in his stead- the next best choice, I reckon. You wanna tell her what happened to Winston? You're here, so you 2 might as well 'ave a chin wag, because I'm tired of explaining what went wrong with him."
Marble Cake sighs and tosses her jacket onto a table. She lazily gestures for the Toreador Ghoul to follow her. The pair head to a table in the corner and sit across from each other. Marble Cake takes the seat closest to the wall.
She stares up at some pictures of employees in their CODACORP coveralls. The same dark blue outfits that Harry and many others were wearing.
Cassandra awkwardly introduces herself by holding out her trembling hand. Marble Cake simply shakes it.
Cass twitches at how deathly cold the other woman's fingers are, "Uh...yeah, hiya. I'm Cassandra."
The Brujah motions to the way her hair is colored, "Most of the people around here call me 'Marble Cake'. I don't give out my real name so easily...Cassandra. It's a pretty name, I suppose."
"Th--thanks. You've got...err...nice hair, yeah."
Marble Cake faintly chuckles, "It's some nearly expired hair coloring that I nicked from the Asda's beauty aisle."
She leans closer and growls a bit, "And if you see me blagging stuff, then you didn't? Alright?"
Cassandra gulps and nods, "Yeah. No problem, y--yeah. Sure."
"Times are tough, love. I do what I have to do to cut corners. This city isn't getting any cheaper by the night. The prices keep rising and rising."
"Tell me about it! So, can I meet Winston? Gregory sent me. As his Ghoul, I have been fully authorized to act, speak, and even negotiate on his behalf. That's why I'm here. I need to see Winston."
A look of uneasiness washes over the Rebel's face, "Winston. Ah. About that..."
"What happened?"
A slight screeching noise echoes throughout the pub as Marble Cake winces slightly, "Yeah, that happened."
"What??!"
Marble Cake patiently explains, "He Frenzied. Bad. Real bad, actually. He turned into a Wight. Some of us nearly lost our limbs trying to contain him. This was a few nights ago. We've managed to contain him down in the sewers. In his state, he's already killed 2 sewage workers."
Cassandra's eyes widen to an impossible degree, "Bloody hell, he's a Wight?!"
Marble Cake grimly nods, "Yup. Harry, in his infinite wisdom, doesn't want to tell a damned soul about it. Usually, it's up to the Sheriff to come in and put down anyone who's succumbed to the Wassail, but 'Arry wants it all done...discreetly. If the Sheriff finds out that we have an Elder Wight on our hands, we'll all be in deep shit."
Cassandra is at a loss for words.
Harry returns, having washed all the blood stains from himself. He drags a chair and sits between the 2 women, "I hope that you're done catching her up on all the shite that's happened around 'ere?"
Marble Cake silently nods.
Harry turns his attention to Cass, "Winston's down in the sewers. He's gone. Fucking gone. I sent in a good lad of mine to try and turn him to ashes. No response. I'm not losing a 4th person to that fucking muppet."
He pulls out a pack of cigarettes and lights 1. He hands the pack to Cass, who declines it with a wave of her hand.
Harry forcefully exhales smoke and leans closer, "We've got a bit of a situation, yeah? Normally, we'd send the Childe in to take care of their Frenzying Sire, but Clive- that fucking muppet- is not amongst us. Ran off to America years ago, he did. Winston's other Childer, meanwhile, aren't anywhere close by. None of them are. Fucking muppets."
(He really likes calling people fucking muppets.)
Marble Cake snatches a cigarette from her fellow Punk and lights it up. The brief flicker of the flame causes her to shudder.
Harry continues, "So, the only thing that we've got left on our grubby little hands is a Frenzied Elder. The longer this goes on, the harder it gets to keep everything under wraps."
