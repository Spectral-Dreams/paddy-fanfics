Act 8: Caitiff
"They have no Clan. It is even perhaps possible that said lineage is long-lost or unknown. A flawed or an unknown lineage tends, more often than not, to lead to certain problems within the more organized hierarchies of Kindred Society. Disdain and contempt are tragically the norm. However, those whom I know seem...acceptable. These creatures have been abandoned by their Sires and are lacking in the support that comes with a Clan's structure. As a result, it is innately fractious. They cling to any sort of protection and acceptance- desperately. Many eke out an Unlife on the fringes. Yet, their unpredictability and volatility are what intrigues me the most. Conversely, it also frightens me." -From the personal diaries of Gregory | Entry: On The Caitiff.
Cassandra awakens to the sweet sound of birdsong emanating from outside. She springs out of her bed, hungry and eager to start the day. Her skin crawls when her eyes sweep over her arms. A lingering feeling of phantom insects scuttling across her flesh causes her to shudder a wee bit.
"Brrrr. Blimey, that was...odd." She shrugs her shoulders and fixes herself some breakfast. While the bathtub fills up with water, she brushes her teeth. As for the most important meal of the day, the Ghoul elects to go for 2 pieces of fried bread and a fried egg.
She singsongs some familiar Nicola Green tune while making the food. Her la la la's fill the kitchen as a part of this makeshift rendition. A bird soon makes its own (sweeter) sound and sits on the windowsill of the Ghoul's kitchen.
Cassandra's eyes widen in surprise. She ceases her singing at once and squints to get a better look at the avian. It's a common greenshank with a certain sparkle in its beady birdie eyes.
"Oh, hiya," Cass tentatively greets the bird. The greenshank sings a few notes and cocks its head to the side. It peers right at her for a few seconds and then chirps a handful of times. It flies around and perches itself back on the windowsill again.
The song it whistles is soft, sweet, and charming. The greenshank bird regards the Ghoul for a few seconds more, bows its head, and flutters away.
"A greenshanks..." Cassandra murmurs, her tone filled with awe and recognition. She only knows that the bird was such because she used to date a birdwatcher.
When she and Cass weren't snogging or fingering each other, the birdwatcher lass would impart and bestow some avian-adjacent knowledge onto her. The Toreador Ghoul might not know anything about the various breeds of the different species, but she's at least a teensy bit educated in that regard.
She's going to chalk up this sighting to being a bit of a good omen.
Right? Because that's what it was?
Right?
Well, Cassandra sure bloody hopes so!
She keeps her brekkie in the microwave oven and gets ready for her bath. Cass sinks into the warm and sudsy water. A deeply blissful sigh leaves her mouth as she submerges and relaxes. She throws her head back and closes her eyes while trying to soak in the much-needed tranquility.
Cassandra steps out of the bathtub after having cleaned herself. Her naked body drips with water as she wraps a towel around herself. She trills her lips a few times and struts out of the bathroom.
Since the work truly starts when the sun goes down, Cassandra opts to wear something on the more casual side- a blouse with puffy sleeves, maroon slacks, a headband on top of her head, and some miscellaneous bits of jewelry. A wee smidge of makeup completes the look.
Once Cass has applied her eyeliner, she pouts at her reflection in a playful manner and carefully slides the pencil into the black pot. She straightens her brunette hair a few times and gives her appearance 1 final sweep. With a satisfied smile, she takes her breakfast.
While Cassandra chews her food, she sees that Nicola Green has recently posted a short clip on her official Facebook page. The Ghoul makes an intrigued sound. Her curiosity is piqued, so she turns up the volume and gives the vid her undivided attention.
Soho Starlet is holding the phone quite close to her face. A somewhat flustered sheen coats her pale skin. Her eyes gaze intensely at the screen, looking a bit unfocused.
Yet, she beams, "Hello, my lovelies! Gloughchester, I just want to thank you for such a magical time. From the bottom of me heart, it's been an absolute pleasure to--to perform for you lovely lot, who always cheer me on. Even now, your love and your support...mean the absolute world to me."
Nicola blows a kiss and concludes, "But unfortunately, this is where we must part ways. Farewell, Gloughchester. And to all of my lovelies out there, my sincerest gratitude to you, as well. Mwah!" A wink and a giggle, and the video ends.
Cassandra wistfully stares at the blank screen. She does, however, feel a small measure of relief.
"We--we'll meet again, yeah? Hopefully? Whenever that is..." Cass whispers to herself and wonders what the future holds.
(If only she knew...)
Greg's Ghoul finishes her scran and cleans the plate. A sigh leaves her mouth as she plops down on the couch. Her hazel eyes drift over to the fireplace- it's been a wee while since she has used it.
For now, however, she reads whatever book she can get her hands on. A gray and gloomy mist pervades the streets and the skyline. A sprinkle of raindrops splash against the windows.
An hour passes...
It's now 10:22 AM. Cassandra blows a raspberry and sets her book aside. She pinches the bridge of her nose and then cracks her neck. A slight breeze drifts in through the open windows.
Now is a good time as any to go about the city, Cassandra reckons. She shoves a few things into a random handbag of hers, puts on a raincoat, grabs a purple umbrella, and heads out to the bus stop.
The Toreador Ghoul patiently waits for the bus to arrive. She has no particular destination in mind- the journey, she reasons, is (usually) far more important than the actual destination.
Cassandra idly hums while staring out the window. Rain ceaselessly beats against the glass. She fixes her eyes on the rivulets running down the window, hypnotically losing herself to the rhythmic movement of the raindrops.
She is soon snapped out of her reverie by the ringing of her Blackberry phone. She sighs and answers it, "Hiya, Amy."
Amy softly chuckles, "Hello, yourself. So, did you see the videoclip that Nicola Green posted? She's leaving so soon. But--but why?"
Cass is wholly privy to the why, but she's not about to disclose an iota of information. So, she shrugs and says, "Eh. I haven't the foggiest. I--I mean, I'm sure there's a perfectly valid reason for it, yeah?"
Amy nods, "Hmm. I'm on my break now and I reckoned that I'd give you a quick ring."
Cassandra's lips quirk up into a cheeky smile, "Oh?"
"Mm-hmm. Maybe we could...Have lunch together?"
"Oh. I--I'd love that, yeah. Somewhere private, right? We don't want anyone seeing us...now that I am sort of famous, yeah?"
"Ah, yes, I hadn't quite thought of that. Yes, we...certainly ought to be careful. And it just so happens that I know the right place. It's a bit...err...pricey, though."
Cassandra winces slightly, "Oh, not too posh, yeah?"
Amy shrugs and stares at the ring on her ring finger, "Eh. My treat. What say you, hmm?"
Cassandra chuckles, "Well, if you're paying. Then I'm all for it, yeah. A--are you sure?"
Amy exhales through her nose, "She won't find out, don't you worry your pretty head. Trust me- I know what I'm doing, Cass."
"I--I look forward to it."
"What's wrong? Something the matter? You sound...glum."
"Err...bad reception on me end, I reckon. Yeah, yeah, let's--let's do lunch, hmm? Don't worry, I won't order the most expensive thing on the menu."
Amy slowly chuckles and watches a few pupils going by. She nods at them and absently rubs her ring finger again. In a lowered voice, she responds, "I'll text you the address. I get a long break today, so we...can make the most of it, eh?"
Cassandra feels a flutter in her stomach, "D--definitely, yeah. 1 PM sharp?"
"1 PM sharp," the other woman echoes and hangs up.
Cassandra waits for a couple more minutes and then disembarks the bus. She graces the bus driver with a sincere smile and thanks him with a cordial nod. As she strides away, the driver gives her a once-over and chugs along.
The downpour seems to have died down a bit, leaving behind a damp and mildewy air. But the light rain pelts against Cassandra's purple umbrella. Cobblestone streets, boutique shops, and trendy cafes are all laid out before her.
Cass has a good deal of time to spare. For some reason, a familiar (and high-pitched) whistle- like that of a bird- grabs her attention. She stops in the middle of the busy walkway and attempts to locate the source.
She squints and spots a greenshank- likely the same one from earlier today- perched on a table of an outdoor restaurant. It sings a few notes and offers the Ghoul a significant glance. Cassandra breathes in sharply through her mouth.
The bird flies a circular motion in the air and dashes off into an alleyway.
"Huh. How odd!"
(Pleasantly or unpleasantly?)
Regardless, Cassandra doesn't pay it much mind. Her eyes dart over to the shops and linger on a boutique store whose outrageously posh prices are advertised right on the large window.
Cass groans, rolls her eyes, and moves on.
Throngs of mortals can be seen going about their days. Business, shopping, working, playing, dining, and the like. The rays of the sun barely peek through the slate clouds as the afternoon slowly looms closer and closer.
While Cass engages in some window-shopping, she spots the very same greenshank perched atop a living statue. The person is decked in lavish regalia and strikes a very dignified pose. He remains still despite the bird sitting atop his crown.
Cassandra tentatively approaches and notices the bird staring right at her with its glimmering, beady eyes.
"Erm..."
The bird extends its neck, chippers a few tunes, and tilts its head to the side.
"Hiya."
The statue fella looks on ahead. Other passersby stop and take pictures of the still-as-the-grave performer. Meanwhile, the greenshank keeps its eyes locked on the Ghoul.
A random lad in a puffer jacket hollers at her, "Oi! Keep your distance, luv! Don't distract him and ruin the shot!"
She flinches.
The bird utters out a low noise, flaps its wings, and takes flight.
Cassandra watches the bird ascend higher and higher. To see what is likely to be the same bird in such a short span of time over and over again is...confusing, at the very least.
Somehow, the Ghoul feels an overwhelming sense of calm.
How intriguing.
Cassandra nods to the puffer jacket lad, presses her lips into a thin line, and takes a few steps back.
Her thoughts swirl with questions while the people around her stop and admire the living statue. They take pictures with phones and digicams, leave little tips, and compliment him on the job well done.
After a few more minutes, the statue man moves. He releases a huge breath and shakes his head. His sea-green eyes sweep over the people, and he heaves a deep sigh. His wrinkly face goes slack, and he steps down from the podium.
He stares at the Ghoul, "Can I help you, miss?"
"You did a good job, yeah?"
"I reckon." He drags out a rather large and sturdy lunchpail from under a bench.
Cassandra's eyes widen when she sees his lunchpail. A surprised little chuckle escapes her.
The statue man regards her with a cryptic smile, "Is something funny, eh?"
She nervously points to his lunchpail, "Nice lunchpail."
The man's painted face scrunches in confusion, "Uh...thanks? What's so funny about that?"
"Err...heh, nothing."
"Oh, no, I insist."
Cassandra nervously shifts her weight around, "Me dad once had a similar looking lunchpail. When I was small, I used to decorate it with--with stickers and the like, yeah."
He opens the lunchpail and pulls out a sandwich wrapped up in thin plastic, "Hm. Interesting. Sounds like your dad was an interesting man, miss."
"He was a miner, he was."
"Ah. That explains why he'd need a big lunchpail."
Cass smiles weakly, "Yeah."
The man unwraps his sandwich slowly, "Funny thing is, my own da was a miner. I remember, as a wean, coming along to those massive strikes back in the 1980s. Judging by your age, you wouldn't have the foggiest as to what I'm talking about. But I remember, very clearly, seeing him and his friends walking the picket lines with their big signs and bright banners."
The Ghoul's total cumulative age is likely close to this mortal performer's age. She herself vividly recalls going along with her father to the same strikes during 1984 and 1985.
Tumultuous times, to say the least.
Cassandra softly smiles, "Yeah, you--you're probably right. I'll leave you to it, then. Bye!"
He nods and chews on his food. Cass fishes into her handbag and tosses some coin into his hat. His sea-green eyes watch her intently as she walks away.
"Strange people you meet every day. Hm, well...thank you for the kind donation, at least."
It's now 12:58 PM. Cass already got Amy's text. With 2 minutes left to spare, she hurries along to the aforementioned restaurant.
The rain ceases for but a short while, leaving behind a dreary, misty atmosphere in its wake. Distant thunder rumbles and occasional flashes of lightning illuminate the slate skies above.
The eating establishment in question is...cozy, dark, but rather welcoming. It's entirely indoors and the windows are tinted. The linoleum floor is smooth, and a colorful array of abstract art decorates the walls. Extremely dim lights hover above the tables.
There are 2 floors. The mezzanine floor looks to be far less occupied than the ground floor. Overall, the interior design is decidedly contemporary. The indoor water fountain serves as the main centerpiece.
"Cor blimey..." is all Cass can manage to say as she takes it all in. Despite it being quite dark in here, there is still enough natural light coming in from the outside to make certain things out.
Amy is casually sitting at a corner table on the top floor, scrolling through her smartphone. She's dressed in a plain blouse with geometric designs, black jeans, and a nose ring. Her short black hair looks like it has recently been washed.
A faint smile blooms upon Amy's mouth when her eyes sweep over the Ghoul. They greet each other with a hug and sit down.
"Glad you could make it," Amy states as a wider smile plays about her glossy lips.
Cassandra chuckles, "You paid a small fortune so that we could have a nice, fancy lunch, yeah?"
Amy shrugs and sets her phone aside. Cassandra notices the tan line on the other lady's ring finger. A blink passes before the Ghoul abruptly glances away and wiggles on the chair.
She awkwardly clears her throat and snatches the menu booklet. It's all a tad on the posh side, but it isn't the most excruciatingly expensive place in the entire world.
Cassandra's eyebrows shoot up when she confesses, "Wow! These prices are actually quite...fair."
"I know. So, how have things been with you, hmm? I don't have much time, so we'll have to make the most of it. Do you fancy a spot of wine?"
"Oh, I'm not sure. S--should you be drinking?"
Amy looks to the side, "It's just a glass, babe. Wine?"
"Uh, sure...white wine," Cassandra mutters and checks her phone. She puts it on silent and looks through the bulky menu booklet.
A waiter strolls over a couple minutes later and takes their orders.
Amy, for her part, goes for a beef Wellington with a side of asparagus and boiled baby potatoes. She is sure to clearly mention her nut allergy to the waiter. The waiter scribbles it down and fixes the other lass with a friendly grin.
Cassandra decides to keep it simple, and she settles on the braised cod on a bed of Schezwan rice with a side of roasted baby carrots and mushrooms. She politely asks for some extra saffron to be added on the fish.
The waiter bows his head and tells the duo that their order will take some time owing to a sudden surge in demand- some outside business is making a rather large request, so the staff is a bit tied up.
The women assure him that they're in no rush and then thank him. He takes their menus and trots off.
"So," Amy begins and leans back in her seat, "you met Soho Starlet and even got to spend some with her! Tell me all about it."
Cassandra looks down at her own clothing and wonders out loud, "I--I'm not underdressed for this place, am I?"
Amy clicks her tongue and lazily waves a hand around, "Oh, don't fret. Relax, you're fine. Tell me, how was it? Being in such close proximity to THE Nicola Green, eh?"
"It was...fun, yeah. Exciting, thrilling, exhilarating...all of those things. And--and so much more."
"You seem nervous, what's wrong? Don't worry, like I said before, I'm paying for today's lunch, so you don't have to stress. All right?"
"No, no, it's not that. Err--sorry, I mean, thanks for the treat, I--I really appreciate it."
Amy smirks, "I'm sure you do. We can always figure out how you're going to repay me." A suggestive look asserts itself all over her face.
Cassandra furiously blushes, "Y--yeah, I reckon that we'll uh...come to an...agreeable arrangement."
Amy drums her fingers on the table and bites her bottom lip, "I've always been one for agreeable arrangements. I want to know how you felt in her presence, babe."
"Giddy. Almost anxious. Nervous. But Nicola is absolutely down-to-earth, no pretensions or fakery. She's a massive sweetheart. You wouldn't believe it, yeah."
"Oh. Wow. That's quite nice, I suppose."
Cassandra leans forward and puts her hand atop Amy's while slowly asking, "And how has your day been, hmm?" The Ghoul's thumb gently caresses the back of the other woman's hand in a circular fashion.
Amy sighs, "Same old, same old. I'd call it boring were it not for the obvious--"
"The crumbling walls and the unwillingness of anyone to do anything about it."
"Yes, precisely. Every day, I see the fear in the eyes of those pupils. Some of them come up to me and ask when it's going to be fixed up. And I--I can't give them an answer because even I'm wondering the same fucking thing."
"It's not fair..."
Amy bitterly scoffs, "Tch, it's worse than that. It's a travesty. We're scheduled to give an MP a tour of our crumbling school sometime...soon. Schedules keep conveniently changing, though."
Cassandra sharply exhales, "Blimey. It's like the people on the top hate us all so much that they don't want to even touch the school. Can't or won't, doesn't matter, either way, the message that us ordinary people get is still the same."
"A rather grim and depressing appraisal, but not one that I wholly disagree with, Cass. Do you recall the Lakanal House Fire of 2009? I tell you, neglect will be the death of all of us, what with all of the money that continues to bleed from the school...and other vital services."
Cassandra grimaces tightly when that tragedy back in London is brought up, "Ah. Yeah. Shoddy renovations, too. Somehow, the powers-that-be are engaging in something far, far worse than neglect. We've got a bunch of right dodgy bastards in charge of keeping us safe, hm?"
Amy grunts, "Well, they're failing in their duty of care towards us. Fucking wankers. I'm just hoping that something will make the right people pay attention. Protests, petitioning, journalists, screeching at bloody MPs, Twitter, something. Anything."
"We shouldn't 'ave to live in constant fear of our surroundings, yeah? It's not fair."
"Tell me about it, babe. Listen, let's...take a breather. As it is, I already have work-related stress. I don't need more. You look lovely today, by the way."
Cassandra returns the heartfelt compliment with a loving smile, "Thanks. And so do you."
"Psssh, oh please, this is just my average, everyday work clothes. Nothing fancy, nothing out of the ordinary."
"You'd look good in anything, Amy. Even a big brown sack would suit you."
Amy giggles and lightly shoves the other woman's arm, "Hah. God, are you ever the charmer, aren't you? What's next, me in a cardboard box?"
Cassandra naughtily smirks, "You without a cardboard box."
Amy purses her lips and chuckles. She favors the woman seated opposite her with a toothy smile, "We'll see about that, eh? How's your vacation treating you? Are you...relieved to be away from your work?"
Cass responds, "Obviously, yeah. But there are times when I can't help but think about...the outstanding stuff before me vacation sort of abruptly began."
"Abruptly began?"
"Oh, err...yeah, err...it's hard to explain. But I'm not, like, totally stressed. Now that I have plenty of free time. I'm still kind of...busy doing...other things and the like. B--but I'm glad that I'm spending this time with you, yeah."
Amy narrows her eyes a little, "If you say so. You work for some very strange people, Cassandra."
(She sure does!)
Cass nervously laughs and averts her gaze. Some sweat begins to form on her face.
"Are you alright, babe?"
"Y--yeah, fine. Just thinking about...things, yeah."
"You mentioned things," Amy states, "what 'other things' would these be, hmm?"
Cassandra shrugs a shoulder and plays with the cutlery on the table, "Ehh...just the sort of things that- for myriads of reasons- I'm not quite certain I can talk about with you."
"Oooh, secretive things, hmm? And what are these things that you ought not to delve into, hm?"
Cassandra's eyebrows go up. She nervously gulps and fixes the other woman with a look of despondency, "I--I can't, sorry. Please don't make me feel any more guilty. Like, I would love to tell you and all, b--but I can't."
Amy straightens her back, purses her lips, and stares right into the other lass's hazel eyes, "Our relationship is a secret. What's 1 more added to the pile, eh?"
"..."
Amy slouches in her seat and sighs heavily, "Oh, please, don't give me that look. Look, babe, I'm not trying to--"
The greenshank bird from earlier somehow made its way INSIDE the restaurant. It flies straight towards the ladies and circles their heads. It lets out a few of its birdy noises and lands on the edge of their table.
Its beady eyes bore right into Cassandra.
Amy's jaw hangs open in disbelief.
Cass's hazel eyes are wide in shock and confusion.
Amy slowly speaks, "W--what is--how did it even get in here??"
Cass shakes her head from side to side while still staring at the bird, "I--I've no idea. I swear, this little bugger has been...following me ever since I saw it on me windowsill earlier this morning."
Amy inquires, "What?? Really??"
Cassie shrugs, "It's the only logical explanation that I 'ave, yeah."
The bird doesn't pay Amy any mind. It remains laser-focused on the Ghoul. Now, it doesn't utter a single sound.
Cassandra's index finger reaches out and cautiously taps the bird's forehead, "Good little bird. Err...nice birdie? Are you the same one that's been following me around? Greenshanks, you lot, yeah? How many of you are there in this city?"
Amy interjects, "Not that many, I'd reckon. Say, how do you know that it's a greenshank?"
"Briefly dated a birdwatcher once upon a time, I did. She was a--a nice gal."
"Huh. I guess we all have our histories and the like."
"Yup."
The greenshank bird emanates a few musical notes and gracefully hops off the table. When the waiter approaches it with a broom to shoo it away, it loudly screeches and immediately takes flight.
"Excuse me for interrupting," the waiter huffs and puffs, "your meals will be ready soon. Thank you for bearing with us, ladies."
He rapidly struts away and hurries back into the kitchen. The other patrons murmur and mutter amongst themselves. What an odd sighting, indeed!
Cassandra awkwardly coughs and rubs her bicep. She sincerely asks Amy, "C--can we please drop it, Amy? Please, for me? I'd much prefer not to dwell on certain aspects of...me life."
Amy nods in resignation, "I can respect that, Cass. Fair enough. It was...wrong of me to be so pushy. I just...want what we have to be honest and open. Or as honest and open as an affair can be."
Cassandra flinches when that is mentioned. She lowers her head, and her eyes catch sight of the cutlery and smooth cloth napkin. A feeling of guiltiness floods within her. She exhales slowly and pinches the bridge of her nose.
Amy notices the uneasiness and queries, "Something the matter? Did I say something wrong?"
"Is--is what we're doing...improper?"
Amy rubs her forehead, "Oh, not this again. For fuck's sake, you make it sound like we're doing something downright illegal. Come on, darling, we've had this conversation before, remember? Many times...and in various stages of undress, if memory serves correctly."
Cassandra huffs, "Right. But you're still married."
Amy pays that remark no heed and decides to swiftly move on, "So, after this, when can we meet next?"
Cass gets the hint and decides to drop it as well, "Hff. Fair enough. I--I dunno. I'll have to check me schedule and--and see when I can fit you in."
"Saucy minx," Amy teases, "you can squeeze me in anytime of the night."
Cassie plainly replies, "That's when I happen to be busy, but I'll...make some time. Hopefully. Maybe you could swing by me place some night and stay there until morning?"
Amy places her chin atop her clasped hands, "A tempting offer. Perhaps. We'll see what happens then, hmm? Tell me, have you thought more about traveling to a new country?"
"I 'ave, yeah. You?"
"Honestly, not much. Believe it or not, but I rather like it here...in England. I've become accustomed to the weather down to my very bones, hahaha! But...she wants to visit the South of France sometime. Oh, and to Germany, too. She claims that it's beautiful in springtime."
Cassandra subtly encourages her, "Well, y--you should go, innit? It's a right good opportunity to pass up on. People never get chances like this all that often, yeah?"
Amy has that uncertain glint in her eyes again, "I'd be missing out..."
"On what?"
Amy fixes the Ghoul with a knowing look that says it all without mouths needing to be moved or sounds needing to be made out loud.
Cassandra chuckles nervously and adjusts her posture, "Y--yeah, that's a valid point, I--I suppose. And I don't know if I 'ave the kind of dosh lying around to come tagging along all secret like."
Amy rests her chin atop her balled-up fist and idly blows air between her pursed lips, "The complexity of it all...us, in particular. It's quite...something at times, isn't it?"
Cassandra agrees by way of a small grunt, "Mm-hmm. You could always--"
Amy firmly interrupts, "No. I--I can't. It's not that simple. Nothing in this world of ours is ever that easy. What, divorce my wife and ride off into the sunset with you, or some tripe like that? Save that for the pictures, Cass."
"Why is it 'not that simple', exactly?"
Amy clicks her tongue, "Please, let's not talk any further about this, OK? Oh, look, lunch is coming." She jerks her chin over to the approaching waiter.
Cassandra peers over shoulder and sighs, "Finally."
She points out the obvious, "But--but we really should have this conversation- seriously- at some point, yeah?"
Amy simply chuckles and languidly waves her hand, "Whatever you say, darling. Now, dig in before it gets cold."
Cassandra shakes her head and puts on a smile for the approaching waiter.
The yummy, piping-hot food is adroitly placed down onto the table, and the waiter bows before making himself scarce. Steam billows up from the plates.
"Gosh!" is all Amy can say in a tone of sheer surprise.
Cassandra is just as astonished when she waves the hot vapor away with her hand, "Woooh! Hot, hot, hot, hot...!"
Amy chuckles and reaches out to squeeze the other woman's shoulder, "Hot? Why, yes, it is hot, isn't it?"
Cassandra's hand goes under the table, and she reaches out to touch Amy's thigh. A flirtatious glance is exchanged.
"Mm, careful, now. You've got an audience..."
Cass gingerly withdraws her hand and murmurs, "Sorry. Yeah. Yeah, you're right. Lots and lots of eyes on us. Better keep it below PG13. Heh."
Amy lightly laughs, "Oh, relax. Do you think that I'm the only married person having an affair in Gloughchester? No, definitely not."
Cassandra raises both her eyebrows as she readies her cutlery. She nervously swallows the lump in her throat, "There's loads of 'em out there..."
Amy casually shrugs and echoes, "There's loads of 'em out there. Hmm. I am well and truly famished. This looks positively delightful."
"So do you," Cass purrs back, "so do you."
"Oh, stop it, you minx. I wouldn't want to leave a moist spot on these fine chairs. The horror..."
Cassandra clicks her tongue and lightly kicks the other gal under the table. They both giggle and start the task of eating through their respective lunches. Various subjects are talked about as they slowly chew and swallow.
People come and go. Some patrons choose to eat here, whereas others opt to take their scran home and gobble it down in their...knickers while watching telly.
White wine is sipped and cutlery gently makes contact with porcelain. The two women joke and laugh about things. A tension, albeit slight, simmers beneath their words- an unspoken language of sorts. Topics drift hither and yon.
There is a familiarity shared between them. Also, an intimacy that simply cannot be ignored...illicit as it may be.
Amy stabs a baby potato with her fork and lifts it up. She chuckles at it before eating it with a delighted expression before stating, "So, I was thinking of popping by the local Sainsbury's to do some shopping- mostly miscellaneous items."
Cassandra makes a face, "Ugh."
Amy casts a sidelong glance, "Have something against Sainsbury's, do you?"
Cassandra nods and scoops up some Schezwan rice with her fork, "You've got that right, yeah." She hesitates for a moment before putting the food into her mouth.
"Care to tell me why?"
"Me mum used to work for them- treated her bad, they did. Wankers. So, I'm...boycotting them. Indefinitely."
"Wow," Amy remarks while shaking her head, "that's quite the grudge, isn't it? You know, you never spoke about your parents. Why is that?"
Cass uncomfortably shuffles in her seat and nearly drops her knife, "I--erm...it all happened a--a long time back."
(In the Ghoul's case, it was definitely quite a long time ago!)
Amy points out, "I mean, you know about my parents and how they didn't quite accept me soon after I came out of the closet. I had to leave the fishing village that I grew up in and--and start from scratch in a big, unfamiliar city." Her eyes begin to water a little.
