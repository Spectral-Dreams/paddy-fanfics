Act 6: Children of Ennamaru
"The Tragedians (or Ennamari) fancy themselves as Machiavellians, scheming, plotting, and manipulating from the shadows. Unfortunately, I have been caught in between their bickering. What was meant to be a simple art deal has somehow turned into something rather messy. The Ennamari are rather full of themselves, I should add. And they are not nearly as powerful as they claim to be. But their proximity to Kindred politics is what makes them volatile, if nothing else. I ought to free myself of their clutches soon." -From the personal diaries of Gregory | Entry: On The Children Of Ennamaru.
The sun is up when Cassandra wakes up with a start, sweating profusely and breathing heavily. She dry-heaves for a few moments before regaining control of her senses. It takes her a few more minutes to calm down and regain her scattered composure.
With the sun up, that means that the Kindred of the UK are now fast in the clutches of their day-sleep.
The Toreador Ghoul shuffles out of her bed and rubs her face with the heel of her palm. A warm bath in the ol' tub and some fresh clothes should help to ease her weary and frayed nerves.
She strips out of her bedwear and lets it all pool on the floor. From there, she brushes her teeth, sings her fave tunes, and sways from side to side while looking at herself in the mirror.
Then, she pouts and makes faces at herself before sinking into the depths of the bubble bath, the fresh flowers atop the water bobbing about. For a while, Cassandra basks in the blissful moment.
After that, she dries herself off and slips into a simple tracksuit- the real duty eternal starts when the sun goes down, so she reckons that there's no point in dressing to impress right now.
She pulls her laptop out, opens the lid, jabs her index finger on the power button, and patiently waits for it to boot up. She checks her emails- just work-related stuff, but she's on vacation now, so there's really no point in answering any of those emails right now.
After some idle browsing, she checks what's up on her Facebook. Just the usual stuff from her many, many friends. Road trips, new uni semesters, a vacay in sunny Spain, going on an indefinite strike, cool clothes at cheap prices, the latest gadgets on sale, and oh, just about anything and everything.
While browsing through a random group, she comes across an overly excited post from a local resident that reads: "hey hey! heya! alright, ya know what time it isssssss! MUNCHIE BOX TIME! Authentic SCOTTISH Munchie Boxes! The best part? Only 5 pound! That's right, only a fiver for each box!"
The post concludes: "They've got it all in those boxes. Kebabs, tikkas, pakoras, garlic bread, chow mein, cherry tomatoes, red onions, naans, chips, fried rice, chicken nuggets, mozzarella stix, coleslaw, you name it, it's probs in there! YUMMY, innit? Just pop by Toft's Road East anytiiiiime!"
Cassandra's eyebrows go up in delighted surprise, "Well, it looks like lunch is all settled, yeah?"
As for breakfast, she reheats some old leftovers and slowly nibbles on them while browsing through the FB. She likes a few things that either elicit a chuckle out of her or generally give her a positive vibe.
But that isn't the only reason why Cass is browsing Facebook in the morning. She looks around, nervously licks her lips, wipes her hands, and brings the laptop closer to her.
There used to be a Facebook group that she was a part of. It was referred to as "The Dead Butler's Society" (DBS for short). It was founded by a Ventrue Ghoul and meant EXCLUSIVELY for Ghouls.
They would regularly chat about servitude and other sundry matters, exchange information on their Domitors (sensitive or otherwise), get their sippy-sips arranged (from someone who isn't the Domitor) and fixed by other members, and offer general moral support to each other.
The Ghoul who founded the group- along with his Domitor- now have a nasty case of head loss. After receiving a dosage of decollation, the group members promptly left. There were, however, rumors that the DBS is back.
But as an exclusive, invite-only WhatsApp group. Cass is hoping to find some leads so that she can rejoin this tight-knit community and catch up with her fellow retainers from all over the UK. No such luck, though.
"DBS Mark II" as it's now being called is far more secretive than Mark I ever was (or could hope to be). However, Cassandra is hoping to get to the bottom of this at some point in the near future.
She's been trying to find a way in- any way in- for the past year. But the new admins take their secrecy, safety, and security VERY seriously. They won't even accept newcomers via referrals, which would normally be an instant sign-up.
And rumors are that the new iteration of DBS is going worldwide, no longer just confined to Perfidious Albion and its dominions. Now, this shit's going global!
And Cassandra would very much like to be a part of it. Call it curiosity; she wants to know how, say, a Toreador Ghoul (like herself) is in a country like China or Uruguay or Estonia or somewhere far flung. She wants to know how these other retainers are holding up.
What are their lives like? What are their own Domitors like?
How old are these Ghouls? What were they before the Blood entered their lives (and bodies)? What do they hope to get out of all of this?
So many questions swim through this lass's head, but she knows better than to pry.
She heaves a wistful sigh, "Ah. Better not tread on any beartraps, yeah? But I--I'm still gonna try and find me way back in there!"
A gray and gloomy mist lingers outside. The rays of the sun barely shine through it. After doing some light reading, Cassandra puts on a pair of sneakers, takes a deep breath, and sets off to go and buy her lunch.
She leaves the Lexus behind and opts for public transportation this time around. The long bus is red in color and starting to show its age. The thin and baggy-eyed bus driver tightly holds onto the steering wheel and keeps his gaze firmly glued to the road ahead.
There's a very large cuppa coffee sitting inside the built-in cup holder. The small digital screen behind the driver is perpetually flickering on and off. He dryly greets her with a brief nod and says nothing more.
She chooses to sit towards the back and lowers the seat down a bit so that she can get into a comfortable position. A gentle pitter-patter of the rain against the glass windows serenades her as the bus makes its way to the next stop.
Numerous office-going types alight at this point, while an equal number of passengers gets on. The Ghoul fiddles with her Blackberry phone, wondering to herself whether now is the time to ditch this for an Android smartphone- it's all the rage these days!
A couple of groups of youngsters board the bus. They're an assorted group clad in puffer jackets, beanies, sunglasses, and varying looks on their faces, some of which have piercings. There's a lad sitting in the back, tapping his feet impatiently on the floor.
Cassandra looks over her shoulder and faintly smiles, thinking back to her younger days, how she witnessed the world changing from the relative privilege of quasi-immortality, and how the rapidly changing world in question shaped her into what she is today.
The bus reaches a stop. Cassandra thanks the bus driver and dismounts. A brisk walk later, and she's finally at her destination- a small row of food shops located at the corner of a wet cobblestone street.
When the rain intensifies, Cass seeks shelter under an awning that's quite wide. She hums to herself, not really minding the precipitation 1 way or another. She pulls out her phone and decides to give Amy a quick ring.
(She's likely on her break, so what better time than now?).
The phone rings and rings until Amy picks up, her voice mildly lilted, "Oh! Hey, babe! I was just about to ring. You still have those cookies for me, right?"
Cassandra softly chuckles, "You'll 'ave to...come and get it from me." Her tone oozes with innuendo.
Amy bites her lower lip, "Hmmmm. I know a way to get my treats from you, but...not tonight. Work's quite a lot more hectic than I'd care to admit. This day is already shaping up to be rather gloomy."
Cassandra tries to lighten the mood by stating, "Maybe you're just mistaking your day for the weather? It's pretty...drizzly outside, y'know?"
Amy groans, "Why are you like this? Shit, sorry. N--never mind. I am literally staring at a cracked wall. This school isn't what it used to be, so we'll see if we get a fresh coat of paint in time for the new semesters. But the painting is--is hardly the issue, eh?"
"Yeah. You wanna do lunch later? Found out about this place through me FB. Authentic Scottish munchie boxes, only for £5! Just a fiver! I'll spend a tenner and get--"
"Ah. As much as I'd like that, I'm not so sure about what they load into those boxes. My wi--she got 1 a while ago and it sort of messed with her stomach, left her with a bit of an upset tummy for the rest of the weekend. And my nut allergy is hardly a laughing matter."
Cassandra's expression falls, "Oh. Right. I hadn't thought about that. Sorry. Maybe we could ask them to put only the...err...non-nutty stuff in there, yeah? Something that's not too...I dunno, greasy either?"
Amy sighs faintly, "Please, listen to me. The answer is still no. Now, I have to go now. Breaktime's almost up and I have some rather interesting faculty meetings to attend. Let's talk tonight, OK? I'll try to come over, but don't make me promise anything. Err...ta-ta."
Cassandra slowly exhales, "Yeah. Bye."
The call ends and the Ghoul is left feeling a bit bummed out. She stands there for a few moments, unmoving, staring blankly at the shopfronts ahead. Once she shakes herself out of her trance, she makes her way towards 1 of the many food stalls.
It's quite hard to pass up on such a bulky thing for a fiver! The lines are rather long and contain a diverse array of people from those in high-vis vests to suited-out office drones and everyone else in between.
A heavyset man with caramel skin, a thick beard, and tattoos all over his arms is in charge of manning the counter. The cash register is rather old and in need of some minor repairs, but it is LOADED with scrilla.
The big man is wearing an apron, chef's hat, and has his sleeves rolled up. He has a fairly neutral expression on his face.
Cass greets him, "Hiya. Saw this on Facebook. Thought I'd pop by and try this out. So..." She has a fiver wedged between her index and middle fingers.
The man grabs it and calls out in a thick Scottish accent, "Oi! 'Mon then! Get a move on! Any special dietary restrictions and/or requirements? We also have kosher, halal, and vegan options."
"Nah. Just load it up with the usual whatever, yeah? Can I get some extra olives with that?"
"Of course. Oi! Extra olives! Chop-chop! We've got a long line and hungry line givin' us all sorts of looks!"
WowWee! The munchie box is rather heavy! Since Cass didn't bring her umbrella, she shrugs and uses the box as one, pulling it over her head. She wanted to go home and enjoy this, but the weather and rain said otherwise.
She feels rather awkward and out of place, however, with the large box shielding her from the elements. Once she finds adequate indoor seating, she gently puts the box down on a table and then opens it. Her nose is hit with an aroma so amazing that she just has to lick her lips and grin from ear to ear.
"Cor blimey! This is hardly a meal for 1! Me and Amy could've easily shared this! Ah well, the leftovers should be nice and good, yeah?"
She eats what she can and takes the rest back home for dinner. Once back in the comfort of her cozy cabin, she plops herself down on the couch and blows a raspberry.
The phone beckons for her attention once more. She spends a few moments rifling through the apps, and then looks up the next scheduled Nicola Green concert.
The Ghoul's eyes widen in surprise when she sees that her favorite singer of all time is coming to Gloughchester. And soon.
A tweet from her official account simply has an overhead picture of the city along with the word "SOON" in bold lettering. Cassandra knows that tickets for her shows tend to sell out INSTANTLY.
She bites her lower lip in anticipation and HOPES to goodness that she'll be able to get a way to see Soho Starlet's live performance! It'd make everything worth it.
Cass squeals excitedly and nods her head furiously, "This is--this is awesome!!"
Gregory's Ghoul finds it rather hard to fall asleep (the reasons are plainly obvious). But she soon dozes off at around 4:06 PM. Her loud snoring can be heard throughout the cabin.
It's a decent nap.
But, sadly, not an uninterrupted one. You-know-who's presence can be felt in every crevice of the place that the Ghoul now finds herself drawn into.
There's a terrible smell in the air. Even the mist is a foul shade of green. Howls and screeches emanate from all conceivable directions.
Sven snaps his fingers, "Hey, hey, hey! Damn, talk about affordable slop, huh? What do they put in that crap, anyway? Keeping the rest for dinner, huh? Smart move. Back in the States, I've seen people throw away tons- and I mean TONS- of perfectly good food on a daily basis. Eugh."
He goes on, "I mean, sure, I'm literally a multi-millionaire, so why'd I care, right? Yeah, well...I wasn't born into millions, y'know? But I'm not about to get all squishy and vulnerable with a Ghoul like ya."
Cassandra is powerless to react or respond in any meaningful way.
The Baali paces around her, "Tell Sheriff Alice I said hi. And tell her that I'm not about to give up my location that easily. Not even to a gun-toting hottie like her, hahaha! Hey, should I make Carla my Ghoul? I've been giving it some thought- it's either that or treat her the same way you treat your...munchie box. Eh, you Brits are crazy when it comes to your talk AND your cuisine, aren't ya?"
Sven rubs his chin in contemplation, "Not too shabby. And don't forget to give your gal-pal those cookies, OK? Yeah, I got a few more hours before I'm pulled outta my beauty nap. But you..."
The Demon Worshipper claps his hands once. When his palms touch, a resounding and guttural howl fills the area, causing Cassandra to flinch in terror. Her eyes pop open and she dry-heaves once again, still tightly clutching onto her belly.
"Shit! Shit!" Cass bangs her mattress and buries her face into her hands, sobbing uncontrollably. She wipes her face and struggles to catch her breath.
Sunset is right around the corner...
Gregory stirs in his day-sleep. He feels the dreaded presence of the foul Aapilu lingering quite close by. Far too close for his liking. It makes him feel rather queasy, even though his Unliving body isn't supposed to process things like that.
Still, he's VERY perturbed by it. This time, the Aapilu is at the side of his bed. Its face is mere inches away from his. The Toreador Neonate trembles as he bears witness to the mouthless, 5-eyed creature of his own Vitae.
Words themselves fail to form and express how Gregory feels about this. But his stiff body and the fact that he is unable to speak speaks volumes of just how afraid he is. He feels a presence behind him, and something firmly grabbing onto the back of his head.
The Aapilu tilts its head and makes that ominous clicking sound. A memory flashes back with almost violent intensity.
Gregory was a Civil Servant in mortal life- a Whitehall lad through and through. Nothing so fancy, mind you. Just reams of paperwork, approving permits, signing off on fairly inconsequential things, reading letters, attending meetings, and doing a fair bit of research here and there.
Then the austerity era (austerity error, more like) rolled in and Gregory lost his job. By then, he'd already been Embraced into the Clan of the Rose. Before that, his wife divorced him after their young daughter suddenly died- she had just turned 21.
(After a few years, Greg's wife would pass away due to natural circumstances).
The distant past is all so jumbled and muddled up in Gregory's head. Memories coalesce and spread throughout his mind like an indescribable amorphous mass.
There's a reason why Greg harbors SO MUCH resentment towards the Thatcher government (and conservativism in general). That time was hard on the world as a whole.
Gregory would always be diligent in his work. His hand would rarely tire while his handwriting remained nothing short of neat, excellent, and in-line with official protocols. He would rarely take breaks, but he would take his lunch on time.
It was almost always fish and chips wrapped in a newspaper. He'd quietly sit on a bench, eat slowly, chew properly, wipe his lips with a napkin, throw out the trash, and head back to the office for more work.
After doing his work, Gregory would go back to a somewhat furnished London flat. The postwar years were rather brutal and sent an entire generation into what seemed like a perpetually hopeless situation.
Greg's wife was a homemaker. She would do most of the cleaning, shopping, and cooking.
And child-raising, too. While the then-Civil Servant wasn't an uninvolved parent, he would always take some of his work home with him- only the urgent bits that needed completion and couldn't wait. So, he could sign off on assorted forms while munching on fish and chips.
The wife and him used to attend plays together- Shakespeare, that sort of thing. The young daughter was left in the care of some close relatives, as is typically the norm with these kinds of arrangements.
They'd clap for the play (if it was good, which it usually was), mingle around for a bit, then head back to their flat. Sometimes, Gregory's wife would have her friends from the local Anglican church over.
Why is the Aapilu doing this to him? Bringing these mixed memories up? Why now?
In the here and now, the Toreador can't help but think to himself, "Why--why are you tormenting me? Damn you!" His lips do not move, however. But his eyes continue to look straight ahead.
More clicking noises come from the abomination as more memories violently spill forth into Gregory's mind.
Greg and his family were middle class (lower-middle class if one wishes to be particular about it). Money wasn't THAT MUCH of an issue...until it was.
The daughter was able to attend a grammar school that wasn't as close to the flat as the parents would've liked, but the bus routes were good and convenient enough. She would always get good grades in her examinations, be diligent, polite, well-mannered, and take part in drama and stage acting.
There were also church groups that she'd take part in on weekends- volunteering, charitable works, good deeds, etc.
But now, Gregory can't even remember the names of his spouse and daughter without a pain shooting through his brain. How can a soul like him end up in a situation like this?
The Aapilu backs away from its victim, slowly and calculatingly.
Things were different back then: the postwar era, rebuilding after the horrors of the Blitz, the emergence of the welfare state, and everything else which had defined that particular epoch. And Greg, his family, and everybody else were very much affected, morphed, shaped, and changed by it all.
The dreaded and abominable creature of the Vitae soon fades from view, but not before staring right at Gregory with its 5 eyes. Its 5 very familiar eyes- the middle one was quite bloodshot, which it usually isn't.
Gregory soon regains control of his body and props himself up with his elbows. He sits there in silence, wondering if what he just saw (and experienced) was real or not. He blinks a few times and gets out of bed with a harsh groan.
After shaking off that dreaded experience, he looks out the window and watches the darkening skies. His enhanced vampiric senses permit him to pick up on a few of the sounds emanating from the lands below.
Some crickets chirp. Owls hoot. Crows caw. Leaves fall from trees and make a soft rustling noise as they flutter down. A full-body shiver runs through the Toreador Neonate, and he gets this ominous feeling that something quite dreadful is about to occur.
He puts those feelings down and heads for a shower. He stands under the showerhead and watches as the nozzles pelt his cold and naked body with warm water. Steam rises and engulfs his pale face.
The sound of the water being poured out from the nozzles reverberates throughout the tiled bathroom.
As for clothing, the Artisan elects to dress lightly tonight- a plain-colored dress shirt, navy-blue slacks, and polished oxfords (a belt is hardly a necessity). The sound of his own footsteps echoes throughout his Bauhaus-inspired Haven as he descends the staircase.
To come from a somewhat moldy London flat to a house nestled in the middle of some sprawling and luxurious acreage covered in trees is nothing short of an extreme contrast in living arrangements.
Gregory spends a few idle moments staring at the vastness of what is now his. And to think that he got it all for pound shop prices. He takes in a deep breath, holds it in, then forces it out.
He focuses his attention on a small pond, surrounded by trees and foliage. It's always comforting to gaze upon that particular body of water.
His train of thought is interrupted when Cassandra knocks on the glass doors. Gregory adjusts his collar, checks that his sleeves are buttoned, and opens the door with a neutral and measured expression on his face. His usually blank face betrays no emotion whatsoever.
Cassandra meekly waves at him, "Hiya, Greg. C--can we talk before you send me off to...err...do whatever I'm supposed to do tonight? I promise, it won't take long." Her demeanor is as jittery and nervous as can be expected of a Ghoul.
The Domitor nods her inside and gestures for her to take a seat in the living room. Cassandra tugs on her denim jacket and sits. She crosses her ankles and takes in a deep breath. Then she lets it out.
Gregory sits across her and inquires, "What seems to be the matter?"
"I--I want to talk to Sheriff Alice again. Sven...he's still bothering me! I--I can't get a proper bloody rest and..." She stammers and goes on and on. Gregory listens attentively.
By the time she's calmed down, he equably tells her, "Very well, then. Get the Lexus. I'll come with you to ensure that things don't get...messy. Sheriff Alice is not the kind of person who is easily swayed by tears or trifled with, for that matter."
Cassandra gulps and says nothing. She gets up from her seat, presses her glossy lips into a thin smile, and briskly walks towards the front door.
She looks over her over shoulder, "We'll talk about what I 'ave to do in the car, yeah? Also, I wanted to ask you something else. But that can wait till later." With that, she exits her immortal master's house.
Gregory sits in the back- he has it all to himself! Cassandra starts the car up and pulls out of the driveway. He says nothing. As for Cassandra, she doesn't say anything either- mostly because she's afraid of what the Tzimisce Sheriff might do or say to her tonight.
She holds tightly onto the steering wheel and shakily asks, "Y--you'll be with me, right?"
"Yes." His delivery- as always- is very dry and devoid of emotion.
While driving, Cass brings up, "I wanted to ask you about something. Before we get to talking about what it is that I 'ave to do for you and all. I promise not to take too long, yeah?"
It's nighttime in Gloughchester and the dense roads are slick with rainwater. It pours down from the heavens, sounding like a cacophony of miniature claps.
Gregory vacantly watches the city scenery roll by through his side window, "Hmm. What is it now?" He briefly looks over at his Ghoul, who continues to focus on the road ahead.
"Nicola Green's coming to this city. Soon. You know how much I--I adore her and--and her music. If--if there's anything you could do to help me see her live performance, I'd be eternally grateful, yeah? Think you could leverage some of your connections to get me- and maybe Amy- in? She's a huge fan of hers, too."
She quickly puts in, "You know me. I wouldn't normally ask something like this, but...this is Nicola Green we're talking about! Soho Starlet! I've never seen her performing live! Never!" The Ghoul bites her lower lip as she anxiously awaits her Domitor's response.
The faintest of smiles, barely noticeable, plays across the lips of the Toreador, "As a matter of fact, you shall be seeing her soon. And it is very much related to the...duties which you're undertaking on my behalf. I...needn't say any more lest you get far too excited. Do you understand?"
Cassandra fervently nods her head, the questions bubbling up in her mind and fighting for dominance. She decides against asking anything further and just leaves it at that.
The Sheriff of Gloughchester agrees to meet with the duo at the back of an upscale French restaurant in the posher part of the Domain. A cruiser with tinted windows is parked just across the street.
Steaming food vapors (and their associated scents) waft throughout the lush interior. A brick wall covered in ivy runs all along the restaurant's outer length, with the ivy covering parts of the restaurant's roof as well.
Sheriff Alice is seated in a cramped room, with her cold fingers clasped tightly together. Her suit and purple undershirt look as crisp as ever. She wordlessly gestures for the Ghoul and Domitor to take the 2 unoccupied chairs.
Cassandra does so. Gregory pulls the chair back, his long legs quickly settling in. He sits with his back straight, arms crossed, and feet firmly planted on the ground.
Cass fidgets with her thumbs and glances nervously between Sheriff Alice and Greg. The Toreador steeples his fingers together and gazes directly at the Sheriff.
The Tzimisce narrows an eye, "Out with it, Ghoul. You asked for this sit-down. Do you have any more vital information for me in regard to the Demon Worshipper? He is still here, of that much I can assure you. So. Out with it. Quickly."
Cassandra begins, "Err...I keep seeing him whenever I sleep- it doesn't matter whether it's day or night, I guess? He's not necessarily threatening me or anything. Just...pestering me. Scaring me, even. He also fancies you."
Sheriff Alice scoffs faintly, "He does now, does he? I don't fancy members of the Sabbat. Hmph. What else did that foul creature have to say? He didn't try anything...unsavory, did he?"
"No! No! Of course not! For a posh wanker, he seemingly knows his boundaries. Look, I'm not here to act as a--a sort of intermediary between you, alright? I just want him found so these bloody nightmares can stop!"
Greg chimes in, "Is there any meaningful progress on your part, Sheriff? Anything? Anywhere? Anyone?"
Sheriff Alice sighs a little, "Not quite. But I do have my people out looking for him--"
She quickly catches herself and glowers at the Ghoul, "--and we WILL find you, Sven." The Stoker stares daggers deep into the pit of Cassandra's very essence, her very being. It makes Cass uncomfortable, but she knows that she has no right to complain or question what is being done.
Sheriff Alice breaks the awkward silence by rolling her shoulders, "Keep me informed if things get out of hand, Ghoul. Is there anything else you'd like to share with me?"
Cass queries, "Greg told me that you're from Wales- he doesn't like Wales very much, I don't know why. So, what's it like? Can you--do you speak Welsh? Do you know how to say the name of that village with the longest name in the world? The one that ends in 'gogo­goch'?"
The Sheriff rolls her eyes, "I really shouldn't be wasting my time talking about this. But I was born in Llanfair PG. But I wasn't brought up there. Thankfully. As for speaking the language, no, I didn't really pick it up much. Maybe a few odd phrases here and there. But as time passed on, my mind made space for far more important things."
The Sheriff motions for someone to come over. She whispers something into their ear and sends them off.
Cassandra winces, "P--please don't threaten me with your gun again, yeah? P--please? It's not me fault that I got turned into some kind of bleeding conduit for the enemy!"
Sheriff Alice shakes her head, "Calm yourself, Ghoul. I concede that it's not your fault for being turned into a conduit. Now, if you'll excuse me. But I have other places to be tonight. You can see yourselves out. Or stay if exorbitantly priced French cuisine is what you seek."
She promptly gets up from her seat and strides out of the room. She's flanked by 2 bodyguards who trail her closely as she exits the building and gets into the cruiser.
Cass smirks, "You fancy a posh croissant, Greg?"
"No. You?"
"Nah. What--what are the prices like in this place?"
"Outrageous, I should imagine. Come along, now. There's no further reason for us to linger here."
"Yeah!"
A little later...
Cassandra is somewhat looking forward to tonight's set of tasks. Once Gregory finishes explaining what is to be done, Cass parks the Lexus and watches as Greg walks back to his Haven.
"Cor blimey, he doesn't go out that often, eh? Ah, well...time to go and meet these twin brothers. Children of Ennamaru."
The Toreador owes these Ennamari twin brothers a favor after having the misfortune to have been caught up in the middle of some kind of intramural scrimmage between the 2 of them.
Given Gregory's proximity to all things art, the favor involved him locating a rare piece from the 1st century. He never thought that his involvement in such matters would go any further than that.
Of course, given his extensive connections, locating the artwork in question was quite the trivial matter- a cakewalk! He really didn't even have to go to the trouble of doing it himself. Just a few phone calls and late-night chats on some shady chatrooms.
Now, the painting sits in his underground vault, waiting to be given off. But to who is the real question at hand.
As such, it now falls on Cassandra to determine who is the rightful owner of this painting, because both of those Ennamari are identical twins with equally valid claims over it.
And it doesn't help that their names are practically identical. Oddly enough, in all his interactions with them, Gregory has never seen both of these Tragedians in the same room at the same time.
1 brother or the other usually handwaves it off by explaining that the other twin is usually busy feeding, tossing dice, playing pool, conducting business meetings, and all manner of things which the other is often not privy to.
How odd. How quaint.
Cass walks up to the doorstep of the villa where 1 of the Ennamari twins resides. She waits for a few seconds before the door is opened by an old and rotund East Asian man with weathered features, tanned leathery skin, thinning gray hair, and sunken eyes.
His clothing is befitting of his status as a butler (of sorts), with a chained pocket watch and black dress shoes which look like they've seen better days (and nights).
The man gives her a low bow and offers his gloved hand. Cassandra gingerly shakes it and winces at how cold the old man's hand is. It feels like she's shaking hands with an icy corpse.
He speaks with quite an authoritative tone, "You are Gregory's Ghoul. Cassandra, I presume? He speaks of you often. This way."
Cassandra's eyebrows go up. She's never heard of Gregory speaking about her to other people, much less those outside of their immediate circle of acquaintances.
The interior of this villa is much like its exterior- drab, but in a Gothic, old-fashioned sort of way. The housekeeping staff is threadbare, reverential, and outwardly quite agreeable. The wallpapers are ornate and look like they belong in a museum rather than an actual house.
The light-brown marble flooring shines brightly under the radiance of the large chandeliers suspended from the ceiling. All of the chandeliers are electronic ones. The fireplace has been sealed off and replaced with a flat-screen TV mounted on the wall.
Alain (who sometimes goes by Alan) is seated in his personal office. He rises to his feet with an elaborate flair and shakes hands with Cassandra. His hands are icy cold, even more so than the butler's!
