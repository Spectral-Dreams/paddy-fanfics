The tall man holds up his finger, revealing a few burns and scars on his hand, "I know your procedures and protocols all too well. But your efforts are wasted here. They are long gone, these...savage creatures. This was a message that they intended to send. They don't want us here."
The operative wonders, "And who exactly is 'we', sir?"
"That, my boy, is classified. Far beyond what a lowly Gen4 operative like yourself would be privy to."
The operative nods and turns away.
The man paces a tight circle around the body, stroking his shaved chin and mumbling to himself. He shakes his head and mutters, "We certainly have our work cut out for us, don't we?"
He heads deeper into the Elbit facility and takes a very important phone call from someone high up in his particular Org. He straightens his posture and licks his thin lips when the voice on the other line asks him about the Birmingham situation.
A woman's stern and somewhat metallic voice demands, "Report, Captain Meadows?"
"Situation is under control, ma'am. Blank bodies left us a little present like a cat leaving a dead and bloodied rat at the doorstep."
"You've lost your only way into the inner circle of the vampiric threat. Was it wise bringing somebody from Israeli intelligence into this? He was, in all likelihood, compromised. It's no wonder that he was dealt with in such a...heinous manner."
Captain Mark Meadows nods solemnly, "Yes, ma'am. That would appear to be the case. What are your instructions, ma'am?"
"Operation Brightburn must proceed at all costs- it is primarily your mission, after all, Captain Meadows. Don't fail us. Branch 16 is counting on you. We are counting on you. Do not fail us. Neutralize the blank body threat by any means necessary. If you have need for more resources, you need only ask."
"Yes, ma'am. As a matter of fact, I think that our presence here in Birmingham has been compromised. I'm not quite ready to risk an all-out war with these creatures."
"What do you intend to do, then? Hmm?" The impatience in her voice grows.
He replies, "I am requesting permission to set up shop in the city of Gloughchester, ma'am. The blank bodies over there have been getting rather sloppy in hiding themselves. And my...source is never wrong about such things."
"What of Birmingham? Is it a lost cause, then?"
"No, ma'am. Not--not necessarily. We will continue to monitor the blank bodies in Birmingham, but I fear that we shall get nowhere if we keep pushing in that direction. Without a man on the inside, we are essentially flying blind."
The lady can be heard shuffling a few papers on her desk and typing on her mechanical keyboard.
She hums, "Permission granted. As a matter of fact...we already have a building that shall serve our purposes well. A very fitting location, indeed. I will send you the address and necessary passcodes as soon as I can."
Even though no one can see him in this dimly lit room, Captain Mark bows his head a little, "That would be most helpful, ma'am. Can we continue to count on your support in other matters, as well? My...contact in Gloughchester still remains aloof about how he is able to acquire such vast information on the blank body threat."
She dryly intones, "Your secretive contact is of no concern to us. The results that he has delivered, on the other hand, are what really matter. Keep your pet informant happy and fed, Captain Meadows. Given the nature of our work, we can't afford to be picky."
Captain Mark firmly nods, "Yes, ma'am. We--we certainly cannot. And what of the...others who we routinely work with? Far be it from me to overstep clearly defined boundaries, ma'am, but--but I can't help but wonder..."
"Your skepticism regarding their involvement is well-founded, Captain Meadows. And it is hardly a secret amongst Branch 16's top brass. Nevertheless, they are an asset that we must make the most of in our operations against the blank bodies."
Captain Mark mumbles, "The lesser of 2 evils. I still do not trust them, ma'am. Nobody sh--should have such powers over--over reality itself!" He almost screams out that last part.
Her voice is cold and unfeeling as she flatly states, "The blank bodies are even worse, Captain Meadows. Focus on them. Just be glad that we're getting...outside help. Even if the ones offering a helping hand aren't exactly what we'd define as 'conventional'."
Captain Mark sighs and says, "Very well, ma'am. I shall keep you fully updated on the developing situation. Is--is the Gloughchester facility...full of them, ma'am?"
"Find out for yourself, Captain Meadows. Good day and godspeed."
Captain Mark lifts his chin and firmly declares, "God save the Queen!"
"God save the Queen," the woman on the other end plainly repeats before hanging up.
Captain Mark Meadows puts his phone away and takes a few moments to gather his thoughts. His eyes are heavy with weariness, yet there is also an unwavering sense of conviction and purpose that radiates from him.
He straightens his posture and walks off to attend to the matters that only a man of his stature and reputation can properly deal with.
