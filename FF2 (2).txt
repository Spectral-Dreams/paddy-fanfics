A new day dawns over Gloughchester. Birds sing their morning songs. The sky is a clear, azure blue with a few clouds in sight.
Cassandra takes a few moments to collect herself before hopping off the bed and going about her daily routine. Disorientation still lingers in her head for a bit but she's able to recover from last night's dreamscape accosting.
She clutches her head and stares at herself in the mirror, "Cor blimey. Am I a--a sight for sore eyes, yeah."
The still-frightened Ghoul squeezes out some toothpaste onto her toothbrush and sets about cleaning her teeth. She gargles some salt water, spits it out, and takes a few more moments to stare at the messy-haired lass looking back at her from the other side of the mirror.
Cass trills her lips and sighs, "Nothing I can't handle. Nothing I can't handle."
After bathing, she steps out of the tub, sopping wet. She slips and almost falls backwards into the basin but manages to catch herself just in time. She lets out a tired huff.
"Bloody 'ell!"
The remainder of the day passes by in a blur. Cassandra swings by the ASDA supermarket, buys some things so she can make a nice home-cooked lunch, and then heads back to her cottage to cook the food up.
She shoots a few texts to Amy while the chopped vegetables are boiling in a pot. Amy texts back and the both of them get to chatting. Time flies by. Cassandra rushes around the kitchen, stirring something else while also checking to see if the food's done yet. The delicious smell fills the air.
Cassandra inhales a lungful of it and sighs contently, "Mm-mmm. Smells bloody scrumptious, innit?"
After taking her lunch, she puts the leftovers in some plastic containers and keeps them in the fridge. From there, she ambles around for a few minutes. By 4 in the afternoon, she decides to take a light nap.
However, an uneasy feeling keeps her awake. It nags at her, asking if Sven will pop out of nowhere again. She glances at the time on her Blackberry phone. The Ghoul lets out a shaky sigh. Who will she tell about this? And she has to tell somebody!
She gulps and feels her stomach churning a bit. Cass takes a deep breath and then exhales.
By the time sleep takes a hold of her, she elects to tell Gregory and Sheriff Alice about this. The former will, hopefully, be understanding. The latter, on the other hand, might not be.
Cassandra soon eases into a restful slumber. The sound of birds chirping fills her ears. The gentle pitter-patter of rain, the soothing smell of the damp earth and grass, and the calming sound of the breeze caressing the trees and blowing through her window brings a certain relief to the napping retainer.
Oh, dear.
Cass is pulled back into the same place, same thing. Sven spreads his arms to either side of his muscular bod and cries out, "Welcome back, Cassie! So, I've been doing some non-invasive prying of your...mind, memories, and personality. You're a pretty fuckin' interesting chick. That's a compliment!"

Cassandra is jolted awake once more. The winds outside, the sounds of nature, and the smells permeate her cottage. She wheezes, her chest rising and falling at a quicker pace. She hugs her knees tightly, her eyes darting around the room.
Once she has calmed down, she scrambles through her wardrobe for an outfit. For tonight, it's some Burberry slacks, a plain white blouse, and some rather stylish white loafers. She adds some bangles and jingling earrings on for good measure. A necklace featuring a red gemstone decorates her neck.
Cassandra downs a whole glass of water, wipes her mouth with the back of her hand, and proceeds to do her makeup. Simple pound shop lip gloss and some moisturizer. A touch of green rouge, nothing too fancy.
A green headband secures the top of her hair. She sprays some rose-scented perfumes that Amy had bought for her. Cassandra steps away from the mirror and examines herself.
She struggles to muster a smile. She simply pats the sides of her legs and sighs feebly.
Oddly enough, the Aapilu didn't haunt Gregory prior to his awakening from the day-sleep. It's worse, actually. The creature of the Vitae merely lingers in the darker corners of the Toreador's Haven, watching him in a literal, crawling manner. Cautiously. The thing is slinking around like a patch of icky darkness.
It tilts its head and crouches while Gregory combs his hair. His cold hands are trembling a little. He forces a deep breath to calm himself. The reflection in his mirror reveals a nervous-looking Domitor.

Cassandra squints but can't quite make out what her Domitor's reading. She clears her throat and the other Kindred turns another page, still not looking up.
"Greg..."

Cassandra's eyebrows shoot up as she steps into the cool basement vault. Scores upon scores of art pieces, sculptures, vases, figurines, and art-adjacent objects are neatly organized against the walls.
Every time she enters in here, she is hit by this feeling of awe that hasn't worn off since the first time. It's like a mini art gallery but super awesome. But there is a bit of guilt lurking deep inside her heart. Why are these items just cooped up here in a basement?
Moreover, many of these pieces weren't exactly acquired through wholly ethical means. And a lot of them do actually belong in a public museum for all to see. Not locked up, hidden away, and kept a secret from the general populace.
Gregory has grown rather indifferent over the long years in dealing with, trafficking, and moving such objects of immense value (NOT strictly monetary). One does what one must to survive, even as an immortal predator of the nocturnal hours.
With purpose punctuating his each and every step, Gregory gently walks over to a golden bust and adjusts its position. He runs his pale, long fingers along the smooth texture of the object.
His dead blue eyes linger over the way it shines under the stark white lights above. His lower lip quivers when the world around him goes blurry. His hearing gets a tad muffled.
All he can focus on- at this moment- is the sheen of the golden artifact. The exquisite details. Every single crevice and nook. The impressive manner in which the imperfections are almost entirely unnoticeable.
That's the Toreador Clan Compulsion playing up. Their Obsession renders them unable to take their attention from whatever (or whoever) enraptures them at the moment. And, admittedly, this particular piece of antiquity is quite the stunner!
Cassandra folds her arms and silently watches along; by now, she has gotten quite used to her Domitor's foibles. She tries to follow his gaze so that she too may be enthralled by whatever's gotten her master all stirred up.
Gregory eventually blinks and regains control of his faculties. Yet again, he reaches out and adjusts the position of the bust. He clicks his tongue in mild annoyance, pulls out his large handkerchief, and gingerly wipes the object's surface clean.
He does that 1 more time before moving on, satisfied with the current placement of the golden bust.
Cassandra stifles a chuckle and casually comments, "Wanna talk about it?"
"I--I'm fine, thank you." He turns his head away and gestures to a sizeable wooden crate in the corner of the vault. He lifts the lid and picks up a chunky-looking scarab that's about the size of a child's clenched fist. It's smooth all over. The outer layer feels like it's been polished by expert hands.
Cass inquires, "What exactly is that?"
Gregory inspects it carefully, "It is rumored that the Blood of Set the Antediluvian is held within this particular artifact. Many have fruitlessly attempted to break through the shell in hopes of acquiring such an elusive prize. None have succeeded. I have personally witnessed someone attempting to utilize a diamond drill on this."
He brings it closer to Cassandra's face and traces the scratches that the aforementioned activity left behind, "All they did was mar the beauty of it. Tch. Tasteless savages. They wanted to blow it open, but I managed to convince them not to."
Cassandra pulls back and purses her lips, "Right. What else is in 'ere?" She leans closer to the box and noses about.
The Toreador nods, "Aside from an assortment of pieces acquired over the years, the rest are trinkets. There's even an ancient tome on hieroglyphics. I secured it at an auction for £10,000. And this was long before we even met."
Cassandra exclaims, "Cor blimey! £10,000?! What's that in today's quid?"
"A rather sizable amount, I should imagine."
"Yeah, probably enough to feed a family for a--a few months, maybe?"
"Hm. Yes. Perhaps. Now, is your Lexus clean?"

If a Ghoul like you tried to fire it, not even the A&E would be able to bring you back. And mortals would likely lose a vital body part owing to the recoil alone."
