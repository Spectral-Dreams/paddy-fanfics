Chapter 40: Of Blade And Serpent (Reprise)
The sun now reigns mightily in the sky. Large swathes of this Middle Eastern metropolis are coated in an almost golden-colored haze. The rays of the sun beat down on the streets and sidewalks, which are filled with busy people going to and fro.
Cars and other vehicles zoom and swoosh about the place, taking many different routes through the city's winding roads. Skyscrapers and tall buildings are visible in the near distance.
People of various professions go about their day, working or keenly clock-watching. The concept of the "golden handcuffs" is very much applicable to a lot of folks in this gilded cage of a city.
Deferred incentives are provided (or merely promised) to employees to encourage them to stay with their company for the longest term. Bonuses, vacay houses, stock options, stuff like that.
One can effectively discourage one's staff from leaving for another company or a different position or what have you. Certain people might see the benefits from such arrangements, but those who are on the lower end of the wage scale are left wondering just what the hell they're gonna get out of all of this.
Life in this Domain (and neighboring Dubai) proceeds along the usual lines. Tourism sees a slight drop but is bound to pick up in the coming weeks. The relentless summer heat, on the other hand, is not gonna be going anywhere anytime soon.
When the scorching sun says bye-bye (for the time being, at least), the vampires in this part of the globe begin to stir awake. Some decide to "sleep in" for just a little longer, while others are already up and about, ready to stalk the night and hunt for their prey...or go about doing other things.
Doon in the wee auld Nossie Warrens...
Caley, that Tremere from Abu Dhabi, is walking behind a group of Nossies. The Usurper has their nose wrinkled in disgust. They shake their head and look at the other Kindred accompanying them.
Most of them are bent over and hobbling along a path that they had committed to memory. They speak in low and harsh tones to each other. Caley is dressed in maroon robes that have been slightly dirtied around the edges.
Regardless, Caley walks on with some semblance of dignity. It isn't long before they're face to face with Tommy himself. The Nosferatu Primogen has his trademark mask affixed to his face. He's inspecting a coin on his somewhat slimy hand.
The 2 make eye contact, and Caley lets out a sigh of relief, "Thomas Bollard...or Tommy as so many so affectionately call you. You understand why I'm here, correct?"
Tommy stares at the robed Usurper, "Aye. I'm surprised, honestly."
"Hmph. Well, don't be. Just know that Prince Helmut Strauss doesn't appreciate...trespassers in what is rightfully his Domain. Our sewer systems are also under the blanket of our unique protection."
Tommy snickers, "Naw need tae be so boastful aboot it. I was only curious and wanted tae know more. My scouts dinnae exactly have a chance tae properly explore, you know? And thank you for no destroying them."
Caley adjusts the loose sleeves of their robe, "The pleasure is all ours. Don't do it again. This time, we return them to you unharmed. The next time however..." they let that ominously hang in the dense and musty air.
Tommy has his deformed hands out in a sort of placating gesture, "Awright, awright. Nae need for any threats and shite like that. We can behave ourselves. But I had my reasons for sending those wee scouts all the way to Abu Dhabi."
"I would love to hear those reasons."
"I'm sure yous would, pal. 'Mon, let's have ourselves a seat and discuss things, aye? Maybe a wee snack too?"
Caley takes a seat on a tatty office chair that someone wheels in for them. However, they decline the other offer, "I shall feed much later. But thank you for being considerate enough, if nothing else."
The Tremere wiggles around until they're comfortable. Tommy gets a rugged wooden chair and has a seat. He folds 1 leg over the other and prompts, "So? Let's be having it."
Caley begins, "I would like to know why you, of all people, would attempt to send a band of scouts to another Domain. Unannounced, I might add. Was this simply a lapse in judgment on your part or do you have some other ulterior motive?"
Tommy lets out a low laugh, "You have me all figured out, do you now? I'll admit, sending my wee auld Nossies into your Domain and being all secretive about it was no a good idea. Well, not entirely. But...it's done and over with. No harm, no foul, eh?"
Caley shrugs, "I suppose so. Don't let it happen again. We take the protection of our Domain VERY seriously. As such, it'd be a damn pitiful shame if any of you Sewer Rats got ensnared. You understand where we're coming from, yes?"
"Aye. I've heard the stories...and the rumors, too."
The Tremere regards the Nossie with narrowed eyes, "No need to appear so gloomy. It's how we've survived- and thrived- for so long. Unlike this mess of a leaderless Domain. But I digress; I'm not here to discuss this city's politics, I'm here to get to the bottom of the thought process behind you sending those scouts."
Tommy taps his mask, "It's like this, pal- I wanted tae see what kind of place you have. I wanted tae get a feel for things. See how spacious your underground areas were and shite like that."
"The people who often feel around for those sorts of things usually have ill intent. And yet, I don't...sense any ill intent from you, Mr. Bollard. So, what exactly were you looking for?"
"It's complicated."
Caley laughs faintly, "Isn't it always. That's about half of my public Facebook profile. Heh. Uh...OK. Never mind. So, you're Scottish, huh? You keep in touch with any Kindred back in the...err...old country?"
The Nossie answers, "Our SchreckNet's still knackered. But we're slowly putting the scattered pieces back together. It won't be done soon, it's gonnae take time, but we're making steady progress. Why do you ask?"
The Usurper takes on a more solemn tone, "The Edinburgh Chantry was recently attacked. It could've been the Second Inquisition for all we know. Prince Helmut is, understandably, worried about this recent and troubling turn of events. He wants to know if there's any info that you people can offer him."
Tommy shakes his head, "I'm sorry, I've not heard anything of that sort. But I'll keep me eyes out for anything that'll be of use to yous, hm?"
Caley goes on to state, "We've also lost all contact with a few other Chantries- Rabat, Thessaloniki, Reykjavík, the list goes on and on. They're falling like dominoes and--and we don't know why." The frustration in their tone is quite apparent.
The Crawler queries, "I know how much yous Tremere value loyalty and structure and all of that. But what I still don't know is why these Chantries are so important tae you lot."
Caley scratches their lower lip, "It's a part and parcel of our Blood, of our Clan's unique identity. No offense, but an outsider like you just wouldn't understand, let alone appreciate, the nuances involved."
"Hmph. Fine. Nae bother. Now, back on the topic at hand..."
"Yes, indeed."
Tommy forces a few breaths through his shriveled-up lungs. He clasps his cold hands together, "I wanted tae know."
Caley is not terribly impressed. They perch an eyebrow, "Know what? Yes, it's admirable- our respective Clans are the only ones who pursue knowledge. Although our paths and approaches may differ, we still strive to find the same sort of answers. The pursuit of knowledge and knowledge alone is a worthy endeavor in and of itself."
The Nossie solemnly nods, "Aye. It sure is, pal. It sure is."
"So, what the fuck were you doing in our territory, huh?"
"I--we just had tae give it a wee swatch."
"Uh...dial the slang down a few notches, would ya?"
Tommy huffs a grating sigh, "I was curious tae see how we'd...fit in, yous might say."
"'Fit in'? We really do not appreciate trespassers, even if they come to us wanting to hold hands and sing kumbaya. You should've at least sent a message to us prior to your little excursion."
"Aye, true. My mistake. I concede that I--I could've gone about it in a more...sensible way, but I'm desperate here, pal! I've no got that many options."
Caley scoffs, "Is that so? Until that Setite announced his claims of Praxis over Dubai and Al-Kushraf, you people have been experiencing a free ride- no Sheriff AND no Prince. You have quite a lot of options...Tommy."
The Nosferatu nods pensively, "It was fun while it lasted. How close are you tae the Prince, the big cheese himself?"
"I wouldn't call Prince Helmut that to his face, but we speak often. Why?"
"Is Abu Dhabi stable?"
Caley snickers, "Depends on your definition of 'stable'. Knowing what you're accustomed to over here in Al-Kushraf, I'd say that Abu Dhabi would likely be a straitjacket in comparison. Why?"
Tommy wistfully remarks, "We had a nice thing going on here. But I don't know anymore. W--was it all a massive farce? Even now, I cannae tell."
Caley tells him, "The Pyramid is about stability and order above all else. I am starting to get an impression of what you're trying to say. And if I'm correct, then it's best if we not only continue this conversation elsewhere but also get more people involved, hm?"
Tommy sits up straighter, "And keep it all under wraps. You're a smart one, aren't you, pal?"
"I know that a Nosferatu's ears aren't often for...show. And I'm well-versed on every Clan's Banes and Compulsions. Do you have a space where we can talk, preferably one that is AWAY from prying eyes...and ears."
Tommy rises up from his seat and beckons for the Tremere to follow him, "Aye. 'Mon! I got just the wee place, pal."
Caley dusts some of the dirt from off of their flowing robe and follows the Nosferatu closely. On the way, many Nossies stop and stare at Caley's heterochromatic eyes. They whisper and point and jab their fingers at the Usurper's face.
The Tremere keeps their head high and gaze straight ahead. Their mind is firmly set on what shall indubitably be an interesting conversation!
A little later, at Paul's Haven...
The Toreador Harpy has been fielding calls left, right, and center. From the time he awoke till now, it's been non-stop. His voicemail is full- the thing beeps repeatedly as new voicemails are added to the queue.
Out of sheer frustration, Paul pulls the landline cord out and grunts while turning off his phone. The Toreador by the name of Gorman is seated in the living room. He looks dressed to the nines.
Paul throws himself on the couch with a heaving sigh. Gorman's head perks up.
"What's the matter, hon?"
Paul rubs his face, "Uggggggh. It's pandemonium, Gorman...people wanna be sure that Sebastian is indeed...y'know?"
Gorman faintly chuckles, "I can only begin to imagine. And we both know why they're so keen on confirming that Seb's no longer an inhabitant of our wonderfully dark world, hmm?"
"Mm-hmm. I heard you and Lester got into some kind of shouting match over who's poised to take Sebastian's place?"
Gorman waves his hand in a dismissive flourish, "Raising one's voice a few octaves soon after waking up from one's beauty sleep hardly qualifies as a shouting match, darling. It was simply a misunderstanding in regard to who ought to succeed Sebastian...Gorsky. Ugh. His name alone gives me the bad tingles."
Paul snickers, "Uh-huh. There's still a massive Primogen-shaped hole that needs filling. And soon. With the Archons coming by, we need to get our damn house in order. Now more so than ever." There is a hint of frustration written all over his pale face.
Gorman reminds his fellow Artisan, "Honey, up until a few nights ago, you might as well have called this Domain an Anarch Barony...owing to the fact that--"
Paul cuts him off, "Yeah, yeah, I--I know. Neither Prince nor Sheriff. To be honest with ya, Seth's whole claiming of Praxis caught me off-guard."
Gorman leans forward and inquires, "You do still plan on being our Harpy, yes? I couldn't imagine anyone else in that particular role. That and the fact that you've become a repository for many, many secrets..."
Paul sighs and gazes at his green socks.
Gorman goes on, "Secrets that our kind are prepared to go great lengths to protect. It's not something that you can walk away from, dear."
Paul mutters, "Maybe that's exactly what I should do. And this whole goddamn Blood Hunt business...it's getting out of hand."
"Ah, yes. That. For what it's worth, I didn't participate in such a distasteful...process. I can't speak for the rest of my Blood, but my conscience is cleaner than a church grandma's. Heh."
Paul briefly laughs at that remark, "It's good to know that there's still some semblance of--of decency in our Clan. Did you hear anything from Sebastian's Ghoul?"
"As a matter of fact, yes. She's treating herself to a spa day...that may as well drag on for a whole lot longer. She wasn't too distraught by her Domitor's sudden...departure; she was simply more concerned about where- and from who- she'll get her dosages of vitamin B, if you catch my drift."
Paul laughs a little louder this time, "Wow. That's pretty much on point for her, I guess."
"Was it as gruesome as they say?"
"Hmm?"
"People who were there last night claim that Seb's screams could be heard all throughout the building- and some of them couldn't even take their eyes off of the raging fires."
Paul shrugs, "You know as much as I know about last night. Svetlana had her reasons for--for destroying him, though. Besides, the whole damn Blood Hunt is--is--it's fucking unjustifiable!"
Gorman fiddles with his shirt's collar, "I suppose that it is. Were you aware that the late Mr. Gorsky had a Childe? He could have had Childer for all I know, but I can confirm for a fact that he did indeed Sire someone many years ago."
Paul pulls up his socks and nods, "Yeah. Some chick in uh..."
Gorman interjects, "Las Estrellas, US of A. Vegas, but in the Southwest...and run by Anarchs. Ugh. So gauche. And here's the kicker: she was black. And we both know how Seb G hated anyone who so much as had even a trace of melanin in their skin. It would've been unheard of for him to Embrace a black woman, but he did it."
Paul puts in, "It was a spur of the moment thingy, right? He--he still resented her for some reason or the other."
Gorman dryly states, "It was the usual reasons. The usual eccentric Seb Gorsky nonsense. Paul, do you know how many times I had to suffer that man doing his face-reading nonsense on me? For Caine's sake, the man extolled the virtues of fucking phrenology in--in polite company!"
Paul sighs heavily, "Yep. He's not gonna be terribly missed, is he?"
"Nope."
"Well, wherever his Childe- or Childer- may be, I wish 'em well."
"Hear, hear."
The Harpy puts on his watch and stares at its glass face for a second longer, "Did they ever keep in touch? Or attempt to?"
"Not that I can recall. Every time the departed mentioned his Childe, he usually followed it up with some impolite slur of the racial variety. The man was truly a piece of work."
Paul's eyebrows shoot up, "Heh. He sure was. He sure was. Anything else on your mind, Gorman?"
The other Toreador slides off his seat a little, "As a matter of fact, yes. Have you heard about that business with the Toreadors of Gloughchester, England? Positively nasty stuff, if even a quarter of the hearsay is to actually be believed."
Paul murmurs, "I have enough on my plate with the Toreadors of Al-Kushraf, UAE. Why, what's going on back there? I--I mean, I heard SOME stuff, but I haven't been able to independently confirm any of it."
Gorman mock gasps, "And here I thought that our beloved Harpy would be on top of things. They're saying a Ghoul is quite mixed up in all of it; a Ghoul with a pretty name that I just can't seem to recall at the moment. You'll have to forgive me, I'm afraid. I do tend to have a terrible memory for anything not immediately relevant."
"It's no worry. Where there's a Ghoul, there's a Domitor. Greg...something, if I'm not mistaken."
"Hmm. I suppose so. You know, hon, I do wonder what Seb's epitaph might say. Now, before you get all pedantic with me, just...hypothesize with me. For but a fleeting moment, if you'd be so kind, hmm?"
Paul flatly says, "'Karl Lagerfeld wannabe'. Everyone called him that- right behind his back or to his goddamn smug face. Oh, and he also stole most of his fashion designs from kids on the Internet. Yeah, he was a real bastard. Ain't no use in sugarcoating that."
Gorman shakes his head from side to side, "My, my, my. Mr. Gorsky is the gift that keeps on giving. His theft is truly news to me. Shocking news, I might add. As much as I despised his company, I was under the firm impression that his designs were o--original and--and classy."
"Hmm. Don't share this with anyone but guess who 1 of the dispatched Archons will be. Go on. Take your wildest guess."
Gorman gasps, "Well, that's...a coincidence. Is it...him?"
Paul's soft smile and subtle nod say it all.
Gorman adjusts his hair a bit, "Well, I suppose I could...err...drop by and say a--a quick hello. By the way, Paul...your fit just isn't doing it for me. The blue suit, the pinned rose, and...the green socks. Ugh."
Paul weakly chuckles and queries, "What do you suggest, Queer Eye?"
Gorman politely responds, "Given recent events, go in for something along the lines of gloomy chic- dark blacks, a tie that isn't dreadfully monochromatic, and a solemn look that truly communicates to the outside world how...distraught we're all feeling right now."
Paul gestures to Gorman's laidback attire, "Says the guy who looks like he's dressed to go to a party."
Gorman admits, "Well, actually, yes. Within certain circles, Sebastian's Final Death is a moment to celebrate. We're breaking out the Pinot O-negatives and Cabernet Reds, Thaumaturgically charged...or so I'm told. Our party shall soon evolve into a full-on orgy, as these things usually do."
He raises his chin and adds, "We might romp about before that. As such, I'm dressed accordingly. But you, darling, need to present yourself with the dignity and decorum expected from your role as a Harpy...who just so happens to be a Toreador."
Paul stands up and nods, "I'll get changed. I wouldn't wanna keep you from your uh...shindig. Just don't get too carried away, right?"
Gorman puts his hand on his chest, "Oh, of course. If these decades have taught me anything, it's that it's better to get to the point before the crowd starts getting bored...oh, and moderation, too. Excess was never really my style. Too much waste."
Paul tells his fellow Artisan, "Yeah, you've got a pretty chill vibe, huh?"
"With a few mild tweaks, the same could be said for you, hon. Listen, I have to go and help set things up. I have a little twist on a game of musical chairs for the Ghouls who'll be in attendance. Nothing morbid or tasteless, I assure you."
Paul lightly smiles and says, "Cool. I'll keep you in the loop and let you know if anything valuable comes up."
Gorman sashays to the front door, "And I'll reciprocate, as I always do, darling. Ta-ta!" he twirls his fingers, winks, and walks out of the Harpy's Haven.
Paul closes the door and stares at it. He laughs to himself and goes to get changed, deciding to take Gorman's fashion advice to heart. He also combs his hair back instead of leaving it all tousled and messy.
Back at Jean-Luc's villa...
The Clanless vampire is busy hanging some clothing out to dry. He shakes them out with a great deal of force before attaching them to the racks that he's put up outside. He clips them with a few large clothing pegs and is done in a few minutes.
Svetlana offers to help but he firmly declines. Even now, he remains so distant and aloof. Once he gets ready, he and his Assamite guest go out in search of a few warm necks to sink their respective fangs into.
The Caitiff parks his IROC-Z near a shuttered shop. Their surroundings appear quite deserted. The warm and arid air wafts around the 2 vampires. Svetlana's nostrils are stung by a burning smell of some kind.
She follows Jean-Luc down a deserted alley, constantly looking over her shoulder to ensure that there are no nasty surprises in any way, shape, or form.
Jean-Luc turns the corner and nods over to a group of people huddled around a burning barrel; thankfully, the fire is small and not strong enough to trigger either vampire's Rötschreck.
The Caitiff says, "Keep close and be mindful, Assamite."
She solemnly nods, "Da. I would tell to you the same thing."
He briefly lets out a tiny, imperceptible chuckle while taking a step closer to the group. Svetlana hangs back and hopes to isolate some hapless wanderer. As Jean-Luc heads for the group, someone just so happens to begin walking away.
Svetlana slowly pads over to the man and grabs him from behind. She covers his mouth and bites into his neck. Viciously. The middle-aged man's flailing arms soon go limp. He lets out a slight moan.
The Assamite licks the wounds on his neck closed and checks for a pulse- faint but still there, thank the Antediluvian. With a slight look of guilt on her place face, she ensures that her victim is at least lying in a comfortable position.
Svetlana sighs and takes her place next to Jean-Luc. He wipes some blood away from his mouth with the back of his hand, grunts, and puts his helmet back on. Without saying another word, they both depart from the scene and head straight for their destination.
It might be risky, but Miss Bobrov wants to go back to her Haven. There's something of importance which she needs to retrieve. After losing her ceramic dagger, she's down a weapon. And there's only so much that can be achieved with a single obsidian dagger...
She utilizes her Obfuscate Discipline to return to her apartment. Once back inside, she shimmers back into view and forces a sigh of relief. She takes a look around her place and takes a short moment to gather her composure.
(It has NOT been easy for the Assamite Neonate- this is NOT how she envisioned things to go. However, she is no fool. It's still not too late to turn the tide and make things right.)
She kneels down and opens the guitar case where her Sire's katana lays. With a trembling hand, she takes the sheathed weapon and inspects it from all angles. It feels so light- so oddly familiar- in her grasp.
Svetlana twirls the sword around a few times. She slashes the empty air and tries to get a feel for John's weapon of choice. If she's gonna take the fight to those who lied to and betrayed everyone, then she's gonna need something capable of taking heads clean off with a single swipe.
To her, it still feels weird to hold it normally, so she opts to hold it reverse-grip style. Now, THAT's more like it! The Assamite focuses and goes through a few of John's familiar stances and footwork.
(The one's which she can bring to immediate remembrance, anyway).
She recalls how her Sire would be like some kind of intense fitness trainer or instructor, always clapping his hands and counting out the steps to be performed. He was big on having the body react and move as naturally as possible.
"Move like water, Svetlana...move like water..." John's words echo in her mind. He seems so close to her- so near- yet so far away.
She wraps the sword in a large towel and goes about the rest of her business before heading back out to the parked IROC-Z. Since she lacks a sheath of any kind, she brings the concealed katana along.
She places the blade on her lap. Jean-Luc stares at the rearview mirror.
Svetlana anticipates his query and answers, "Is extra weapon. I...need this. I need it if I am going to have any chance of Judging the unjust and doing what is necessary."
Jean-Luc drives on and dryly mutters, "Of course."
While driving, Svetlana can feel the Celerity within her stirring. She winces and wiggles around on her seat.
Jean-Luc inquires, "What are you doing, Assamite?"
She shakes her head and assures him that it's nothing to be worried about. Yet, the Caitiff can't help but notice a very slight yet notable tremble in her voice.
Even now, she's still afraid of what might be waiting for her around the corner. Without the guidance of her Sire, she feels a little bit lost and unsure of many things.
And the fact that she is literally the last Banu Haqim in this entire city does very little to alleviate the pressure and stress. Even with John's sword by her side, there is no getting around the fact that she is going up against a substantial force who are armed, dangerous, brutal, and determined to get the job done.
All things considered, this is gonna be a helluva battle!
"1 battle after another," Svetlana softly murmurs to herself.
While driving, an Emirati police officer holds out his hand and beckons for the vehicle to stop. Jean-Luc pulls the car over to the side of the road. Svetlana shakily gasps and involuntarily holds onto the katana's hilt. The Assamite's expression soon shifts from frightened to fiercely determined.
She's just about ready to flash out of the car and end this fucking cop if need be.
Jean-Luc keeps his helmet on. He looks over his shoulder and shakes his head, "Let me handle this."
Svetlana still keeps her fingers firmly wrapped around the katana's hilt. The officer approaches the car and Jean-Luc rolls down his window.
He looks to be in his 40s. His beard is short and decently trimmed. His skin is on the darker side. He speaks with a heavy accent when he greets, "As-salamu alaykum (AR: Peace be upon you)."
Jean-Luc quickly and curtly responds, "Wa alaykumu s-salam (AR: And upon you be peace). What do want?"
The officer looks over his shoulders and lowers his voice, "I have a message for...her. I--I come in peace."
Svetlana is Obfuscated, but she knows that this mortal cop is talking about her. Jean-Luc remains composed, "I don't know what you speak about."
With a shaking hand, the officer produces a slip of paper, "Please, habibi. M--make sure that she gets this. Please." There's fear in his eyes. His voice oozes desperation and he seems to be doing his best to maintain a cool demeanor.
The Caitiff snatches the piece of paper and unfolds it. An address is scrawled onto it. The cop simply says, "I am simply the messenger here, habibi. You--you may go. Please ensure that...she gets that. Soon, please. Thank you. Be well."
Jean-Luc fires up the IROC-Z and speeds away. The paper is wedged between his forefinger and middle finger. Svetlana takes it and reads through it. She recognizes the handwriting all too well.
The Assamite Neonate lets out a small growl.
Jean-Luc faintly chuckles, "It is a trap of some kind, isn't it?"
Svetlana mulls a few things over in her head. Her green eyes drift to the katana wrapped in the towel- could this be an opportunity to finally end this once and for all?
In addition to the address, Seth's handwriting further mentions: "Only you, no one else."
Svetlana asks the race driver, "Do you know where this place is?"
"Yes. The Tannous Enterprises building. Not many in city know it, but I do. Raced by it many times. Undergoing expansions and renovations. You're not actually planning on going? It--it's a trap!"
With determination lacing her tone, she tells him, "I have to end it. This could be my opportunity to do so. Only I am to go. Alone. It is safe to assume that even Seth will be there...alone."
Jean-Luc queries, "Can you trust anything that man says?"
"Nyet, you cannot. That is the point. Look, Jean-Luc, you have already done enough for me. T--thank you. This is something that I must do alone. Simply drop me off and let me do the rest. I--I appreciate everything that you have done for me...truly, I do."
"I know. And I've certainly gotten into enough trouble by helping you- I will have to go and deal with the consequences- whatever those may be."
Svetlana gives him a look of sympathy.
The Unbound vampire shakes his head from side to side and sighs, "Are you sure that you can manage? And what if it IS an ambush, hmm?"
Svetlana affectionately stares at the sword that used to belong to her Sire, "Then I will...fight, like I've always done, like I--I always will. I will fight until the bitter end if--if I have to."
Jean-Luc merely nods, "Ah, I see. You truly are Banu Haqim."
The drive goes by in a blur of bright, flashing lights. After 2 whole hours, the IROC-Z rolls up to the front of the sprawling tower of Tannous Enterprises. Scaffolding surrounds the under-renovation structure. Building materials and heavy machinery are scattered about.
Nobody seems to be around. It's eerily empty. A few insects can be heard buzzing in the background.
Svetlana looks around and says, "Thank you for driving me here. I...go now."
Before she steps out, Jean-Luc turns over and puts his hand on her knee. This elicits a slight yelp of surprise from the Assamite. She tries to jerk away.
Jean-Luc pulls up his visor and looks into her green eyes. He sees a mix of emotions- fear, determination, dread, worry, and anger.
Svetlana looks at his glassy, bloodshot eyes and murmurs, "I--I will go now."
"Do you want me to stay back? Help you?"
"Nyet. I already made my decision. I do not want for you to get more tangled up in this mess. You already have enough problems to deal with, no?"
Jean-Luc's chuckle is mirthless, "I do, yes. You are quite considerate of that fact, Assamite. If you somehow survive this, my villa is open to you. You know my number. Call me and I will come pick you up."
Svetlana wanly smiles, "Check in with Camille and Aaron if you get chance. Keep...keep them safe if you can."
Jean-Luc sighs a little, "I'm not sure if I can keep such a promise, but I--I will try. Good luck, Svetlana."
She takes the katana out of the towel, readies her obsidian dagger, and exits the car. She gently closes the door and cranes her neck to gaze at the towering skyscraper.
There's no telling what awaits her on the inside.
Up at the 43rd floor, Seth Steale is pacing about. The floor-to-ceiling glass windows overlook the cityscape and the distant sea. Some stars twinkle above. His surroundings mostly consist of debris, discarded tools, paint buckets, plastic sheets, and various other building materials.
When the elevator dings, Seth stands still and sets his magical snake head cane aside. He clasps his cold hands behind his back and dreamily stares out at the horizon.
A small smirk plays on his features when he senses Svetlana's approaching footsteps, "I am not a monster, Svetlana."
Svetlana sneers at him, weapons at the ready, "Then what are you?"
