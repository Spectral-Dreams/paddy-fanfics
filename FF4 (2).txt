Chapter 41: Final Death In The Summer Midnight
The sun makes its presence known and felt in this part of the world. Mortals rise from their slumbering whilst vampires fall to whatever counts as their own unique form of "slumbering" the whole darn day away.
And in this gilded cage of a Domain, some day-sleep a whole lot easier than many others.
Cars, trucks, bikes, buses and such zoom and swoosh across the asphalt- almost everywhere is littered with early-morning traffic. Everyone's going (or coming from) somewhere. Some are doing it in a hurry while others take it slow, steady, and easy.
The sun viciously beats down on the busy metropolis, causing the air to be heated up and a lot muggier than expected.
Not everyone is going about their lives. A few buildings are under construction, but it's mostly residential homes and shops being erected. So long as the right parties get their whack, everything shall be just dandy and right as rain.
(This damn place could actually use some real rain).
It goes without saying that the people who will inevitably be residing in those really nice-looking homes shall be of a...different caliber to the ones currently building them under the sweltering heat.
Exploited immigrant laborers toil under the sun. Most have their passports seized and locked in safes. Vicious beatings and discrimination of all varieties are tragically commonplace. But all is well and good as long as the scrilla keeps flowing in.
Even at that epoch of time, a large (upward) transfer of wealth is taking place. "Theft" is probably a more accurate word, and those who are victimized are only too aware of it...or starting to wake the hell up. It's a slow (but sure) process.
Better sooner than never at all!
And something has to be done about these various issues. Knowing is simply not enough.
While Svetlana day-sleeps, something inexplicable (bordering on sinister) draws her to Elysium of all places. Her near-Torporous body twitches and tics while the sun shines down on the world below. Afternoon soon makes its appearance, ushering in yet another wave of intense heat and humidity.
The mortals go with the flow, as it were. Evening couldn't come sooner. And when it does, traffic and such intensifies. People rush home, or head out, or maybe just take an idle stroll, or get some shopping done, or grab a bite, or whatever else.
Once the sun has completely set, Svetlana groggily wakes up and slowly gets out of bed. She does a few stretches and groans a few times. Even now, she still feels pulled towards the Grand Alhambra.
After showering and getting all cleaned up, she slips back into the bedroom and sees random clothes laid out on her bed. Jean-Luc even took her old, flecked, torn, and stained clothes and tossed them away.
Svetlana finds a t-shirt amidst this pile- a little on the looser side of things. She also finds a pair of faded slacks. She lets her slightly frayed hair be. The Assamite stares at her pale reflection in the mirror. The woman staring back at her has heavy eyes, a sullen expression, and a tired look written all over.
Ms. Bobrov firmly resolves to end this tonight, no matter what, and by any means necessary. She stays true to her principles, intends to honor the memories of the fallen, and avenge those who were robbed from this world too soon. Her resolve is strong, and yet...fear, doubt, and uncertainty still linger.
As the minutes go by, the Assassin's eyes begin to water, and her lips begin to tremble. Jean-Luc is busy cleaning the numerous shelves in his Haven. He has a bottle of cleaning spray in 1 hand and a rag cloth in another.
When Svetlana offers to help him, he brushes her off and insists that he is perfectly capable of doing it. Yet again, he keeps up his defensive shell, and it gets increasingly harder to tell where Jean-Luc ends and N0S4A2 begins...or vice versa.
Regardless, the Caitiff does ask, "So, Assamite...what is the plan for tonight? Hmm?"
Svetlana sits down on a nearby chair and joins her cold hands together, "I go to Elysium. You only drop me off. I--I do not know how to explain this, Jean-Luc, but I feel like I--I must go there. I HAVE TO!"
Jean-Luc runs the cloth along an empty shelf, sprays some blueish liquid onto its surface, and wipes it down again. He doesn't bother looking back at the Assamite.
He offers, "OK. I will drop you, then. But it's risky, isn't it?"
Svetlana shrugs, "Last time I went, I was fine."
"Very well. We might be a little delayed tonight. I have some things that I must attend to."
"Da. Is no issue. And--and thank you."
Jean-Luc gives her a half-smile, "Hm." After that, he goes off to his room to get dressed. He soon emerges wearing a dark-brown leather jacket, blue jeans, and scuffed sneakers. He puts his trademark helmet on.
From there, he pulls a heavy duffel bag out of a false panel behind his wall. He slings it over his shoulder and heads for the door.
Svetlana furrows her brows, "What is in bag?"
Jean-Luc lets out a muffled chuckle. He lets the bag drop to the ground with a thud. Without saying a word, he takes a knee and unzips it. Svetlana's eyes widen when she sees all the cash inside! A tiny gasp of disbelief escapes her.
Jean-Luc takes out a thick wad of cash, dryly inspects it, and shoves it back in. He quickly tells her, "It's not for me or for my benefit. My debt to the Sheikh is...sizable, Assamite. You would not understand."
Svetlana doesn't push the issue, but she notes the slight pain and frustration in the Unbound Kindred's tone of voice. In a way, she can relate due to having financial and economic issues of her own.
For nights on end, she hasn't worked. Many of her babysitting clients refuse to answer her messages on FB. Calls to regulars are made in vain- no one picks up or returns them. Her only avenue of income is slowly drying up.
Which does beg the question: should she snatch victory from the jaws of defeat, what will she do? How will she proceed from there? If and when it's all over, will the blade still need to be wielded?
Will Svetlana even have a place in Al-Kushraf or will she have to run? Again. And where to? Moving around is not an easy thing for anyone in such a precarious position- mortal or immortal alike. And if things like these cannot be put on hold or indefinitely paused.
So many questions swirl around in her mind. Her Unlife has taken quite the drastic turn in such a short amount of time. Jean-Luc merely drives- traffic is denser than usual on this evening, so it's gonna be a long one!
The duo manages to score some warm bites along the way- their victims are breathing but lacking in a few pints of the good (red) stuff.
