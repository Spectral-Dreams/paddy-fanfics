Chapter 38: Of Serpent And Blade
The sun now reigns supreme over Al-Kushraf, UAE. It's a scorching hot day, and the Unliving are fast asleep- or as "fast asleep" as powerful, blood-hungry corpses can be.
Ain't no Snake can do their Protean shit when the sun's out. Nope, nuh-uh. But when the evening comes around, then looking over one's shoulder shall hardly suffice.
Anyways, the mortals go about their day. School buses chug along the usual routes that they've likely taken millions of times. Students of various ages and nationalities are seated, laughing and talking amongst themselves. Some look a bit worried about what the future might hold for them; others seem content enough.
Their parents and guardians stare at spreadsheets or go out shopping at this or that fancy boutique store. Life in the Domain goes on as usual.
Some reports of excessive violence over the past few nights are conveniently suppressed by the authorities. Tourism appears to be on the rise once more. The image-obsessed Emirati authorities do all that they can to maintain the status quo- shittier influencer campaigns, perverse incentives to make sure everyone maintains a positive vibe, and so on.
It's all a goddamn ruse. Underneath the surface of it all, a war between immortals is raging on. Blood is consumed with wild abandon, and ashes are strewn all over the gaffe. Oh, yeah, those who were Blood-Hunting the Assamites might've done a bit of Diablerie here and there.
(What happens in a Blood Hunt STAYS in a Blood Hunt...usually.)
The day is longer than usual, so that means the vampires will awaken from their day-sleep even later than usual. Once the sun makes its grand descent below the horizon, however, things begin to pick up quickly!
Before stirring from her day-sleep, Svetlana's mind is dragged elsewhere, back to her initial nights as a Fledgling in the Banu Haqim Clan. Her Sire wasted no time in training her, giving her a strict (but not overly so) training regimen.
During the evenings (much like these!), John would book out a martial arts studio for a few hours and vigorously train his Childe alongside a few others. The then-Fledgling would spend most of her nights fighting wooden training dummies, chunky punching bags, and the like.
She never did get quite good at the whole Kenpo thing, like her Sire was; Svetlana would often slip up on the more intricate kicks or get confused by some of the more advanced techniques.
But once those dual daggers were gripped by her cold hands, all bets were off. John adapted- remarkably- to his Childe's choice of melee weaponry and adjusted the nightly training regimens accordingly.
John wasn't just teaching Svetlana how to fight, but he was also imparting valuable lessons of staying composed under pressure, maintaining an unwavering resolve, and avoiding being overly sentimental when faced with adversity.
More importantly than all the stabby-stabby, however, were (and are) the teachings of the Blade of Haqim- it's connection (however it still struggles to exist on these nights) to the liberation of Palestine (by any and all means necessary), the necessity of upholding true justice, and the importance of maintaining one's Humanity and principles in such inhuman and barbarous times.
Back in the here and now, Svetlana's eyes pop open at last, her cold body curled on the ground into a sort of fetal position. She yawns widely and groans while also stretching. The air is still thick and muggy, and it doesn't appear to have gotten any better.
Svetlana quickly slips into the green tracksuit and heads out. Ravinder is ready as well. He spreads his arms out, "Good evening! Sleep well?"
The Assamite forces a quick nod and says nothing.
The Ravnos points out, "There is a gym nearby. We can find someone to feed on over there. The showers are also quite good, haha! Ever since coming here, that has been my go-to spot!"
"Hmm. What else are we going to do tonight?"
Ravinder rubs the base of his neck, "Ah, yes. Good question. How many of your fellow Assamites do you think might still be out there, hmm?"
Svetlana is unsure, "Is hard to tell. Even if I knew, it--it feels risky to contact them, no?"
"Maybe, maybe not. Sometimes, Svetlana, you have to take some bold risks and hope that it will work out...decently well. I would not be where I am had I not taken certain risks. But I do know that such advise isn't suitable for everybody..."
A heavy silence falls over both vampires for several seconds before Ravinder speaks again, "Arey, sorry. You are looking nice in the tracksuit. It almost matches your green eyes. Almost."
Svetlana presses her lips into a thin smile and silently walks away. She turns her gaze skywards and spots only a few twinkling stars in the dark sky above. Ravinder follows her upward gaze and softly chuckles.
The gym in question is a brightly lit facility full of bold and flashy colors that make no qualm about popping out. Oranges, blues, neon-greens, and the like. It looks like something from a slick kind of future.
Loud, motivational-type music blares through the speaker systems while the patrons pump iron, bounce around on exercise balls, or run on silent treadmills.
With her tracksuit, Svetlana isn't too out-of-place in such an establishment. Ravinder nods her over to a corner and instructs her, "Don't worry about getting spotted. But that's no excuse to be careless. You already know this, don't you?"
"Da."
"We will meet here once we have done what we came here to do. Showers are bloody amazing, by the way. Don't turn the tap all the way- the water comes at full force even when the tap is half-turned."
Svetlana licks her lips and nods, "I will keep in mind. And...thank you. For--for helping me."
The Raven puts his hand on her shoulder and looks into her green eyes, "John was a good man. And you are a good woman. I will do everything in my power to keep you safe, but you have to make an effort to come up with some sort of plan to deal with all of this madness, OK? No pressure, of course. Take your time."
Svetlana meanders off to find a warm (albeit sweaty) neck to sink her fangs into. Even at this hour of the evening, a lot of gymgoers can be seen, hard at their workouts and what have you.
Ravinder sidles around the perimeter, observing everyone with keen interest and narrowed eyes. The Wanderer spots a buff lad who might very well be tonight's...dinner.
For her part, Svetlana has her dead eyes set on a skinny woman well into her 50s.
The vampires soon go through the whole messy rigmarole of stalking, isolating, savagely biting, taking (but not too much), licking the fang-induced puncture wounds, and letting the still-breathing bodies of their victims slump onto the floor.
From there, Svetlana takes a shower. She likes the way the water feels but decides against spending too long when she hears a few regulars approaching. Once clean, the Assamite quickly dries herself off and slips back into her green tracksuit. She waves her head around, her blonde hair whipping behind her, and makes her way to meet up with Ravinder.
The Ravnos smells like lavenders and roses. He wears a broad smile on his ashy face and confidently strides out of the gym like nothing ever happened.
Well, it can't be put off forever. The onus is on Svetlana Bobrov to come up with some sort of a plan to...well, to...fight back, to do what, exactly? She's not entirely sure in regard to what is expected of her at this trying time.
All she knows right now is that things aren't just going to go back to the way they were. This is the new normal, the abnormal, so to speak.
Ravinder Rao revs his bullet bike a few times, the engine fiercely roaring as he pats the seat behind him. Svetlana hurries along, the Beast within her feeling somewhat satisfied by its sanguine treat. The Hunger is eternal; such is the price that one pays.
(But that ain't the only charge!)
Something stirs deep within Svetlana's Unliving being. It speeds up with each passing moment.
At Elysium...
A tense atmosphere pervades the 140th floor of the Grand Alhambra. The Setites are in a meeting that was called by a pretty influential Elder around these parts. It's regarding Seth's claims of Praxis over the cities of Al-Kushraf and Dubai. Of course it's about that.
Murmurs and mutters waft through the air. The lights are adequately dim enough to the point where no Viper feels even the slightest twinge of discomfort.
The man of the hour himself is yet to show his pale face around here. There's an unease amongst those present. Even then, a few of them are in their usual cliques and whatnot, chattering, tittering, tattling, and lightly hissing.
Amongst a circle of chairs stands a middle-aged man with hardened and wrinkly features. He has dull, droopy brown eyes. He's on the taller side and is of average weight.
The man is dressed in the accoutrements of what can be construed as a middle manager at some kind of MNC- slacks, polished shoes, striped long-sleeve shirt with a firm collar.
He holds his head high and speaks with a stiff British accent, "Now, where in Apep's name is our High Priest?"
Someone from within the group puts in, "Brother Rupert, calm yourself. Seth will get here when he gets here."
Rupert claps back, "We are not Licks, meant to be kept waiting around so that Mr. Steale can waltz in here whenever he bloody well pleases! We have work to do! We have other, greater matters to see to!"
Another member says softly, "Rupert, we are with you...and we happen to see merit to what you're saying. Seth is overstepping his bounds. He needs to be reined in. And by Typhon, he will. Now, finish your drink."
The member in question gestures to a tall glass of thick red liquid on a small glass table.
Rupert downs it in a singular gulp and winces at the pungency, "How very thoughtful of Seth to not keep us all waiting on an empty stomach."
Rupert is no ordinary Setite. He is an Elder- almost on par with Absalom's total cumulative age. Moreover, Rupert is the Childe of a very powerful Hierophant, a powerful Hierophant who claims to have known the Antediluvian Set personally.
Of course, Rupert is the name that this Elder currently goes by- he's had to do a lot of name-changing over the centuries! And it gets increasingly harder as technology and the general knowledge base advances!
Right now, this Serpent is presenting himself as the face of a group of dissenters and holdouts who flat-out refuse their High Priest's claims of Praxis over the 2 Domains.
(It's 1 thing to have someone from outside the Clan saying "no". It's a whole other thing to have those of one's own Blood saying "no" and voicing it publicly.)
Absalom sits quietly in the far corner, keenly observing. His passive Auspex permits him to comfortably hear everything that this tight little group of naysayers are yapping about. For now, he keeps to himself and patiently awaits the arrival of the man who WILL become the Prince of Dubai and Al-Kushraf.
Seth's not gonna like what he's gonna hear from his fellow Typhonists, but he has to hear it all the same.
It ain't long before the High Priest strides in casually, nay a care or bother in the whole wide world. He's dressed in a fine brown suit, a mustard yellow shirt with the top buttons undone, snakeskin boots that appear imported, and a pair of sunglasses.
And the magical snake head cane with its mesmerizing emerald eyes! A staple of the man's entire ensemble.
His unctuous smirk is clearly visible, even in the dimly lit room. Edwin is dressed plainly, his head hung low, almost in shame.
Seth's Ghoul doesn't say much these nights. He keeps his mouth closed and simply goes with the motions, figuring that it's far too late to be having a conscience crisis anyway.
With his head held high, the Prince-to-be approaches his fellow Sand-Snakes and greets them all, "Blessed be. And what brings all of you together? It's a fine night, isn't it?"
Rupert remains seated. He stifles a groan, "Yes, yes. Blessed be. We need to talk. Please, sit down. You are going to want to hear what we have to say, Seth. I strongly advise that you take a seat, silence yourself, and listen carefully. And please, remove your bloody sunglasses!"
Seth is a little taken aback by the harshness of the Elder's words. He complies and sets his butt down on a seat right opposite Rupert. He puts his cane on his lap, clasps his cold hands together, and leans forward with a slightly expecting look.
Rupert glares daggers at the ambitious Neonate and begins, "You are our High Priest."
Seth chuckles faintly, "Oh. Wow. I wasn't aware of that. How very revealing, haha!"
Some light laughter escapes from within the circle. Yet, the mood gets more tense than anything else. Edwin stands a few paces away, refusing to make eye contact with anyone for a myriad of reasons.
Rupert doesn't chuckle, "You are nothing more than our High Priest, you shall serve as nothing more than our High Priest."
Seth blandly asks, "Is there a point to this--"
Rupert raises a firm finger, "Silence! Has your Sire taught you nothing of respect, Neonate? You are our High Priest- nothing more, nothing less- as such, you would do well to know your place within the hierarchy of the Ministry."
Seth lets out a small sigh, "I do. And after taking Praxis, I will continue to serve as your High Priest. It would be my honor- my privilege- to do so. It'll be quite a burden for 1 man to shoulder, but with Typhon, all things are possible."
He proudly spreads his arms out and declares, "And I am proof of that! In such a short time, I have ascended to greater highs. Highs that even Rafael couldn't have possibly imagined!"
Rupert is unimpressed, "The Blood of Set courses through your veins, yet there are times when you seem to possess the Arrogance of a Ventrue...or the fanciful delusions of a Toreador."
Seth side-eyes the Elder, "Uh...what?"
Rupert gives a half-smile and shakes his head, "It only proves that you are simply in over your head, and therefore not ready for the office of Prince. And 2 entire cities? Had we shown such daring ambition in our time, we would've been destroyed on the spot!"
Some of the folks within the circle signal their agreement with a murmur or 2. Edwin stands stiffly where he is. He gulps slowly.
Seth huffs and tries to maintain a cool exterior, "Huh. Is that so? Well, times have changed since the so-called 'Dark Ages'. Not that all change is good change, mind you. Heh. I...that was a very interesting comment. Something I'm sure we'd all love to explore further if we had--"
Rupert waves a hand dismissively, "You will sit where you are and you will listen to me. Normally, I shan't outright contradict the High Priest in such a blatant fashion, but we see no other alternatives. And you are only making things needlessly complicated, Seth."
The Prince-to-be narrows his eyes, "Excuse me? My Praxis will take the Ministry to new, never-before-seen heights. It'll be unprecedented! Your support would--would mean the--"
The Elder Viper scoffs bitterly, "You shan't be receiving our support. While you were taking your sweet time in arriving here- an indicator of how disrespectful and impudent you can be- we have come to the unanimous decision to contest your claims of Praxis over the Domains."
If Seth could go red in the face, right about now is the moment when he would. He lowers his head, averting his gaze from all the dead serpentine eyes burrowing into his very being. He can feel the pressure of their stares eating through him bit by bit.
Rupert condescendingly points out, "You appear to have a short memory. Let me refresh it for you: Sybil is the rightful ruler of THIS Domain- I simply do not care for Dubai; let it be left to its fate or let someone else have it."
Seth mumbles, "Sybil's been missing for years--"
Rupert interjects, "Last I heard, someone claims to have spotted her in Athens, Greece. If memory serves correctly, there is still a Founding Temple in that city. I am yet to confirm such rumors- my letter to the stewards is still awaiting a response. But I am confident that Sybil shall soon return to take what is rightfully hers...not yours. Or anybody else's, for that matter."
The High Priest can't help but smirk, but only slightly- he himself planted (spread) those rumors around. Sybil's done for and she ain't coming back, but it's best to not let this crowd in on that deeply dark secret!
Rupert sternly regards Seth, "Have you anything to say for yourself? Why so silent all of a sudden, hmm? A far cry from that rousing speech which you delivered the other night!"
Some of the vampires let out a few laughs but quickly zip it when Seth glowers at them. He eventually gathers his composure and lamely requests, "Brother Rupert, can I speak with you...privately. Please? I--if you don't mind?"
Rupert picks up on the shakiness in Seth's voice and plainly nods, "Ah. Of course. Shall we?"
The 2 of them head towards a window. From all the way up here, the view of the city below is just bright flashes and flickers of neon lights and a lingering haze.
They pass by Absalom and a few other Malkavians, Toreadors, Ventrue, and Nosferatu speaking to each other about fairly trivial matters.
Rupert runs a hand through his brown hair and ruffles it around until he's satisfied, "Hmph. I should've gotten that blasted hair transplant when given the opportunity. Never mind. So, what is it that you wished to discuss, hmm?"
Seth looks troubled, "Why are you doing this to me?"
"Tch. You are taking this as a personal attack, an affront, to you? I would've thought that your ego would've been tempered by all your years as High Priest. Has your tenure as a spiritual guide not humbled you enough? How dare you aspire to greater power!"
Seth seethes with rage and desperation in equal measures, "The world owes me for all the shit that it put me through. This is me collecting what's mine! Deal with it: your precious Sybil is gone...with the wind. If she gave a damn, then she would've come back already. I'm not gonna stand idly and let this kind of opportunity pass on by."
Rupert opines, "For a Neonate- someone barely an Ancilla- your ambition is quite remarkable. But at some point, it becomes arrogance bordering on lunacy- perhaps all that time you've been spending with that Malkavian has rubbed off on you in the wrong sort of way."
Seth glowers at his fellow Corrupter, "Fu--goddammit. Why, why are you doing this? Why now? We need to present a unified front. There's an ongoing Blood Hunt--"
Rupert lifts a finger, "Ah, yes, yet another matter that we must speak about. I'd prefer to do this publicly. There's no use whispering in the corner like...schoolchildren, hm?"
Seth sighs heavily, "Fair enough. This wasn't what I was expecting when I was summoned here tonight. I was counting on the support of my own Clan, and now I can't even fucking get that."
He returns and slumps into 1 of the unoccupied seats amidst the circle.
Rupert calmly saunters back to his seat and easily takes control again. He looks around the room, casting an approving glance upon all who side with him and his vision.
Edwin remains silent and stays where he is. His eyes dart between everyone present, trying desperately to avoid getting dragged further deeper into the much. As it is, he's already up to his neck in all sorts of unpleasant things, whether as an accomplice or a reluctantly complacent witness to the sociopathy of his Domitor.
The billionaire sociopath in question has a resentful look written all over his face. He clearly feels that his status within the Ministry is slipping away. Slithering away, perhaps?
Rupert fiddles with the collar of his shirt before speaking, "We will hold out, no matter how long it takes. We will patiently wait for SYBIL to return to this Domain and claim what is rightfully hers and HERS ALONE! She will be our Prince and WE will follow her lead! Until then, we keep faith and await her return."
He adds, "We pray and hope that it shall be soon, but we prepare ourselves in the case that her return shall take a while longer."
A few folks nod along agreeably; some of them even lightly clap and titter in approval.
Seth dryly puts in, "If it is Typhon's will for her to return, of course. Who can know the ways of Apep, hmm?"
Rupert clicks his tongue and ignores that, "Now, there is another matter for us to discuss: the ongoing Blood Hunt. We are pleased to know that nearly all of the Assamites in this Domain are being...purged. We concede- fully- that the Blade of Haqim's teachings have only served to corrupt and lead many astray."
He looks at the High Priest and states, "In that regard, you have made the right call, Mr. Steale. But there is something that...clouds your judgment. I suspect that you know what- or more accurately, who- I am speaking of, correct?"
Seth swallows the lump in his throat and shrugs, "You seem to be on a roll, Rupert. Don't let me get in the way of your posturing and grandstanding."
Rupert brushes that off, "This is a serious matter. Treating it with levity is to your own detriment."
"Uh-huh. Go on."
The Elder does so, "There is an Assamite of the Blade of Haqim. A woman. Blonde hair, green eyes, late-20s, Slavic, I believe."
Seth lowers his gaze and bites his lower lip. A single word escapes his mouth, "Svetlana..."
"Yes. Your infatuation with this Assamite is no secret to us. And it is blinding you to the harsh realities! It comes, to us, as a great surprise that SHE is exempt from destruction while everyone else of her Blood have already been reduced to dust and ashes!"
Seth can feel his dead heart clench inside his chest. The thought of anything happening to his precious makes him want to explode into little fiery particles. But he can't show any sign of weakness or immaturity in such an esteemed group of Setites.
He gathers his composure and squares his jaw, "I can save her, Rupert. I can fix her. The others are too far gone, but I know that there's hope for--for Svetlana Bobrov. She can be tamed, be freed of the shackles of modernity, and be made anew!"
Rupert groans, "Be that as it may, you yourself demanded the complete and total destruction of the Banu Haqim after what Svetlana's own Sire had done to you. As such, we are immediately overturning your exemption: Svetlana Bobrov is to meet her Final Death just as every single Assamite in this Domain already has...or soon will, Typhon willing."
Seth sucks some air through his teeth, "What?! That is not--"
Rupert interrupts him sharply, "It has been settled. We will find her and we will destroy her. I have been informed that there are now only a handful of those wretched Assamites in this city. Rest assured, the woman you fancy shall soon be--"
Seth loses it. He lunges forward and grabs the Elder by his shirt collar. His cold, dead amber eyes gleam with a mixture of anger and desperation, "NO! You can't do this to me! How--how dare you!"
Gasps emanate from the other bloodsuckers in the circle. Even Absalom quirks an eyebrow and turns his attention to the commotion.
Seth shakes Rupert and snarls, "Y--you don't have the authority or--or the right to go behind my back like this! NO! I am the Prince! I WILL BE THE PRINCE!"
Rupert shoves the High Priest off of him, "How utterly stupid of you, boy! Very well then! You have made your choice and your position known to us all! Know that I shan't forget this! Given your recent antics, I am now starting to doubt your capabilities- and competency- as our High Priest!"

I know every single thing that there is to know about not only your shady dealings over the decades but also your...more private and personal indiscretions. It would be such a shame if those found their ways to the wrong set of ears, huh?"

"It's a fucking promise."
"Since when did you become so--so impudent? I recall a time when you knew your place, when you were humble. But that was a time when your Sire was around to keep you and your many ambitions- and delusions- in check. His falling into a Torpor has only served to embolden you in all the wrong bloody ways!"
Seth huffs, "Forget about Rafael. This doesn't concern him- none of it does. He'll get up when he's ready. This is between me and you, Rupert."
Gasps of shock and wide eyes all around! Even Absalom's expression softens into a concerned one. Edwin uncomfortably shuffles around.
Seth growls, "You have no right to override a damn word of mine. NONE. I demand that you rescind everything that you said tonight and--and repent for your sins against the High Priest."
Rupert counters, "I shall do no such thing. Not when the High Priest has lost his sense of direction and purpose. A Setite without vision and clarity is no Setite at all- they are simply floundering in the muck!"
Seth retorts, "You don't know shit. But if you keep going down this path, so help me Apep, I'll--"
His threat is interrupted by Alexander. The young Setite pants and hastily announces, "We--we found her! Svetlana! We--we found her! She was last spotted near uh...Sufi Street. The Ravnos on the motorbike is still with her! What are your orders, chief?"
Rupert speaks up before Seth can put a word in, "Destroy her and the Ravnos helping her- he is an accomplice. As of now, that Assamite is no longer exempt from the Blood Hunt."
Absalom looks between both men, his gaze lingering on the aspiring Prince, "It is most intriguing. Seth?"
Seth is at a loss for words. The pressure isn't really aiding matters. He remains deathly silent.
Rupert smirks and declares, "We shall interpret his silence as a concession, then. Alexander, what are you waiting for? The gun to go off?! Gehenna?! HURRY ALONG! Destroy her and anyone else who so much as offers sympathy to her! Before the next sunrise, the last of the Banu Haqim Clan in this Domain shall be reduced to dust!"

Alexander doesn't even bother asking Seth for approval or what he thinks on the whole matter. He simply grabs a bottle of blood and takes a few swigs as he makes his way for the elevators.
Seth's shoulders are slumped. He wordlessly throws himself back on the chair while a vacant, spaced-out look plays on his features. He knows that the odds are heavily stacked against him, but he can't...give up.
He can't let go, not when so much power and greatness is inches away from his grasp! He can practically taste sweet, sweet victory on his tongue!
Absalom comes over and places a hand on the High Priest's shoulder. He asks to speak with him...in private. Seth is more than willing to oblige. The other Setites stare at the Malkavian Elder and feel uneasy when he icily glares back at them.
Rupert sniffs, "Absalom...or whatever your name is, you would do well to not interfere in matters that do not concern you. You- of all people- should know what happens when the young amongst our kind start to get...ideas."
Absalom merely replies, "And you should know what happens when our kind remains firmly stuck in a bygone era. We must adapt."
Rupert dismissively waves his hand, "Tcah. Adapt. I've bloody adapted perfectly well, thank you. It's getting harder, but we persist only due to the boundless grace of Typhon. Regardless, you are only a new arrival to this city- you still have no idea how things have been running for the past few decades."
Absalom responds, "You underestimate the depths of my understanding, Serpent. I do not just understand, I know things, I anticipate outcomes, and I am always prepared for any situation that comes my way."
Rupert sneers, "Oh, please. You Lunatics put far too much faith in your nonsense visions, deranged babblings, and other such...eccentric idiocy. We Setites, on the contrary, rely upon Apep alone to guide us on such turbulent and troublesome nights!"

"Yes. Let us speak."
As the duo depart, Rupert defiantly calls out, "It shan't make a difference! Your declaration of Praxis over the Domains is over, Seth! And Svetlana's free ride comes to an end...tonight! This Domain shall be purged of those maniacs, fanatics, radicals, and terrorists!"

Well, shit.
Seth flops down on a couch in a private room and rubs his face while Absalom calmy sits down on a swiveling office chair.
The High Priest groans and grumbles, "What do I do, what do I do, what do I fucking do?!"
Absalom urges, "Calm yourself. We shall deal with this. Listen closely to me..."
The Viper sighs, "Not like I got much of a choice, huh?"
"They will not succeed in destroying Svetlana tonight."
A lone tear of blood escapes Seth's eye. It falls onto his shirt sleeve, leaving a small stain on it. He shakily asks, "H--how can you be so sure of that?"
The Elder Seer answers, "It matters not. Be assured that Svetlana will not be going to her Final Death on this night..." he trails off, stopping himself from adding more. More than he already has said, and more than he probably ought to say.
(And Absalom is already quite privy to Miss Bobrov's inevitable fate, but he's not about to blurt that out!)

The High Priest is seething with anger and rage. Absalom is as cool as an old-timer corpse cucumber.
Out of nowhere, Seth babbles, "I--I love her, Absalom. I'm crazy about her. I'm...madly in love with Svetlana Bobrov..."
Absalom sighs, "Right now, you must focus yourself on the consolidation of power before you think of such personal matters. She may yet come around; she may not. What matters is not showing weakness during this important and delicate stage of the transition period."
Seth grits his teeth and closes his eyes tightly, "I know Rupert all too goddamn well. I know where he keeps his dirt buried. Before this, he used to come to me for advice of a spiritual nature. I'd counsel him and we got along fairly well."
"That is all well and good, but it seems to matter not anymore. Not after what he did...after he openly questioned your judgment, Seth. An aspiring Prince such as yourself cannot be permitted to falter or even show hesitation and indecisiveness. That is the downfall of many on nights such as these."
"Hmm. I--I think I have a few ideas on how to deal with Rupert and his ilk of naysayers and--and doubters."
The Malkavian Primogen nods approvingly, "That is good to hear. You need not disclose the nature of your plans. Suffice it to say that they will serve your purposes, yes?"
"Yup. That's what I'm hoping for, anyway. These things tend to get messy. And people are unpredictable...unreliable, too. But I'll look into it from my end. Uh...thanks. You really are cut out for this sorta thing, huh?"
Absalom shrugs indifferently, "I believe that it is my destiny, High Priest. As such, I would strongly recommend that you do not read too much into it. I am only doing what is beneficial for all of Kindred Society."
Seth wipes his face, "The worthy parts of Kindred Society, right?"
