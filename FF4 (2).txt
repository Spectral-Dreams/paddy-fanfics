Chapter 39: On The Run
The sun rises in all its splendorous glory and majesty. This part of the world is coated in a harsh yellow-white light; it's rather unforgiving out there, to be honest. Warm air wafts through the streets as morning commuters start their daily grind.
Students go to school, tourists leave hotels to go and check out the sights, workers show up at the worksite, factory floor, office spaces, or wherever else they're compelled to sell their labor power for a scrap of a wage, benefits (if any), and whatever other perks might be included.
Another day in the Al-Kushraf, UAE. Another mundane and ordinary day. All the weather reports are unified on saying (more or less) the same thing: it's hot outside.
Blistering heat permeates the entire Domain. At times, it feels almost unbearably suffocating. The Kindred, regardless, remain as still as statues. Their supernatural bodies rejuvenate themselves while in the throes of the day-long slumber.
The remainder of the day gives way to afternoon- traffic already starts to pick up at this point. People rush around in a mad dash, going hither and yon, to and fro.
When the sun finally slips beneath the horizon and sinks out of sight, the denizens of the dark night begin to rise from their day-slumbering. Their respective Beasts stir them awake and compel them to seek out the only thing capable of sustaining its eternal Hunger: blood.
Red, reddish, fresh, squeezed, drawn, stale, salty, sweet, young, old, it matters not. Well, unless one is a Ventrue- those lot are picky eaters!
Anyways...
Svetlana is awakened by the echoes of a giggling child. At this point in time, it's an all-too-familiar giggle- that of the Wraith of little Chloe Goodall.
