Chapter 42: Ascent
Local and international news is abuzz with the explosions that rocked the Grand Alhambra last night. The fault is pinned on gas leaks and the like. Aside from the many casualties, no significant damage was done.
Authorities flat-out refuse to give a solid estimate on the number of casualties. But they reckon that it's pretty damn high.
The owners of that fine establishment could not be reached for comment. At all. Despite all the bleakness and tragedy that has unfolded, life seemingly chugs along. But certain people can feel a terrible and oppressive change in the air.
Farah hastily shoves as much of her crap as she can into a suitcase. She blows off the last of her savings on a plane ticket back to the USA. She just clicked on whatever flight was leaving first- it didn't matter which one, it didn't matter where!
She needs to get out! Her Desperation drives her to abandon the battle against the many lurking dangers in this city. She can feel it- something wicked this way comes.
She even closes her user account on Hunter-net.org, but before doing so, she sends a final farewell message to some of the people whom she has grown close to.
The Imbued Hunter profusely apologizes for bailing at the last minute. She states that she's needed elsewhere. ScalyHand06 (Seth) is also a recipient of her message. He'll just shrug and move on after reading it.
The flight leaves before the afternoon. Farah gulps and gets settled into the airplane's plush seat. Her hand trembles as she tries to put her seatbelt on. A hostess widely smiles at her and offers to help.
After taking care of that, Farah leans back in her seat and deeply inhales. She shakily exhales and hopes that her harried appearance won't raise any issues (or eyebrows). When the plane finally lifts off, her breathing becomes easier.
She glances out the window and tears up a little. The Hunter wistfully mumbles, "We couldn't bring the Reckoning to you, Al-Kushraf. I--I'm so sorry. Maybe another day, another time, a--another life, eh? I...I don't know."
Farah shuts her eyes and leans against the headrest. Despite the toddler who occasionally kicks the back of her seat, she snoozes away during most of the flight.
When the plane lands somewhere in the Northeastern United States, she breathes a huge sigh of relief and thanks her lucky stars. Yet, the images of last night's fierce fight constantly flash before her eyes.
She rubs her temples and groans softly. She can't get Svetlana's green eyes out of her mind. There are a few local Hunter Cells that she might be able to link up with. Even now, her Vengeance Drives her to seek out the entities that go bump in the night.
After picking up her suitcase from the baggage claim, she goes through the motions of exiting the airport. She can't let things end like this. The Hunter of the Martial Creed hails a taxi and decides to swing by some old haunts.
Maybe some old faces will still remember her after all these years.
Back in Al-Kushraf, the sun begins its slow descent. The evening light casts an ethereal glow all over the city. Things were bound to change, and not necessarily in a good sort of way.
Mr. Bean's eyes pop open. Despite what he had done, he went through the day-sleep without too much of a hassle. He sits up and rubs his tired eyes. He elaborately stretches himself out, yawns for show, and hobbles over to the penthouse living room.
Absalom watches the cityscape. The Elder has his chin raised high.
Mr. Bean groans, "Hrrrmf. Good evening. Um...snooze well?"
"Yes. You?"
"I guess so. I'm still seeing the damn seagulls, Absalom. And they're all flying the same patterns, but there's something odd and--and different about them now?"
The Elder Malk simply says, "Oh? Interesting."
Mr. Bean looks down at his bare feet and mumbles, "They--they--all the seagulls are like, translucent. Kinda like ghosts. So, when they fly together, I still see Svetlana's face...but it looks all so ghostly. Like the uh...holograms in the Star Wars movies, y'know, the ones where--"
Absalom holds up his hand, "Yes, I know what you are referring to. What did you expect would happen once you had brought Final Death upon that Assamite?"
Mr. Bean glares at his fellow Seer, "Well, I--I was expecting the damn vision to stop! Not this! Not whatever the hell this is!"
Absalom calmly orders, "Calm yourself. So, you are still seeing the same vision?"
"But all ghostly and weird-like! Like, I don't wanna keep seeing her face! It--it makes me feel kinda guilty about the whole thing, man."
Absalom points out, "Guilt is yet another weakness of yours. Besides, it was a Blood Hunt. You were in the right to destroy her. She herself had slain a Primogen- in our Society, such a grave crime oftentimes results in Final Death. As I said, you did what you had to do."
He adds, "It was your inevitable destiny."
Mr. Bean lowers his head and sighs, "This wasn't how I was expecting to feel, Abs. I asked for her forgiveness. D--do you think she forgave me?"
"That...is between you and the one you still pray to. I do not wish to discuss such matters."
Mr. Bean frowns and grumbles, "Y'know, Abs, a Sire is supposed to help and--and nurture their Childe, right? Well, you aren't exactly doing much of that!"
Absalom rolls a shoulder, "Was it not I who found you amongst the Trash? Was it not I who revealed your true nature as a Child of Malkav? Was it not I who shared with you the gifts of the Blood and taught you the ways of our Clan?"
The Elder shakes his head and goes on, "I do not expect or even want gratitude from you. I have gone without such things for quite some time. All I ask is that you learn to control yourself and your powers. That is why you are here with me- not because I wish to be your friend or anything like that."
"Yeah, well...it'd be nice to be treated a little better, y'know?"
Absalom turns on his heel and stares daggers into Mr. Bean's very being. The latter is left shaking in his boots. He gulps and mumbles, "Uh...do--don't look at me like that. S--stop."
The Elder Seer narrows an eye and takes a step closer to the Neonate. He leans in close and inspects him. Mr. Bean trembles and falls to his knees. He holds his hands out in a supplicating gesture and pleads, "PLEASE, PLEASE! Don't--don't leave me!"
Tears of blood freely flow down like twin waterfalls. Absalom scoffs and steps away from him, "I do not plan on leaving you any time soon. Our fates and destinies- for the time being- are quite intertwined."
Mr. Bean holds onto Absalom's cold hand and shakily bites into it. He takes a little of the Elder's Vitae and even goes so far as to lick the blood that trickles from his wrist. He gasps and lets go of the other man's hand.
Absalom pulls his sleeve down and grimaces, "This is the way things must be. I have chosen you and chosen...you now are. Before we leave, there are some things that we must go over."
Mr. Bean stands up and wipes his face. Red streaks and smudges stain his palm. He sniffles and nods his head, "Anything, man. Y--you name it and I'll do it."
Absalom ushers his fellow Seer to the couch. They sit down and go over a few things.
"I want you to keep quiet about destroying the Assamite. Say only what needs to be said to the Archons who shall question you. We have nothing to hide but be on guard when in their presence. DO NOT utilize your Auspex anywhere near them, understand?"
Mr. Bean nods and asks, "But why?"
Absalom grimly answers, "Archons do not like it when such things are done. They will seize upon any reason to destroy you if they sense that you are using the Blood against them. So, DO NOT utilize your Auspex in their presence. Even I cannot save you from their wrath."
Mr. Bean gulps, "A--alright, man. OK. Next uh...tidbit of info?"
The Elder Malkavian sternly says, "Mention ONLY what needs to be mentioned. DO NOT volunteer any unneeded, irrelevant, or unwanted information. Avoid tangents, stories, anecdotes, references to popular media, and the like. These Archons are serious people and do not have much patience for those who waste their time."
The young Malk nods and mumbles to himself, "OK. OK. No Star Wars references- sequels or prequels. Got it. What if they ask me a deeply uncomfortable question, though?"
Absalom flatly replies, "Answer to the best of your ability. Do not act like an erratic fool. Archons do not suffer fools gladly. Understand this clearly for I shall only tell it to you once."
"Yeah, yeah. Next? I--I feel like I should get a checklist here, haha!"
"No. Commit it to memory- it is the difference between Unlife and Final Death. They will ask you about Svetlana Bobrov. Tell them everything as it transpired. Given her own crimes, you will be cleared of any wrongdoing. It was a justified destruction, regardless of what happened before and after."
Mr. Bean gulps, "Right. Sure thing. But--but what about Prince Seth? He kinda had a crush on her, right? Didn't you mention that??"
"Forget about him. I will deal with him. Worry not, he will not harm you."
Mr. Bean presses his lips into a thin line, "And--and if he tries to?"
Absalom plainly echoes, "He will not harm you."
Mr. Bean isn't too reassured by it. He runs a hand through his bleached hair and blows out a raspberry, "OK. Anything else?"
Absalom gets up from his seat and answers, "We will discuss it later. Get yourself cleaned up. Wear something formal. Archons are not casual friends and should be treated with the respect with which they are rightfully due."
Mr. Bean gulps uneasily, "Y--yeah. I'll pull out my Sunday best. Heh."
"Hm. Yes. As will I."
Before Absalom departs, Mr. Bean tells the Elder, "Y'know, when I started having that vision, I--I couldn't make heads or tails of it, but now...it's just as I had foreseen. And yet...and yet...I still feel weird about it. It's not exactly how I was expecting to feel, y'know?"
"The conclusion is not what you expected. Hm. I will concede that it is odd. These ghostly seagulls- describe them to me in detail. But not now. Later on. Tonight, we witness the confirmation of a new Prince."
Mr. Bean grins from ear to ear, "Oh yeah! Awesome!"
A somber atmosphere hangs over the city at large. Not many people wish to go out after the latest spate of explosions. People remain indoors and even order some takeout from their favorite local restaurants and pubs.
Traffic is sparse and the streets are eerily quiet. Emergency services are on high alert in case another attack occurs.
The Al-Noor Hotel is just as lavish as the Grand Alhambra was. Soft lighting, high ceilings, expensive rooms, and a good location to boot. It's not as tall as the other place, though- only 36 floors.
Somewhere on the 18th floor, a large conference room has been taken over and cordoned off. Only the most elite of Al-Kushraf's Kindred are permitted to attend.
The 3 Archons have already arrived and have already questioned a few people connected to last night's happenings. The trio can easily sense that there's a lot of tension in the air.
The Toreador Archon is named Arthur. He's got a smooth, clean-shaven face and looks to be in his early-20s. His trimmed eyebrows, brown eyes, and long nose give him a charming, boyish appearance. He runs a hand through his black hair and clears his throat.
He speaks with a soft and refined British accent, "So, Mr. Kincaid. You're certain about everything you've told us thus far?"
The Ventrue Primogen nods.
The Tzimisce Archon is named Thaddeus. He's in his indeterminate 40s and bald-headed. He has very dark skin and lifeless hazel eyes. He sternly stares at Mark and crosses his arms over his broad chest.
Mark nervously averts his gaze.
The Lasombra Archon is named Miss Chevalier. She is a tall and unhealthily pale-skinned French woman wearing a hijab. She's decked out in fine jewelry and sports a very intense shade of lipstick.
Her deep green eyes are locked onto Mark's person. She has not said a single word to anybody, yet she still somehow manages to get her will and intentions across to her fellow Archons.
These 3 aren't necessarily ordinary Archons.
Arthur is the type of buster who'll dazzle you before fabulously ending you.
Thaddeus, for his part, has very unusual day-sleeping requirements. He always requests a small, empty, windowless, and cold room to rest in. But that doesn't mean he's weak or anything like that. Oh, no.
Ms. Chevalier cannot speak- she is permanently mute. Rumor has it that she either severed her own vocal cords as a part of her pre-Embrace testing process (it's a Lasombra thing done to gauge the wills and resolve of potential Childer) or that somebody really powerful used Dominate to silence her tongue...forever.
As such, she telepathically communicates with her fellow Archons. And it kinda helps that all 3 of them are practically Blood-Bound to each other.
The Shadow lifts her chin and stares at the back of Thaddeus's bald head. He slightly winces and asks Mark, "How often did you associate with John Suarez and Svetlana Bobrov?"
Mark wiggles around on his seat and replies, "Just--just the usual, y'know? Barely, only necessary. We--we kept our interactions to the barest of minimums, heh."
Arthur strokes his chin and regards the nervous Primogen, "You know, Mr. Kincaid, it is rather curious how the Ventrue Clan didn't contribute much to the eradication of the Second Inquisition presence in this Domain. Apart from the monetary donations you and some of your associates made, you did nothing to assist in this splendid endeavor."
The Toreador places his hands on the wide conference table and presses, "Why is that, hmm? Why didn't the Ventrue make a more direct contribution to what is arguably a just cause?"
Mark sighs, "I--I dunno, dude. Like, we sorta helped. Doesn't cheering from the sidelines count??"
Ms. Cheavlier wordlessly shakes her head from side to side.
Thaddeus chimes in, "No, it does not. That being said, you are clear of all wrongdoing. You didn't have any direct involvement in what happened after the SI was driven away from these lands. But you must stay for a few nights- there might be certain follow-up queries that we will ask of you."
Mark groans and slouches, "Man, you guys are boring."
Arthur chuckles, "Oh, tell me about it. Mr. Kincaid. You may go. Be a lad and send the next one in."
Mark tugs on his bulky fur coat and grumbles, "Whatever."
The Blue Blood Primogen leaves the room and is replaced by Mr. Bean and Absalom. Ms. Chevlier grimaces foully and shakes her head subtly.
Arthur holds up a finger and states, "No. Absolutely not. 1 at a time--"
He stops mid-sentence and stares at Absalom's long hair, "My, my, my. Absalom darling, what luscious hair you have!"
Thaddeus rolls his eyes and grumbles, "Oh, not this again. Try to focus, please."
Arthur takes a step closer and shakily asks the Elder, "Do you mind if I...stroke it?"
Absalom shrugs, "It makes no difference to me."
The Toreador Archon runs his fingers through the Elder's hair like they're the greatest thing in the whole wide world. Arthur bites his bottom lip and says, "So soft. I could never get the frizzy bird's nest on my head to do this. You must tell me what shampoo you're using."
Thaddeus rubs his temples and shakes his head, "Arthur..."
Ms. Chevalier holds up her hand and smiles a little. She keenly stares at the 2 Malkavians. She locks eyes with Mr. Bean and telepathically relays something that only he can hear. His eyebrows shoot up and he nervously waves at her.
She bows her head slightly.
Thaddeus grunts a little, "OK, exchange tips later. Which one of you would like to answer our questions first?"
Mr. Bean gulps and takes a seat. Absalom pats his charge's tense shoulder and walks off without saying a single word. He closes the door on the way out. From there, he quietly sits down on a laid-out chair and observes his surroundings.
Carpeted flooring, chandeliers that hang from the high ceiling, and an air of mystery and intrigue. He feels right at home in such a place. He glances at the door and calmly watches a few more figures filter into the room.
Amongst them is a very nervous-looking Paul Devers. The Toreador Harpy has a pained expression on his face and keeps on rubbing his sore wrist.
Mr. Bean survives the questioning process with no difficulty whatsoever. Absalom is called in and he too survives the grilling.
A little while later, the 3 Archons finish the last of their interviews and leave the room. Some extra chairs have been laid out. Almost all of them are occupied.
Thaddeus clears his throat and addresses the audience, "As you all know, the events of these past few nights have warranted our investigation into the matter. There have been some rather shocking developments."
Ms. Chevalier silently stands in the front and looks on with cold indifference.
Arthur chimes in, "Yes, that is true. But it has seemingly resolved itself quite...messily, I might add."
Thaddeus shrugs a shoulder and goes on, "In any case, we have made a decision regarding the leadership of these Domains. On behalf of the Ivory Tower, we recognize and validate the claims of Seth Steale, High Priest of the Ministry, as Prince of both Al-Kushraf AND Dubai!"
Arthur speaks, "Yes. We do. These Domains are far too vital to our interests to be left leaderless again. The limbo state that many of you here in Al-Kushraf are accustomed to comes to an end on this night."
A few of the more vocal Kindred begin to loudly protest the decision. Ms. Chevalier glowers at them in such a way that they end up slinking back to their seats like scolded puppies.
Arthur thanks her with a brief nod, "Thank you for that, dear. Yes. We have questioned all of you- there is, of course, still more that we need to know about, but that can wait until later. Right now, we must solidify the claim of your new Prince and confirm him officially."
Thaddeus calls out, "Will the Prince and his Sheriff please come forward?"
Seth Steale, the man of the fucking hour, emerges from a changing room. He's adorned in a flowing liturgical chasuble. The material is soft and ivory in color. It flows down to Seth's bare ankles.

Arthur nudges the Stoker and whispers, "Oh, me likey his outfit very much. Maybe I ought to get myself something like that, eh?"

Mr. Bean whoops and cheers till Absalom yanks his wrist. The Elder Malkavian leans back in his seat and observes the rest of the ceremony.
