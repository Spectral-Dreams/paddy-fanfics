Chapter 38: Of Serpent And Blade
The sun now reigns supreme over Al-Kushraf, UAE. It's a scorching hot day, and the Unliving are fast asleep- or as "fast asleep" as powerful, blood-hungry corpses can be.
Ain't no Snake can do their Protean shit when the sun's out. Nope, nuh-uh. But when the evening comes around, then looking over one's shoulder shall hardly suffice.
Anyways, the mortals go about their day. School buses chug along the usual routes that they've likely taken millions of times. Students of various ages and nationalities are seated, laughing and talking amongst themselves. Some look a bit worried about what the future might hold for them; others seem content enough.
Their parents and guardians stare at spreadsheets or go out shopping at this or that fancy boutique store. Life in the Domain goes on as usual.
Some reports of excessive violence over the past few nights are conveniently suppressed by the authorities. Tourism appears to be on the rise once more. The image-obsessed Emirati authorities do all that they can to maintain the status quo- shittier influencer campaigns, perverse incentives to make sure everyone maintains a positive vibe, and so on.
It's all a goddamn ruse. Underneath the surface of it all, a war between immortals is raging on. Blood consumed with wild abandon and ashes strewn all over the gaffe. Oh, yeah, those who were Blood-Hunting the Assamites might've done a bit of Diablerie here and there.
