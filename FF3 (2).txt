He drops his briefcase and slides down against the wall. The Daimonion within him remains oddly silent, but she empathizes with her host.
As such, she materializes into Daniella's form and attempts to comfort him, "Markham, it's OK. It's gonna be alright. Hey, little-big brother? Look at me."
The Baali refuses to look up at the form his long-gone sister, "Y--you're not her. Stop--stop pretending. Just--just go away, go back inside! Agreeing to your terms landed me in this mess!"
Not-Daniella tuts, "Now, that's just unfair! I've been nothing but true to my word. Besides, Paul deserved what was coming to him. Sure, you didn't sacrifice him yourself, but you delivered him up. He was...helpless and at your mercy. And what did he do? Hmm?"
"..."
"Doesn't what Paul did...remind you of something? Didn't you feel so hopeless and helpless when Charlie Chesterfield hit me with his car and drove off? When the same thing happened to poor Rhonda, you now had the power to DO something about it."
Markham wipes his face till streaks of red stain his palms. He forces a few breaths through lifeless lungs and gathers his scattered (and quite broken) composure.
He gets up and sniffs, "Watching Cha--Charlie get acquitted was the most awful thing I--I've ever had to go through."
The Daimonion places her arm around her host, "And imagine if Paul had also been...acquitted, hmm? You and I both know that the courts would not touch him. As it was, the authorities refused to arrest him...even after you incessantly demanded it. So...we took matters into our own hands, didn't we?"
Markham lets out a sigh of frustration, "You don't have to remind me."
The Daimonion slyly comments, "We played only a small part in that vile creature's demise, his...necessary sacrifice to the Great Beyond."
She widely gestures to the people walking around, "As far as they are concerned, however, Paul Cho is still...missing! And look at what happened to poor Rhonda--"
Markham's lower lip quivers, "Please, don't. Enough. Just--just stop! Go away, go back...inside. I--I can't do this anymore, I'm done. I'm fucking done with all of it! This fucking city has been nothing but a nightmare. I should NEVER have accepted this assignment!"
The Daimonion dissipates from view. Daniella's form crumbles into millions of ashes that float up into the air.
She daintily waves her hand, "You'll never be done with me...my dear host. You'll never be rid of me. Worry not, we shall figure something out. I've brought you this far, have I not?"
Markham scoffs and storms away. He hails a taxi and makes some pretty major decisions during the long ride. He is determined to see them through before the night draws to a close.
The Baali's jaw is set tight, and his hands are clenched into fists. His heart is a cold, dead thing. He's never felt so...empty on the inside. His gray eyes are deader than dead.
The drive takes a while, and Markham has to pay a small fortune owing to certain extenuating circumstances.
He steps out of the cab and the cab zooms away. He calmly greets Raul (the Gangrel mechanic) with a firm handshake.
The Baali steps into the Feral's messy, grimy garage and directly asks, "I need a ride. Anything will do. I don't even care if it's stolen, just something to get me the fuck out of this goddamn city by tonight."
Raul wipes his hands with a cloth. His eyebrows go up in surprise, "Wow. OK. Easy now. Easy. I uh...I might have some rides. Rides which might or might not be boosted, if you catch my drift."
Markham firmly reiterates, "I don't care. Now, how much is it gonna cost me?" he has a disturbingly intense look on his face.
Raul gulps, "Well, there is 1 which I have in mind. It comes with a spacious trunk, too. Y'know, in case you need to day-sleep and motels are just outta reach. Gimme a few minutes, hermano. Wait right there, OK? I'll be right back."
From a backroom, the Outlander calls out, "Oh, did you hear the news about what's been going on around this joint? There's been a change in management."
