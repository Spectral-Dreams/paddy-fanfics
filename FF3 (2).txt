Back in Flimont City, the talk of a strike is all but unavoidable. Some work stoppages have actually happened. Desperate workers brave the intense summer heat with placards and protest.

But the slimy union officialdom does all it can to quell the restive masses. They also try and isolate people from each other, in an effort to neuter any developing sentiments of solidarity amongst the different sections of the working class.
The hot sun bakes the city into submission, and it's becoming apparent that the heat isn't about to let up or abate anytime soon.
